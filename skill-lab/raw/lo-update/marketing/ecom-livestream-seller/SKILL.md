# SKILL: Kịch Bản & Chiến Lược Livestream Bán Hàng TMĐT

## 1. 3 LOẠI LIVESTREAM TMĐT

### 1.1. Livestream Bán Hàng Thường (2-4 tiếng) — Daily Selling

**Mục đích:** Regular revenue generation, build loyal community, predictable viewers

**Tần suất:** 3-5 lần/tuần, fixed schedule (ví dụ: Thứ 3-5 lúc 20:00)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Routine, viewers expect you at same time
- Mix of products (5-10 sản phẩm khác nhau)
- Relaxed pace, conversational
- High interaction (Q&A, comments, minigames)
- Regular customers familiar with flow

**Target KPIs:**
- 2-5 hour average live time
- 500-5000 concurrent viewers (tùy shop size)
- 20-50% conversion rate (very achievable for repeat customers)
- 1-5M GMV per livestream (depending on price point)

**Platform:** TikTok Live, Shopee Live, Facebook Live

---

### 1.2. Livestream Flash Sale/Event (1-2 tiếng) — Campaign Đặc Biệt

**Mục đích:** Quick money, urgency-driven, spike in sales, special promotions

**Tần suất:** 1-3 lần/tháng, announced in advance

**Đặc điểm:**
- Limited-time offers (exactly 1-2 hours)
- High discounts (25-50% off)
- Scarcity messaging (only X units)
- Faster pace, more exciting
- Attract new customers + loyal ones

**Target KPIs:**
- 1-2 hour duration
- 1000-10000 concurrent viewers (much higher than regular)
- 40-70% conversion rate (high urgency)
- 5-20M GMV in short burst (concentrated selling)

**Use cases:**
- Seasonal sales (CNY, Holiday, Back to school)
- New product launch event
- Clearance sale
- Partnership/collaboration launch
- Birthday sale (brand's "birthday")

**Platform:** TikTok Shop, Shopee Mall Flash Deal, Facebook Ads driving to Live

---

### 1.3. Livestream Ra Mắt Sản Phẩm (30-60 phút) — Product Launch

**Mục đích:** Build hype, educate about new product, exclusive launch feeling

**Tần suất:** 1-2 lần/tháng khi có sản phẩm mới

**Đặc điểm:**
- Focused on ONE product (or small product line)
- Deep dive into features, benefits, story
- Q&A time for buyer concerns
- Exclusive launch pricing/bonuses
- Celebrity/influencer collab possible

**Target KPIs:**
- 30-60 minutes focused time
- 2000-20000 viewers (novelty attracts)
- 30-50% conversion rate (new product curiosity)
- 2-10M GMV (smaller but focused)

**Use cases:**
- New seasonal collection
- Reformulated product
- Collaboration product
- Brand expansion (new category)
- Tech product (high-interest)

**Platform:** TikTok Shop Premium, Shopee Live Premium, Instagram Live + website

## 2. KỊCH BẢN CHI TIẾT LIVESTREAM 2 TIẾNG

### 2.1. Timeline Breakdown

```
TOTAL DURATION: 2 hours (120 minutes)

Phút 0-10: INTRO & WARM UP (10 min)
Phút 10-30: SẢN PHẨM #1 (20 min)
Phút 30-40: MINIGAME #1 (10 min)
Phút 40-60: SẢN PHẨM #2 & #3 (20 min)
Phút 60-70: MINIGAME #2 (10 min)
Phút 70-90: SẢN PHẨM #4 + COMBO DEALS (20 min)
Phút 90-100: FLASH SALE CUỐI (10 min)
Phút 100-120: TỔNG KẾT & LAST CALL (20 min)

KEY PRINCIPLE: Alternating selling ↔ engagement
4 cycles of: Sell (20min) → Engage (10min)
```

### 2.2. Chi Tiết Phút 0-10: INTRO & WARM UP

**Mục đích:** Set energy, welcome everyone, establish tone, technical check

**Script:**

```
Phút 0-1:
Host on camera, big smile, energy high
"Xin chào mọi người! [Waving] Chào lại những bạn quen thuộc,
chào bạn mới lần đầu ghé qua nhé!"

[Visual: Brand logo animation or intro screen]
[Music: Upbeat brand intro music]
[Text overlay: "WELCOME TO [BRAND] LIVE"]

Phút 1-3:
"Hôm nay mình chuẩn bị 10 sản phẩm hot lắm.
Có cái gì giảm cực mạnh."
[Visual: Quick montage of tonight's products]

"Nếu bạn chưa follow mình, follow giúp mình được không?
[Point to follow button] Bấm follow để không miss livestream tiếp theo."

[Text overlay: "HIT FOLLOW!", "NEXT LIVE: [DATE/TIME]"]

Phút 3-5:
"Trước khi bắt đầu, mình xin hỏi một chút nhé."
[Interactive questions popping up]

"Có ai xem lần đầu không? [Wait for comments] Yay, chào!"
"Có ai mua lần rồi? [Wait] Thank you so much!"
"Ai muốn mua hôm nay? [Wait] Yesss, come, come!"

[Respond to comments in real-time, acknowledge names]

Phút 5-10:
"Okay, let's start! Hôm nay mình sẽ bán 10 sản phẩm.
Sản phẩm nào hết thì hết nhé. Stock có hạn."

[Show all products lined up or displayed on screen]

"Có minigame, có flash sale. Ai may mắn sẽ được giảm thêm."

[Visual: Minigame prizes displayed]

"Okay, let's go! Sản phẩm số 1..."

[Text overlay: "LET'S START!", "PRODUCT #1 COMING UP!"]
[Music: Transitions to selling music (more energetic)]
```

**Technical check behind scenes:**
- Verify camera clear
- Audio working (viewers can hear you)
- Lighting good
- Products visible
- Live comments visible to you
- Alerts turned on (followers, gifts, new viewers)

life thay đổi.' Mình nghe mà xúc động."

"Đó là giảm 33% luôn!"

"Nhìn kìa, vừa vặn hoàn hảo. Vải mềm, không ngứa.
Wear cả ngày mà không khó chịu."

If consumable (skincare, food):
[Show product being used, close-up of result]
"Xem nè, apply xong da sáng lên ngay. Không dính, không dầu."

If non-wearable (accessories, home goods):
[Show in use, demonstrate functionality]
"Cái này để trên table, vừa đẹp vừa tiện dụng."

[Voiceover while showing]:
"Bạn nào đang cần [PRODUCT TYPE]? Comment YES nếu bạn quan tâm!"

[Text overlay: "COMMENT YES IF INTERESTED!"]
[Read comments while showing product]

Phút 20-25:
"Bây giờ mình nói về offer hôm nay."
[Text: "TONIGHT'S SPECIAL OFFER"]

"Nếu bạn mua ngay, mình có bonus [BONUS ITEM] kèm theo."

[Hold up bonus item, show to camera]

"Bonus này thường bán 50K, hôm nay mình tặng free cho ai mua
sản phẩm này."

[Text overlay: "FREE BONUS: [ITEM] (Worth 50K)"]

"Plus, nếu bạn mua từ 2 cái trở lên, mình giảm thêm 10% nữa."

[Text overlay: "BUY 2+ GET EXTRA 10% OFF"]

"Nên hôm nay giá thực tế là: 1 cái 399K, 2 cái 718K (thay vì 798K)."

Phút 25-28:
"Okay, ai muốn mua? Link trong shop, bấm add to cart rồi checkout."

[Link to product appears on screen]
[Text: "TAP TO BUY!", "LINK IN DESCRIPTION"]

"Bạn nào đã add to cart, comment ADDED nhé, mình thấy."

[Wait for comments, acknowledge: "Yay thank you!", "Bạn tên gì nhân tiện?"]

[If comments come in: "Oh wow, 20 người already added!")

Phút 28-30:
"Mình biết không phải ai cũng ready mua ngay.
Không sao, livestream còn 90 phút nữa.
Chúng ta có nhiều sản phẩm khác, chắc có cái phù hợp với bạn."

[Transition to minigame]
"But before we move on, chúng ta chơi minigame nho nhỏ nhé!"
```

**Key principles for this 20 minutes:**
- Start strong: why this product, why now, why discount
- Show genuine enthusiasm (fake = audience leaves)
- Build urgency subtly: "stock có hạn", "offer chỉ hôm nay"
- Interact constantly: read comments, answer questions
- Use text overlays heavy: price, offer, link, CTA
- Demo/lifestyle moment: people buy experience, not just product
- Bonus items: increase perceived value
- Clear link/CTA: 3 times mention how to buy

### 2.4. Chi Tiết Phút 30-40: MINIGAME #1 (Engagement) - 10 phút

**Mục đích:** Keep energy up, audience tired of selling, create fun moments, give rewards

**Game 1: Guess the Price**

```
Phút 30-32:
"Chúng ta chơi game nhỏ nhé! Guess the Price!"

[Visual: Game intro music, excited music]
[Text overlay: "GUESS THE PRICE GAME!"]

"Mình sẽ show cái sản phẩm tiếp theo, bạn đoán giá nó bao nhiêu.
Ai đoán đúng, mình tặng voucher 50K."

Phút 32-35:
[Show next product without revealing price]
"Nhìn xem, cái này là cái gì? Đoán giá nó bao nhiêu?"

[Show close-up, show quality]

"Comment số tiền vào nhé! Có bao nhiêu ý kiến thì làm bé."

[Read comments as they come in]
"Oh bạn này nói 299K! Bạn kia nói 499K! Bạn này nói 399K!"

[Build suspense]
"Tiếng mười, chín, tám..."

Phút 35-37:
[Reveal price]
"Reveal! Giá nó là... 349K! [Drumroll sound effect]"

[Check who guessed closest]
"Ai đoán 349K? Nobody exact? Thì ai đoán gần nhất?
Đoán 399K - gần hơn ai! Bạn tên gì?"

[Read their username]
"Thank you bạn! Mình sẽ send voucher 50K cho bạn.
Bạn sẽ nhận notification."

[Visual: Voucher appears, goes to that person]

Phút 37-40:
"Cái này là sản phẩm sắp bán tiếp. Nếu bạn muốn, follow mình,
ready cart nhé!"

[Transition back to selling]
"Okay, sản phẩm #2 coming up!"
```

**Game 2: Comment & Win (Easier)**

```
Phút 30-35:
"Lucky draw game! [Excited music]"

"Ai comment từ 'YES' hoặc emoji heart, mình sẽ chọn ngẫu nhiên
1 người để tặng voucher 100K!"

[Clear instructions on screen]
[Text: "COMMENT 'YES' OR HEART EMOJI TO WIN 100K VOUCHER"]

[Khuyến khích mọi người comment]
"Come on everyone! Comment! Comment! Comment!"

Phút 35-38:
[Count comments as they flood in]
"Wow, 200 người comment rồi! 300! 400!"

"Okay, chúng ta random... 3, 2, 1..."

[Use tool or random picker to select 1 winner]

Phút 38-40:
"Người chiến thắng là... [Username]!
Xin chúc mừng! [Confetti sound effect]"

[If they're in chat, acknowledge them directly]
"Chào [Username]! Thank you for joining! Bạn sẽ get voucher 100K!"

[Move to next product]
```

**Game 3: Trivia (Most Engaging)**

```
Phút 30-32:
"Pop quiz! [Game music]"

[Text: "TRIVIA GAME - ANSWER CORRECTLY TO WIN 100K VOUCHER!"]

"Ai biết [TRIVIA QUESTION ABOUT BRAND]?"

Example:
"Mình ra đời vào năm nào?"
"Brand mình sử dụng ingredient gì?"

Phút 32-35:
[Read answers as they come in]
"Nghe bạn nói 2019... 2020... 2021..."

[Build suspense]

Phút 35-38:
"Đáp án đúng là... 2020!"
"Ai nói 2020? Wow, quite a few people!"
"Mình sẽ pick... bạn này!"

Phút 38-40:
[Announce winner, give voucher]
"Xin chúc mừng! Voucher đã gửi!"
[Move to next product]
```

**Best practices for minigames:**
- Keep it 8-10 minutes (not boring people for too long)
- Rewards must be valuable (100K voucher = $4-5, worthwhile)
- Rules must be SUPER clear (simple = more participation)
- Read winner's name OUT LOUD (makes them feel special)
- Make it fun: cheering, celebration sounds, emojis
- Transition smoothly back to selling after
- Run 2-3 games per 2-hour live (this prevents fatigue)

**Key for bundling strategy:**
- Pairing complementary products increases average order value
- Bundle discounts feel generous (save 150K vs save 50K)
- Clear options (single, duo, trio) make decision easy
- Urgency on limited stock (scarcity)
- Read comments to acknowledge orders coming in

## 3. CÂUTALK CÓ SẴN - SELLING SCRIPTS

### 3.1. Giới Thiệu Sản Phẩm

**Format:**
```
[Pickup product]
"Sản phẩm tiếp theo là [NAME]."

[Show close-up, rotate]
"Nhìn xem, [PHYSICAL FEATURE]."

[Point to key details]
"Cái này có [FEATURE #1], cái này có [FEATURE #2]."

[Explain benefit]
"Vì vậy nên, nó [BENEFIT]. Bạn sẽ [RESULT]."

[Emotional hook]
"Mình personally [PERSONAL STORY]. Thế nên [REASON TO RECOMMEND]."
```

**Real example - Skincare:**
```
"Sản phẩm tiếp theo là Vitamin C Serum."

"Nhìn xem, chai glass đẹp chứ? Chất liệu giúp bảo vệ Vitamin C khỏi ánh nắng."

"Có 15% L-ascorbic acid, light texture, absorb nhanh."

"Vì vậy nên, nó brightness skin, fade dark spot, smooth texture.
Bạn sẽ có glow skin."

"Mình personally dùng nó 3 tháng, skin mình sáng lên rõ.
Thế nên mình muốn share tới bạn."
```

### 3.2. TẠOOFORT - Khắc Phục Nghi Ngờ (Objections)

**Objection #1: "Giá quá mắc!"**
```
Voiceover: "Mình nghe comment 'Giá hơi cao đó'."

Respond: "Mình hiểu! Nhưng hãy nhìn cái này.
Sản phẩm này dùng được 3 tháng.
Tính ra mỗi ngày chỉ 4K.
Bạn uống cà phê mỗi ngày 20K, nhưng skincare 4K là quá rồi à? [Laugh]"

[Text overlay: "30,000 / 90 days = only 4K/day!"]

"Plus, hôm nay mình giảm 30%, giá còn hợp lý hơn nữa."
```

   - Viewers can add to cart without leaving
   - Checkout within TikTok app

2. Flash sale feature
   - Set time window (exactly 10 minutes)
   - Drop price dramatically
   - System shows "Flash Sale Active!"
   - Creates urgency naturally

3. TikTok coins/gifts
   - Viewers can send gifts (costs coins)
   - You convert to cash
   - Thank them individually: "Oh, bạn vừa send gift! Thank you!"
   - Tipping culture - additional revenue

4. Trending sounds

   - Increases discoverability
   - Hook viewers from feed

   - Can stream simultaneously to multiple accounts
   - Reach double audience

**Advantages:**

- Voucher/coupon system
- Games (Shopee has built-in minigames)

**How to optimize:**

1. Shopee vouchers

   - "This voucher only available during this stream"

5. Live comments integration

## 5. LIVESTREAM SETUP KỸ THUẬT

### 5.1. Essential Equipment

```
CAMERA:
- Smartphone (latest model best, but even 2-3 year old ok)
- Webcam (if using computer, Logitech C920 or better)
- Tripod or stand (so you have hands free)
- Position camera at eye level (not below, not too high)

LIGHTING:
- Ring light (most important!)
  Budget: 300K-800K
  Best for face, makes skin look good

- Natural light (window if possible)
  But need backup for evening

- Avoid: Backlit (dark face),
         Side harsh light (bad shadows),
         Too dim (phone camera noisy)

MICROPHONE:
- Smartphone mic (okayish but pick up background noise)
- Lavalier/clip mic (good cheap option, ~100K)
- USB condenser mic (good quality, ~300K+)
- Headphones (so you can hear comments/alerts)

BACKGROUND:
- Clean, organized, brand-colored if possible
- Hang backdrop or use plain wall
- Leave room for product display
- Keep consistent between streams

INTERNET:
- Minimum: 5 Mbps upload speed (use speedtest.net to check)
- Ideal: 10+ Mbps for stable 1080p
- Ethernet (wired) > WiFi (wireless)
- If using phone, WiFi is fine but have mobile data backup
```

```
TikTok Live:
- TikTok Shop integrated
- Product cart auto-displays

- System handles most technical aspects

- Smooth streaming quality focus

Facebook Live:

## 6. POST-LIVESTREAM ANALYSIS

### 6.1. KPIs to Track

```
VIEWING METRICS:
- Total viewers (how many people watched any part)
- Peak concurrent viewers (max people watching at same time)
- Average watch time (how long did average person stay)
- Completion rate (% of stream people watched till end)

ENGAGEMENT METRICS:
- Comments (engagement level)
- Likes/reactions (sentiment)
- Share count (viral potential)
- Follower growth (from stream)

SALES METRICS:
- Units sold (critical)
- Total GMV (gross merchandise value)
- Average order value (AOV)
- Conversion rate (% of viewers who bought)
- Cost per acquisition (how much you spent on ads if ran ads)

FINANCIAL:
- Revenue (after refunds, payment processing fees)
- Profit margin (revenue minus cost of goods)
- Return on investment (if spent on ads)
```

## 7. KỲ LIVESTREAM TỐI ƯU VIETN NAM

### 7.1. Best Days & Times

**Weekdays vs Weekends:**
```
Weekdays: Mon-Fri
- Weekday evening: 7pm-10pm (after work, before bed)
- Peak: 8pm-9pm (dinner just finished, settling in)
- Secondary: 12pm-1pm (lunch break if targeting office workers)

Weekends: Sat-Sun
- 11am-1pm (morning shopping browsing)
- 3pm-5pm (afternoon relaxation)
- 8pm-11pm (evening)
- Weekend viewership lower than weekday (people out)
  BUT conversion higher (more time to think, less rushed)
```

**Seasonal optimized timing:**
```
Regular season: 8pm-9pm weekdays is golden
Chinese New Year season: Earlier, 7pm-9pm (family time)
Summer: Can go later, 9pm-10pm (more evening activity)
Winter: Earlier is better
Black Friday/Mega sale: Vary schedule (surprise viewers)
```

### 7.2. Ideal Frequency & Schedule

**Starting out (Month 1-3):**
```
Schedule: 2x per week
Days: Tuesday + Thursday (consistent schedule)
Time: 8:00 PM
Duration: 1 hour
Why: Build routine, audience knows when to tune in
Goal: Get 100-500 concurrent viewers
```

**Growing (Month 4-8):**
```
Schedule: 3-4x per week
Days: Tue, Thu, Sat (spread out)
Time: 8:00 PM mostly (one weekend 11am if testing)
Duration: 1-2 hours
Why: Increase frequency = more touch points
Goal: Get 500-2000 concurrent viewers
```

**Scaling (Month 9+):**
```
Schedule: 4-5x per week
Flagship: Tue 8pm (2 hours)
Regular: Thu 8pm (1.5 hours)
Casual: Wed/Fri 8:15pm (1 hour each)
Weekend: Sat 11am or 8pm depending on product
Duration: 1-3 hours (varies)
Why: More opportunities = more revenue
Goal: Get 2000+ concurrent viewers
```

## 8. REVENUE MODEL & MONETIZATION

### 8.1. Multiple Revenue Streams

**Stream 1: Direct product sales** (Primary - 70% revenue)
```
- Customer buys product on livestream
- You pocket 85-95% after platform fees
- Typical margin: 40-60% (depending on product cost)
- Most reliable revenue
```

**Stream 2: Affiliate commissions** (Secondary - 15%)
```
- If selling via Shopee/Lazada, get % commission
- Typically 5-10% of GMV
- Works even if don't own product (just promote others)
```

**Stream 3: Gifts/tips** (Tertiary - 10%)
```
- TikTok/Facebook viewers send gifts (costs them real money)
- You convert gifts to cash, platform takes 30-50%
- You net 50-70% of gift value
- Can be significant if audience engaged
```

**Stream 4: Sponsorships** (Future - 5%)
```
- Once you have 5000+ viewers, brands want to sponsor
- They pay you to feature/mention product
- Typical: 2,000-10,000 per sponsorship per stream
```

### 8.2. Pricing Strategy

```
Product pricing on livestream:
- Regular price: 100% (normal selling price on Shopee)
- Livestream price: 70-85% of regular (15-30% discount)
- Flash sale price: 50-60% of regular (40-50% discount, limited time)

Combo pricing:
- Single item: Regular
- 2 items: 15% total discount
- 3 items: 25% total discount
- This drives AOV up

Tiered offers:
- Entry (lowest price): Get new customer hooked
- Middle (regular): Most profit
- Premium (bundle): Highest AOV

Formula:
- Entry product: High volume, lower margin (30%)
- Mid product: Good balance (50%)
- Premium/combo: Higher margin (60%+)
```
