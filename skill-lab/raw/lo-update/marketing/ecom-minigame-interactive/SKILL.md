# Minigame & Interactive Content Cho Thương Mại Điện Tử

## 1. Tại Sao Minigame Tăng Engagement

Minigame = interactive content. Engagement rate 5-10x cao hơn static post.

Data:
- Static post engagement: 2-5%
- Interactive post (poll, question): 5-15%
- Minigame (contest, quiz): 15-40%

Reason: Participation > passive viewing. Minigame buat khách hàng active, excited, want to share.

Bonus: Minigame collect data (email, phone, preference) = lead magnet.

## 2. 15 Loại Minigame TMĐT (+ Khi Dùng)

### Minigame #1: Spin Wheel
Khách spin wheel, random prize.

**Mechanic:**
- Click "spin"
- Wheel rotate, stop on random section
- Win prize (discount code, gift, free shipping)

**Khi dùng:**
- Lead magnet (collect email to spin)
- Event activation (Tết, Valentine, etc)
- Traffic burst

**Rules template:**
"Mỗi người spin 1 lần/ngày. Giải thưởng: 10% - 50% discount, free shipping, free gift. Spin bây giờ!"

**Duration:** 1 event = 1 tuần

**Platform:** Website, Facebook

**Example:** "Tết wheel of fortune - spin để win prize"

### Minigame #2: Quiz
Khách answer question, hasil reveal score + recommendation.

**Mechanic:**
- 5-10 question multiple choice
- Answer reveal next question
- Final result show "your type" + product recommendation

**Khi dùng:**
- Lead magnet
- Product recommendation engine
- Educational + fun
- Segmentation (identify customer type)

**Rules template:**
"Answer 7 questions tìm skin type ideal bạn. Result akan recommend 3 product. Quiz gratis, no email required. Play now!"

**Duration:** Evergreen (always available)

**Platform:** Website, Facebook, Instagram

**Example:** "Which coffee flavor match your personality? Take quiz"

### Minigame #3: Poll
Simple binary or multiple choice poll.

**Mechanic:**
- 1 question
- 2-4 option (visual or text)
- Instant result reveal
- Follow-up: "Choose X → here's gift for you"

**Khi dùng:**
- Engagement quick boost
- Audience insight (preference data)
- Social proof (see what others choose)
- Decision aid (see popular choice)

**Rules template:**
"Pilih favorite: A or B? Vote sekarang. Hasil reveal dalam 30 detik. Pemenang mendapat [gift]."

**Duration:** 3-7 hari per poll

**Platform:** Instagram Story, Facebook poll, TikTok poll

**Example:** "Pilih color: Indigo or Black? Vote → unlock promo"

### Minigame #4: Guessing Game
Khách guess harga, quantity, atau answer.

**Mechanic:**
- Game: "Guess harga serum ini?"
- Khách input number
- Hasil: Show correct price, how close they were
- Winner: Closest guess atau exact win prize

**Khi dùng:**
- Price perception check
- Entertainment
- Viral (unique answer per person)

**Rules template:**
"Guess harga serum ini. Closest 10 guess akan win 20% discount. Submit via comment atau form. Paling dekat = biggest discount!"

**Duration:** 3-5 hari

**Platform:** Facebook, website

**Example:** "Guess number biji coffee dalam jar - exact guess win jar!"

### Minigame #5: Find Difference
Khách find perbedaan 2 ảnh (before/after, design variation).

**Mechanic:**
- Show 2 ảnh side-by-side atau carousel
- Khách identify perbedaan
- Upload answer / tag location
- Fastest correct win

**Khi dùng:**
- Attention detail awareness
- Product detail highlight
- Fun engagement
- Viral (easy share)

**Rules template:**
"Find 5 differences antara kemasan lama dan baru. Screenshot jawaban, post di comment. First 3 correct win free sample!"

**Duration:** 5-7 hari

**Platform:** Facebook, Instagram

**Example:** "Spot difference: real product vs competitor"

### Minigame #6: Caption This
Khách write caption untuk ảnh/video funny.

**Mechanic:**
- Show ảnh/video (often product in funny situation)
- Khách comment caption funny
- Vote best caption
- Winner: Most upvote atau admin choice

**Khi dùng:**
- User-generated content
- Entertainment
- Community building
- Viral (share funny caption)

**Rules template:**
"Apa caption yang cocok untuk ảnh ini? Comment caption paling funny. Paling upvote akan win [gift]. Voting 7 hari."

**Duration:** 10-14 hari (creation + voting)

**Platform:** Facebook, Instagram

**Example:** "Caption this: Product in weird situation"

### Minigame #7: Fill in Blank
Khách complete sentence / phrase.

**Mechanic:**
- "[Product] membuat aku ___"
- Khách reply dengan words
- Collect semua answer
- Funny/touching answer win

**Khi dùng:**
- User-generated testimonial
- Emotion data
- Community insight
- Shareable content

**Rules template:**
"Complete: '[Product] membuat aku ___'. Reply di comment. Paling creative / touching akan feature di story + win gift!"

**Duration:** 7-10 hari

**Platform:** Facebook, Instagram

**Example:** "This coffee makes me ___" contest

### Minigame #8: This or That (Binary Choice)
Quick preference poll, 1 question, 2 answer.

**Mechanic:**
- "Product A or Product B?"
- Choose → reveal result
- See percentage A vs B
- Soft CTA: "Learn why people choose A"

**Khi dùng:**
- Quick engagement
- Audience preference insight
- Feature comparison indirect
- A/B testing audience

**Rules template:**
"Pilih: Serum atau Sheet mask? Vote sekarang, lihat audience prefer apa. Surprise: semua voter dapat 10% code!"

**Duration:** 3-5 hari

**Platform:** Instagram Story, TikTok

**Example:** "Jeans atau shorts? Vote & see result"

### Minigame #9: Would You Rather
Binary choice dengan emotional weight.

**Mechanic:**
- "Would you rather: [scenario A] or [scenario B]?"
- Both relate to product indirect
- Share result, spark discussion

**Khi dùng:**
- Engagement + entertainment
- Audience psychology insight
- Conversation starter
- Shareable (people disagree, debate)

**Rules template:**
"Would you rather: [A] atau [B]? Vote, lihat friends pilih apa. Winner (random) dapat [gift]."

**Duration:** 3-7 hari

**Platform:** Instagram Story, Facebook

**Example:** "Would you rather: expensive skincare or cheap but work?"

### Minigame #10: Countdown Timer
Real-time game, khách rush to action sebelum timer habis.

**Mechanic:**
- Game available 24 jam only / per hour only
- Timer visible, tick down
- Limited quantity prize
- Urgency = rush action

**Khi dùng:**
- Urgency creation
- Traffic boost specific time
- Scarcity trigger
- Real-time engagement

**Rules template:**
"Flash game: Setiap jam 12-1pm, 5 orang dapat 50% discount voucher. Spin game mulai 12pm sharp! Game end 1pm or stock habis."

**Duration:** 1 event per day / per week

**Platform:** Website, app, email

**Example:** "Midnight madness game - available 12-1am only"

### Minigame #11: Lucky Draw / Raffle
Khách enter draw, random selection menang.

**Mechanic:**
- Khách participate (purchase, signup, share, comment)
- Auto entry atau manual submit
- Random draw, winner reveal
- Prize shipping

**Khi dùng:**
- Incentivize action (purchase, signup, share)
- Community building
- Excitement (anticipation)
- Legal (if follow regulation)

**Rules template:**
"Setiap purchase dapat 1 entry. Total 100 entry, random draw tanggal [date]. 1 winner dapat [big prize]. Good luck!"

**Duration:** 2-4 minggu

**Platform:** Shopee, Lazada, website, email

**Example:** "Buy now, auto-enter iPhone raffle"

### Minigame #12: Comment To Win
Khách comment specific text to participate.

**Mechanic:**
- "Comment 'READY' untuk enter"
- Random khách comment = winner
- Winner announce next day

**Khi dùng:**
- High engagement metric boost
- Algorithm boost (high comment)
- Reach amplify (comment visible)
- Lead capture (email from winner)

**Rules template:**
"Comment 'SERUM' di post ini, auto-entry lucky draw. Random commenter akan menang 50% discount voucher. Draw bersama jam 6pm!"

**Duration:** 1-2 hari

**Platform:** Facebook, Instagram

**Example:** "Comment 'YES' untuk win free item"

### Minigame #13: Tag To Share
Khách tag friends, spread viral.

**Mechanic:**
- "Tag 3 friends yang bakal suka serum ini"
- Khách tag, comment public
- Encourage tagging = viral
- Both tagger dan tagged dapat entry

**Khi dùng:**
- Organic reach amplify (tag viral)
- New audience reach
- Community building
- Lead capture (tagged friends)

**Rules template:**
"Tag 3 friends biar mereka tahu serum ini. Both bapak & friend dapat entry lucky draw. Semakin tag, semakin menang chance. Semakin viral!"

**Duration:** 7-14 hari

**Platform:** Facebook, Instagram

**Example:** "Tag friends, both of you get discount"

### Minigame #14: Check-in Challenge
Khách check in irl or digital, verify participation.

**Mechanic:**
- Khách visit store, take selfie
- Post IG story tag location + brand
- OR: App check-in untuk loyalty point
- Collect check-in → earn reward

**Khi dùng:**
- Drive foot traffic (if offline)
- Loyalty program (if app)
- UGC collection (selfie)
- Location data

**Rules template:**
"Visit toko kami, check in di Shopee/app, selfie tag #[hashtag]. Collect 5 check-in → unlock reward tier!"

**Duration:** 1 tháng

**Platform:** App, Instagram, TikTok

**Example:** "Midnight store check-in = double points"

### Minigame #15: Loyalty Level Up / Achievement Badges
Gamified loyalty program structure.

**Mechanic:**
- Khách earn point/level berdasarkan action (purchase, refer, review)
- Unlock badges / tier
- Higher tier = better benefit
- Leaderboard (optional)

**Khi dùng:**
- Repeat purchase incentivize
- Referral incentivize
- Community competition
- Long-term engagement

**Rules template:**
"Earn points: Buy (1 point per 1k) → Review (10 point) → Refer (50 point). Collect 100 point = Silver tier, unlock 10% lifetime discount + priority support. 500 point = Gold tier, unlock 20% + free shipping lifetime!"

**Duration:** Ongoing loyalty program

**Platform:** App, website, email

**Example:** "Loyalty level: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum"

## 3. Kịch Bản Chi Tiết Cho Từng Loại (Rules, Prize, Duration, Template)

### KỊCH BẢN #1: Spin Wheel Tết

**Setup:**
- Wheel divide 8 section: 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%, Free gift, Free shipping, Discount code
- Player spin 1 lần per day, max 7 ngày

**Prize breakdown:**
- 10%: 40% chance
- 20%: 25% chance
- 30%: 15% chance
- 40%: 10% chance
- 50%: 5% chance
- Free gift: 3% chance
- Free shipping: 1% chance
- Discount code: 1% chance

**Duration:** 7 ngày (Tết eve period)

**Rules:**
"Spin Tết wheel, win discount up to 50%. Mỗi orang spin 1 lần per hari. Spin 7 hari, dapat max 7 discount. Discount tidak accumulate, pilih 1 favorite apply. Valid hingga [date]."

**Mechanics:**
- Need email to spin (lead capture)
- Code email, play kembali bisa lihat previous spin
- Result instant, code email to customer
- Code valid 30 hari

### KỊCH BẢN #2: Quiz Skin Type

**Setup:**
- 7 question about skin characteristic
- Result: Oily, Dry, Combination, Sensitive, Balanced (5 type)
- Each result show 3 product recommendation

**Question:**
Q1: Sebum level? (Oily / Normal / Dry)
Q2: Sensitivity? (Very / Moderate / None)
Q3: Skin goal? (Clear / Glow / Hydrate / Anti-aging)
... etc

**Result:**
"Bapak: Combination sensitive skin. Recommended serum: [A], [B], [C]. [Button: See full collection]"

**Lead capture:** Email optional tapi "subscribe → get quiz result PDF + 10% off"

**Duration:** Evergreen

**Rules:**
"Take quiz gratis, find your ideal serum. Result personalized untuk skin bapak. Subscribe untuk PDF + discount!"

### KỊCH BẢN #3: Caption This

**Setup:**
- Ảnh funny: Produk dalam situasi absurd
- Khách caption dalam 10-50 word
- 7 hari voting
- Admin + community vote

**Prize:**
- Winner: 3 orang
- Prize: [Produk paket 1jt], [voucher 500k], [voucher 200k]

**Mechanics:**
- Comment di post (public)
- Other dapat like/upvote comment
- Day 7: Announce 3 winner (highest upvote + admin choice)
- Tag winner, announce di story

**Duration:** 7 creation + 7 voting = 14 hari total

**Rules:**
"Write funny caption untuk ảnh ini. Most upvoted + admin favorite 3 orang dapat hadiah. No profanity, keep it fun! Vote buka day 7-14."

### KỊCH BẢN #4: Loyalty Level Up

**Setup:**
- Point system:
  - Buy: 1 point = 1k rupiah
  - Review: 10 point
  - Refer: 50 point per success
  - Birthday: 50 point bonus

- Tier:
  - Bronze (0-100 point): 5% lifetime discount
  - Silver (101-300 point): 10% + priority support
  - Gold (301-500 point): 15% + free shipping
  - Platinum (500+ point): 20% + concierge

**Mechanics:**
- App track point automatic
- Dashboard show progress
- Tier unlock instant (notification)
- Benefit apply automatic

**Duration:** Ongoing

**Rules:**
"Earn point every purchase, review, referral. Unlock tier untuk benefit. Higher tier, better perks. Point tidak expire. Tier semakin tinggi, semakin exclusive benefit!"

## 4. Minigame Untuk Setiap Platform

### Facebook Minigame:

**Best type:** Comment-based (caption this, comment to win, tag friends), Carousel game, Instant game

**Advantage:** High reach, easy shareability, comment algorithm boost

**Example:**
"Comment 'SERUM' untuk win. Tag 2 friends yang bakal suka. Each tag = 1 entry. Random commenter menang. Announce jam 6pm 🎁"

**Tools:** Native poll (quick), instant game (more complex), app integration

### Instagram Minigame:

**Best type:** Story poll/quiz, carousel game, caption this, tag friends

**Advantage:** High engagement, intimate audience, visual, ephemeral (create urgency)

**Example:**
"Story poll: Serum or mask? Vote & see. 50% both vote dapat 10% code. Voting close dalam 24h"

**Tools:** Story poll/quiz native, carousel swipe, DM game

### TikTok Minigame:

**Best type:** Challenge (duet/stitch), quiz in comment, trending audio game, hashtag challenge

**Advantage:** Viral potential, organic reach, youth demographic

**Example:**
"Duet video: Show your skincare routine dengan serum kami. Use #SkinCareChallenge untuk feature. Top 5 video dapat [gift]"

**Tools:** Challenge format, comment-based, hashtag, trending audio

### Zalo Minigame:

**Best type:** Broadcast game, comment-based, official account game

**Advantage:** High engagement (notification dense), Vietnam-specific, intimate audience

**Example:**
"Official account broadcast: Spin wheel game. Comment 'SPIN' để nhận kết quả. Mỗi ngày 5 người may mắn nhận 50% voucher"

**Tools:** Broadcasting (reach all subscriber), interactive content plugin

### Website/App Minigame:

**Best type:** Spin wheel, quiz, countdown timer, loyalty game

**Advantage:** Full control, data collection, conversion tracking, repeat play

**Example:**
"Homepage: Spin wheel daily. Collect 7 spin → unlock prize. Play anytime, track progress di dashboard"

**Tools:** Gamification plugin (Gleam, Riddle, Interact), custom development

### Email Minigame:

**Best type:** Click game (interactive email), quiz, link-based game

**Advantage:** 1-on-1, segmentation possible, lead nurture

**Example:**
"Email subject: 'Spin wheel untuk win - open email'. Inside: Spin game, show result, send code."

**Tools:** Interactive email (Stripo, Dyspatch), native email game

## 5. Gamification Untuk Loyalty Program (Long-term)

Beyond 1-off minigame, gamification structure dalam loyalty program:

### Structure:

**Level system:**
- Beginner (0-50 point)
- Bronze (51-150)
- Silver (151-300)
- Gold (301-500)
- Platinum (500+)

**Point sources:**
- Purchase: 1 point = 100 rupiah
- Review: 10 point per review (max 5/month)
- Referral: 50 point per successful referral
- Social share: 5 point per share
- Birthday: 50 point bonus
- Anniversary: 50 point per year customer
- Quiz completion: 10 point
- Spin wheel: 5 point (daily max)

**Benefits per tier:**
- Beginner: 5% discount
- Bronze: 7% discount + priority support
- Silver: 10% discount + priority + free shipping
- Gold: 15% discount + priority + free shipping + birthday gift
- Platinum: 20% discount + concierge + VIP access new product

**Badges/Achievement:**
- "100 point warrior" (first 100 point)
- "Loyal customer" (1 year member)
- "Brand advocate" (5+ referral)
- "Review master" (10+ review)
- "Social butterfly" (10+ share)

**Leaderboard (optional):**
- Top 10 point earner = month benefit (feature, extra discount)
- Encourage competition

### Mechanics:
- Dashboard: Point, level, progress to next level, available benefit
- Notification: Level up alert, achievement unlock, point earn update
- Email: Monthly statement, tier benefit reminder, expired point warning

### KPI:
- Repeat purchase rate (higher = better loyalty)
- Customer lifetime value (CLV increase)
- Referral rate (badge incentivize referral)

## 6. Legal Compliance: Luật Khuyến Mại VN, Rules Giveaway

### Vietnam Law (Quyết định 27/2015/QĐ-TTG):

**Requirement:**
- **Transparency:** Rules jelas, tidak hide condition
- **Fairness:** Random selection genuine (no manipulation)
- **Disclosure:** Advertiser/organizer identity clear
- **Prize validity:** Prize real dan delivered
- **Eligibility:** Clear who eligible (age, location)
- **Liability:** Organizer liable for prize delivery

**Specific rules:**
- Prize value declare (must match actual)
- Deadline clear (start + end date)
- Winner selection transparent (date, method)
- Withdrawal period: Winner dapat withdraw dari giveaway 30 hari setelah announcement

### Facebook/Instagram/TikTok Platform Rules:

**Prohibited:**
- "Influencer endorse" without disclosure (#ad)
- Personal data misuse
- Misleading prize info
- Fake winner (bukan real account)
- Duplicate entry incentivize

**Requirement:**
- Disclaimer: "This promotion is not endorsed/administered/sponsored by [Platform]"
- Rules publicly visible
- Winner selection documented
- Privacy respect

### Recommendation Practice:

**Template rules:**
"GIVEAWAY RULES

Organizer: [Brand name]
Period: [Start] to [End]
Eligibility: Vietnam resident, age 18+
How to enter: [Method]
Prize: [Item description + value]
Winner selection: Random draw, [date time]
Winner notification: Email within 48h
Prize delivery: Within 14 days
Withdrawal period: 30 days from announcement
Disclaimer: This promotion not endorsed by [Platform]

[Brand terms link]"

**Safety practice:**
- Publish rules BEFORE launching game
- Save draw evidence (screenshot, date time)
- Verify winner real (check profile, engagement)
- Deliver prize documented (screenshot shipping receipt)
- Keep records 1 year

## 7. Metrics: Engagement, Lead, Sales

**Engagement metrics:**
- Participation rate (% audience yang ikutan)
- Comment/share volume
- Reach amplify (organic reach comparison baseline)
- Viral coefficient (sharing rate)

**Lead metrics:**
- Email collected
- Signup conversion
- Quiz completion rate
- Lead quality (active vs inactive)

**Sales metrics:**
- Conversion minigame → purchase
- AOV (average order value) minigame participant vs non-participant
- Repeat purchase rate (loyalty game impact)

**Benchmark:**
- Participation rate: 5-15% (good: 10%+)
- Email collection: 20-40% (good: 30%+)
- Conversion rate: 5-20% (good: 10%+)
- Repeat purchase: 30-50% (good: 40%+)

**Sample calculation:**
- 1M reach → 100k engage → 10k email → 1k purchase → $10k revenue
- Cost game development: $500-2000
- ROI: 5-20x

## 8. Minigame Calendar (Kapan Jalankan)

**Timing strategy:**

**High engagement time:**
- Around holiday (Tết, Valentine, 8/3)
- Friday evening - Sunday (leisure time)
- Lunch time 12-1pm, evening 6-8pm
- Email: Tuesday-Thursday (open rate highest)

**Frequency:**
- 1 big game per tháng (lucky draw, giveaway)
- 2-3 small game per tháng (quiz, poll, caption)
- Daily small game (spin wheel, loyalty check-in)

**Calendar sample:**

**January:**
- Weekly quiz (skin type, product recommendation)
- Countdown minigame (Tết prep)
- Loyalty point bonus (new year)

**February:**
- Tết spin wheel (main event game)
- Valentine quiz (match product)
- Lucky draw (biggest giveaway)

**March:**
- Caption this (post-event engagement)
- 8/3 poll (women preference)
- Referral challenge (refer friends, earn point)

**April:**
- Quiz (season preference)
- Poll (color preference)
- Check-in challenge (store visit)

... continue each month based on seasonal event

## 9. Budget Planning: Cost Game Development + Prize

### Cost breakdown:

**Game development:**
- Simple (quiz, poll, spin): $100-500 (use free tools)
- Medium (custom game, integration): $500-2000 (freelancer)
- Complex (app game, real-time): $2000-10000 (developer)

**Platform cost:**
- Native (Facebook poll, Instagram story): Free
- Third-party (Gleam, Riddle, Interact): $0-500/month
- In-house development: $1000-5000 one-time

**Prize cost:**
- Small game (10% discount code): $0 (cost nothing)
- Medium game (product free): $50-500 per game (product cost)
- Large game (big prize): $1000-5000 per game (cash/product)

**Sample budget $10k/year:**
- Game development: $2000 (initial setup)
- Prize pool: $5000 (big giveaway 2x + small prize ongoing)
- Tool subscription: $1500 (Gleam/Interact)
- Promo amplify: $1500 (ads to promote game)

### ROI estimation:

If game drive $20k revenue → ROI 2x. Good investment.
