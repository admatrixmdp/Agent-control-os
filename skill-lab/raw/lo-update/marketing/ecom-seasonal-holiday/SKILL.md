# Seasonal & Holiday Content Marketing Cho TMĐT VN

## 1. Tại Sao Seasonal Content Quan Trọng

Holiday = time when khách spend money. Seasonal content có spike engagement 3-5x normal time.

Data: During Tết, engagement ↑ 400%, conversion ↑ 250%. During Valentine, beauty product sales ↑ 500%.

Smart TMĐT = plan seasonal campaign 3 tháng trước, execute 1 tháng trước, amplify 2 minggu sebelum, close sale dalam event.

## 2. Lịch Lễ Hội VN Đầy Đủ (365 ngày marketing opportunity)

### Q1 (Tháng 1-3):

**Tết Nguyên Đán (Early Feb - Tet period): BIGGEST event**
- Timeline marketing: T-30, T-15, T-7, T-3, T-1, Day 1-3, T+5
- Intensity: Maximum
- Sales opportunity: Highest

**Valentine (14/2): Consumer event**
- Target: Couple, gift-givers
- Product: Jewel, beauty, fashion, gift
- Intensity: High

**8/3 (International Women's Day): Marketing event**
- Target: Women, gift for women
- Product: Fashion, beauty, skincare, gift
- Intensity: High

### Q2 (Tháng 4-6):

**30/4 - 1/5 (Quốc Khánh): National holiday**
- Timeline: T-7, T-3, T-1, T+2
- Theme: Patriotic, travel, family, freedom
- Intensity: Medium

**Ngày của Mẹ (1st Sunday May): Family event**
- Target: Children gift for mom
- Product: Gift-worthy (jewelry, skincare, fashion)
- Intensity: High

**Ngày của Bố (3rd Sunday June): Family event**
- Target: Children gift for dad
- Product: Tech, gadgets, watch, gift
- Intensity: Medium

### Q3 (Tháng 7-9):

**Back to School (Aug-early Sept): Seasonal**
- Target: Students, parents
- Product: Stationery, gadgets, fashion, backpack
- Intensity: High

**Mid-Autumn Festival (15 Sept lunar calendar = ~Oct Western):**
- Target: Families with children
- Product: Gift, decoration, food, lantern
- Intensity: High

**Trung Thu (Moon Festival): Cultural event**
- Timeline: T-14, T-7, T-3, T-1, Day-of
- Theme: Traditional, family, lantern, moon cake
- Intensity: High

### Q4 (Tháng 10-12):

**20/11 (Teacher's Day): Appreciation event**
- Target: Students, gratitude
- Product: Gift-worthy (flowers, gift box, book)
- Intensity: Medium

**Halloween (31/10): Western but trending**
- Target: Youth, Gen Z
- Product: Costume, candy, decoration, party
- Intensity: Low (not traditional VN)

**Christmas (25/12): Western holiday trending VN**
- Target: Urban, younger demographic
- Product: Gift, decoration, gadget, fashion
- Intensity: Medium

**New Year (31/12 - 1/1): Celebration + resolutions**
- Target: Everyone
- Product: Fitness (gym), learning (course), self-care
- Theme: "New year, new me"
- Intensity: High

### Bonus Days (Marketing opportunites):

**Birthdays month:** Every brand capitalize on brand anniversary

**Flash sale days:** 9/9, 12/12, etc (single-digit date auspicious in VN culture)

**Seasonal (not calendar-based):**
- Back-to-school (May-Aug)
- Wedding season (Nov-Jan)
- New office season (Sept)

## 3. Framework Untuk MỖI Dịp (Detail, Replicable)

Cho MỖI dịp, sediakan:

### PART A: TIMELINE MARKETING

**T-30 (1 tháng sebelum):**
- Content: Teaser, awareness
- Channel: Organic social, email
- Goal: Plant idea di mind customer

**T-15 (2 minggu sebelum):**
- Content: Education, benefit-focused
- Channel: Paid ads start, organic social amplify
- Goal: Build desire

**T-7 (1 minggu sebelum):**
- Content: FOMO, urgency, last chance
- Channel: All channel, email daily
- Goal: Trigger purchase intent

**T-3 (3 hari sebelum):**
- Content: Flash deal tease, urgency peak
- Channel: All channel, SMS if have
- Goal: Create urgency, decision-forcing

**T-1 (1 hari sebelum / Eve):**
- Content: Final call, deal reveal, live session
- Channel: All channel, real-time interaction (livestream)
- Goal: Last push before event

**Day 0 (Event day):**
- Content: Live content, customer stories, real-time update
- Channel: Stories, livestream, real-time engagement
- Goal: Momentum, FOMO peak

**Day +1 to +5 (After event):**
- Content: Thank you, case study, testimonial, feedback
- Channel: Email, social, SMS
- Goal: Loyalty, repeat purchase, referral

### PART B: 5 CONTENT IDEAS (SPECIFIC, ACTIONABLE)

### Example: TẾT NGUYÊN ĐÁN

**Content Idea #1: "Tết Preparation Guide" (Educational)**
- Format: Carousel, blog, video
- Hook: "Chuẩn bị Tết, bạn chưa biết cách nào?"
- Content: 10-step guide, mỗi step sản phẩm recommended
- Goal: Education + soft sell
- Timing: T-15

Example: "10 bước chuẩn bị Tết:
1. Deep clean nhà (natural cleaner)
2. Skincare glow-up (serum + mask)
3. New outfit (fashion)
4. ..."

**Content Idea #2: "Customer Tết Story" (Storytelling)**
- Format: Long-form post, video interview
- Hook: "Cách [customer] celebrate Tết dengan [product]"
- Content: Personal story, emotional connection
- Goal: Emotional resonance, relatable
- Timing: T-7

Example: "Tết năm nay mẹ tôi thấy con gái khỏe hơn, xinh hơn. Nhờ serum vitamin C dùng cả năm."

**Content Idea #3: "Tết Lookbook / Styling Guide" (Visual inspiration)**
- Format: Carousel, video, blog
- Hook: "Outfit cho Tết với [budget]"
- Content: 5 styling dari sản phẩm
- Goal: Inspiration, see product in context
- Timing: T-14

Example: "Tết outfit dari áo/pants/shoes chúng tôi - 5 cách mix"

**Content Idea #4: "Flash Deal Tease" (Urgency)**
- Format: Story, carousel, post
- Hook: "Tết sale dự định 50%, but stock limited"
- Content: Sneak peek product, countdown
- Goal: FOMO, anticipation
- Timing: T-7, T-3

**Content Idea #5: "Tết Giveaway / Minigame" (Engagement)**
- Format: Comment-based, story poll, video
- Hook: "Comment để nhận Tết gift"
- Content: Game, random winner, prize
- Goal: Engagement, reach
- Timing: T-3 to Day 0

### PART G: VISUAL STYLE GUIDE (Mood, color, element)

**Tết Visual Theme:**
- Color: Red, gold, white (auspicious)
- Mood: Warm, joyful, family-oriented, traditional+modern blend
- Element: Lantern, flower (plum), lucky money, family gathering
- Font: Serif (traditional) + modern sans (balance)
- Photo style: Warm lighting, close-up family moments, product lifestyle
- Avoid: Dark color (unlucky), individual solo shot (highlight family)

**Valentine Visual Theme:**
- Color: Red, pink, gold
- Mood: Romantic, tender, intimate
- Element: Rose, heart, couple, candlelight
- Font: Script, elegant
- Photo style: Soft focus, couple, close-up, emotional moment
- Avoid: Sad imagery, solo sad person

**8/3 Visual Theme:**
- Color: Pink, gold, purple, white
- Mood: Celebratory, powerful, confident, joyful
- Element: Flower (tulip, rose), woman empowerment, mirror, self-care ritual
- Font: Bold, modern, confident
- Photo style: Woman confident, self-care ritual, empowerment, diverse representation
- Avoid: Damsel-in-distress imagery, passive woman

## 4. CONTENT TẾT CHI TIẾT (Quan trọng nhất, có many sub-periods)

Tết VN phức tạp. Có 4 sub-period, mỗi period có vibe khác:

### TẾT SUB-PERIOD #1: PRE-TẾT (T-30 to T-8)
**Vibe:** Preparation, urgency, spring clean, glow-up
**Content focus:** How to prepare, product recommendations, cleaning guide, skincare routine
**Promotions:** Early-bird discount, pre-book discount
**KPI:** Awareness, education, lead generation
**Example posts:**
- "10 bước chuẩn bị nhà cho Tết"
- "Tết glow-up guide - skincare routine 3 tuần"
- "Tết outfit ideas - mix & match guide"
- "Tết makeup look - tutorial 5 style"

### TẾT SUB-PERIOD #2: FINAL WEEK (T-7 to T-2)
**Vibe:** Last-minute shopping, urgency peak, excitement
**Content focus:** Final reminder, stock update, quick delivery guarantee, last call
**Promotions:** Flash deal, free shipping, last-minute bundle
**KPI:** Traffic, conversion, sales
**Example posts:**
- "Tết sale final week - stock update"
- "Last minute Tết gift ideas - ship today"
- "Tết surprise bundle - limited stock"
- "5 reason bạn should order TODAY"

### TẾT SUB-PERIOD #3: TẾT EVE + DAYS (T-1 to T+3)
**Vibe:** Celebration, gratitude, family, joy, real-time engagement
**Content focus:** Live content, customer stories, thank you, family moment, behind-the-scenes
**Promotions:** Last minute order, thank you gift, loyalty bonus
**KPI:** Engagement, community building, brand love
**Example posts:**
- "Live unboxing - Tết morning surprise"
- "Customer Tết story - thank you video"
- "Team Tết celebration - behind the scene"
- "Thank you note - appreciate customer"
- "Tết memory share - tag photo"

### TẾT SUB-PERIOD #4: POST-TẾT (T+4 to T+14)
**Vibe:** Reflection, case study, loyalty, next step
**Content focus:** Customer testimonial, case study, feedback request, referral incentive
**Promotions:** Thank you loyalty gift, referral bonus, VIP early access next sale
**KPI:** Case study collection, referral, loyalty building
**Example posts:**
- "Tết feedback - bagaimana experience bamu?"
- "Customer before/after Tết"
- "Thank you Tết sale summary - recap"
- "Referral bonus - invite friend untuk benefit"

## 8. Seasonal Metrics & KPI

Track per seasonal campaign:

**Campaign KPI:**
- Traffic ↑ (% increase vs baseline)
- Conversion rate ↑ (% increase)
- AOV (average order value) ↑
- Email open rate
- Paid ads ROAS
- Organic reach

**Benchmark seasonal vs non-seasonal:**
- Tết: 3-5x traffic, 2-3x conversion
- Valentine: 2-3x traffic, 1.5-2x conversion
- 8/3: 2-3x traffic, 1.5-2x conversion
- Back-to-school: 2-3x traffic, 1.5-2x conversion

**Success metric:**
- Revenue seasonal campaign > 40% annual revenue
- Loyalty customer from seasonal = higher LTV (repeat purchase)

## 9. Integration Seasonal + Regular Content

Don't abandon regular content during seasonal. Mix:

- 60% seasonal content (during T-30 to T+5)
- 40% regular content (education, product feature, brand story)

Reason: Avoid appear "only care about selling during holiday". Maintain brand value-add, education, community.

## 10. Common Seasonal Mistakes & Fix

**Mistake 1: Start too late**
- Fix: Plan T-60, produce T-30, launch T-15

**Mistake 2: Oversell, undersell value**
- Fix: 70% education/value, 30% promotion

**Mistake 3: Same content every year**
- Fix: Refresh every year, new angle, new product

**Mistake 4: Ignore cultural sensitivity**
- Fix: Research cultural meaning, respect tradition, don't over-commercialize

**Mistake 5: Forget post-event follow-up**
- Fix: Plan T+7 case study, thank you, loyalty capture

Seasonal content is goldmine. Plan well, execute consistent, measure result, iterate yearly.
