# SKILL: Phân Tích Đối Thủ Cạnh Tranh E-commerce

## I. GIỚI THIỆU

Phân tích đối thủ (competitor analysis) là quá trình nghiên cứu kỹ các cạnh tranh chính của bạn trong thị trường. Mục đích:
- Hiểu rõ market landscape
- Xác định gaps & opportunities
- Học hỏi từ cách họ làm
- Xây dựng competitive advantage
- Đặt ra mục tiêu realistic

---

## II. FRAMEWORK PHÂN TÍCH 7 DIMENSIONS

### Dimension 1: PRODUCT (Sản Phẩm)

**Những gì cần phân tích**:

**A. Product Range & Variety**
- Số lượng SKU (stock keeping unit) họ bán
- Danh mục sản phẩm
- Mix giữa bestseller vs niche products
- Private label products (sản phẩm riêng)
- Exclusive products

**Ví dụ phân tích**:
```
Competitor A:
- 500 SKU across 5 categories
- 70% imported, 30% local
- Private label: 10% of sales
- Best sellers: Ao/Quan, Giay, Tui xach

Competitor B:
- 200 SKU across 2 categories
- 100% local/artisan products
- Unique: Handmade items (private label 50%)
- Niche audience: Sustainable fashion
```

**B. Product Quality & Features**
- Material quality (premium vs budget)
- Craftsmanship
- Unique features (e.g., waterproof)
- Sustainability/eco-friendly
- Durability/warranty

**Bảng so sánh**:
| Dimension | Competitor A | Competitor B | Bạn |
|-----------|------------|------------|-----|
| Material quality | 7/10 | 9/10 | ? |
| Durability | 6/10 | 9/10 | ? |
| Uniqueness | 5/10 | 8/10 | ? |
| Price point | Medium | Premium | ? |

**C. Product Innovation**
- Có ra sản phẩm mới thường không?
- Trend-following vs trend-setting?
- Seasonal collections?
- Limited editions?
- Customization options?

**D. Sizing & Fit**
- Size range: XS-4XL?
- Fit guide: Detailed?
- Model height/weight transparency?
- Alterations offered?
- Return policy on fit issues?

### Dimension 2: PRICE (Giá)

**Những gì cần phân tích**:

**A. Price Points**
- Entry level price: Bao nhiêu?
- Average price: AOV?
- Premium price: Max?
- Price by category (áo $10, quần $20, etc)

**Ví dụ**:
```
Competitor A Price:
- Entry: $5 (basic t-shirt)
- Average: $25
- Premium: $80 (special items)
- AOV: $30-40

Competitor B Price:
- Entry: $15 (minimalist design)
- Average: $45
- Premium: $120
- AOV: $50-60 (premium positioning)
```

**B. Discount Strategy**
- Regular discounts? 10%, 20%, 50%?
- When do they discount?
  * Seasonal: 11.11, Black Friday?
  * Clearance sales?
  * Member exclusive?
  * Flash sales?
- Discount frequency: Weekly, monthly, quarterly?
- Min purchase for discount?

**Ví dụ discount strategy**:
```
Competitor A:
- 11.11: 50% off
- Black Friday: 40% off
- Weekly: -10% on selected items
- Member: Additional 5% off
- Frequency: Very frequent (dilutes brand value)

Competitor B:
- 11.11: 30% off (selective)
- Black Friday: 25% off
- Monthly exclusive: 15% for members only
- Frequency: Strategic (maintains brand value)
```

**C. Pricing Psychology**
- Price ending: $9.99 vs $10?
- Bundle pricing: 2 for $40 vs 2x$25?
- Tiered pricing: Different prices by volume?
- Psychological anchoring: Original price shown?

**D. Payment Terms**
- Installment options? 3x, 6x payment?
- Interest-free financing?
- COD? Bank transfer? E-wallet?
- Which method is prominently shown?

**Price Positioning Matrix**:
```
          Luxury (High Price)
                  ↑
       Premium    |    Competitor B
                  |
Average Price     |    BẠNCÓ
                  |
Budget/Value      |    Competitor A
                  |
         Mass-market (Low Price)

High Quality ← Quality → Low Quality
```

## III. SPY CONTENT: DETAILED ANALYSIS

### What to Track

**A. Content Frequency**
- How many posts per day?
  * Facebook: Counting
  * Instagram: Feed posts, stories, reels
  * TikTok: Daily uploads?
  * YouTube: Video schedule

**Example**:
```
Competitor A Social Posting:
- Facebook: 2-3 posts/day (high volume)
- Instagram: 1 post/day + 5 stories (consistent)
- TikTok: 0-1 videos/day (low priority)
- YouTube: 1 video/month (sporadic)

Competitor B Social Posting:
- Facebook: 1 post/day (strategic)
- Instagram: 1 post/day + 3 stories (quality)
- TikTok: 2-3 videos/day (high priority)
- YouTube: 1 video/week (consistent)
```

**B. Content Types & Formats**
Track what formats get the most engagement:

**Facebook**:
- Single image posts
- Carousel posts (5-10 images)
- Video posts
- Live streams
- Poll posts

**Instagram**:
- Feed posts (1 image, 5-image carousel)
- Stories (daily)
- Reels (short videos)
- IGTV (longer videos)

**TikTok**:
- Trending sounds
- Challenges
- Behind-the-scenes
- Duets/stitches
- Product demos

**YouTube**:
- Product reviews
- How-to tutorials
- Hauls
- Vlogs
- Unboxing

**Content Type Analysis**:
```
Competitor A Top Formats:
1. Carousel posts (Facebook) - 45K avg reach
2. Reels (Instagram) - 25K avg reach
3. Stories (Instagram) - 15K avg reach
4. Video posts (Facebook) - 20K avg reach
5. Single image (Instagram) - 8K avg reach

Competitor B Top Formats:
1. TikTok videos - 500K+ avg views
2. Reels (Instagram) - 50K avg reach
3. Carousel posts (Facebook) - 30K avg reach
4. YouTube videos - 50K+ avg views
5. Stories (Instagram) - 20K avg reach
```

**C. Top Performing Content**
Identify their most viral/engaging posts:

**Tracking**:
- Top 5 posts by engagement (likes + comments + shares)
- Top 5 videos by views
- Top 5 reels by views
- Most saved/bookmarked posts

**Analysis**:
```
Competitor A Top Content:
1. Carousel: "10 styling tips" - 15K engagement
2. Reels: Trending sound + product - 8K engagement
3. UGC: Customer testimonial - 6K engagement
4. Poll: "Which color?" - 4K engagement
5. Behind-scenes: Factory tour - 3K engagement

Pattern: Educational + trending sounds + UGC perform best
```

**D. Hashtag Strategy**
- Which hashtags do they use?
- How many hashtags per post? (typically 10-30)
- Are they using trending vs branded hashtags?
- Success rate: Do their hashtags get engagement?

**Hashtag Analysis**:
```
Competitor A Hashtags:
- Branded: #CompetitorA (50K followers)
- Product: #[ProductName] (100K+)
- Trending: #Fashion, #Style, #OOTD (1M+)

**E. Engagement Rate Analysis**
Calculate their average engagement:

**Formula**:
```
Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares) / (Followers) × 100
```

**Benchmark**:
```

- Followers: 50,000

Competitor B:
- Followers: 30,000

**F. Audience Demographics** (if visible)

## VIII. SWOT ANALYSIS TEMPLATE

### Strengths (Điểm mạnh)

**Competitor A Strengths**:
- ✓ Lowest price in market ($10 vs $25)
- ✓ Established brand (10 years)
- ✓ Available on all major platforms
- ✓ Fast shipping (2-3 days)
- ✓ High volume marketing budget

**Your Strengths**:
- ✓ Better customer service (respond within 1 hour)
- ✓ Premium materials (better quality)
- ✓ Unique designs (not mass-produced)
- ✓ Social responsibility (CSR programs)
- ✓ Loyal community (NPS 70+)

### Weaknesses (Điểm yếu)

**Competitor A Weaknesses**:
- ✗ Poor quality (customer complaints frequent)
- ✗ Bad customer service (slow responses)
- ✗ Low repeat purchase rate (25%)
- ✗ High return rate (5%)
- ✗ No community engagement

**Your Weaknesses**:
- ✗ Lower brand awareness (new market entrant)
- ✗ Limited inventory (startup size)
- ✗ Higher price point (premium positioning)
- ✗ Smaller marketing budget
- ✗ Limited color/size options

### Opportunities (Cơ hội)

**Market Opportunities**:
- Growing demand for sustainable fashion (+15% YoY)
- E-commerce growth in VN (20% YoY)
- TikTok commerce rising (untapped by Competitor A)
- Corporate gifting trend (B2B opportunity)
- Premium market growing (willing to pay for quality)

**Competitor-specific Opportunities**:
- Steal Competitor A customers with quality + good service
- Target Competitor B's premium niche with better value
- Build TikTok community (Competitor A weak here)
- Start loyalty program (they lack this)
- Influencer collaborations (differentiated approach)

### Threats (Mối đe dọa)

**External Threats**:
- Global fast fashion brands entering VN
- Raw material prices rising (squeeze margins)
- Supply chain disruptions
- Increased competition

**Competitor-specific Threats**:

## X. ACTION PLAN: NHỮNG GÌ LÀM TỐT HƠN

### Phân tích Competitor A (Budget leader)

**Những gì họ làm tốt**:
1. Aggressive pricing ($10 vs $25)
2. High volume advertising (everywhere)
3. Fast shipping (2-3 days)
4. Wide product range (500+ SKU)

**Các lĩnh vực yếu**:
1. Quality (poor materials, fading)
2. Customer service (slow responses)
3. Repeat purchase (only 25%)
4. Brand loyalty (replaceable)

**Action plan để đánh bại**:
1. **Match their logistics**: Negotiate with courier for 2-3 day shipping
2. **Exceed their quality**: Use premium materials, be clear about it
3. **Own their weakness**: "Tired of low quality? Try [Brand] for premium without premium price"
4. **Target their customers**: Run retargeting ads for "quality upgrade"
5. **Highlight feedback**: Show reviews comparing quality: "Lasts 1 year vs 3 years"

### Phân tích Competitor B (Premium leader)

**Những gì họ làm tốt**:
1. Premium positioning (high price, justified)
2. Exceptional quality (durable)
3. Strong values (sustainable)
4. Great customer service (95% response rate)

**Các lĩnh vực yếu**:
1. Limited product range (200 SKU)
2. High price point (inaccessible to many)
3. Slower growth (niche market)
4. Complex checkout (3+ steps)

**Action plan để đánh bại**:
1. **Own the mid-market**: "Premium quality, accessible price"
2. **Expand product range**: Offer more variety than they do
3. **Faster checkout**: Optimize 1-page checkout
4. **Same values, better price**: "Sustainable AND affordable"
5. **Undercut slightly**: $18 vs their $25 for same quality
6. **Target their customers**: Run retargeting ads for price-conscious premium buyers
