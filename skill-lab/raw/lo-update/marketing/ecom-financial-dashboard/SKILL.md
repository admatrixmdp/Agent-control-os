## PHẦN 2: UNIT ECONOMICS

### 2.1. CAC - Customer Acquisition Cost

**CAC = How much you spend to get 1 new customer**

**Calculation:**

```
Total marketing spend / Number of new customers = CAC

Example:
- Month 1 Marketing spend: 8,300,000 đ
- New customers acquired: 200
- CAC: 8,300,000 / 200 = 41,500 đ per customer

Breakdown by channel:
Facebook Ads: 3,000,000 → 100 new customers → 30,000đ CAC
Shopee Ads: 2,500,000 → 60 new customers → 41,666đ CAC
Influencer: 2,000,000 → 30 new customers → 66,666đ CAC
Other: 800,000 → 10 new customers → 80,000đ CAC

Channel efficiency ranking:
1. Facebook: 30K CAC (best)
2. Shopee: 41.7K CAC
3. Influencer: 66.7K CAC
4. Other: 80K CAC (worst)

Action: Increase Facebook budget, reduce "Other" channels
```

**CAC payback period:**

```
Formula: CAC / (AOV × Margin)

Example:
- CAC: 41,500đ
- AOV: 500,000đ
- Margin: 50%
- Profit per order: 250,000đ

Payback: 41,500 / 250,000 = 0.166 orders = 16.6% of first order
→ Break even on first order! ✓ (Customer pays for themselves)
```

**CAC benchmark:**
- Good CAC: Should be covered by first 1-2 orders
- Bad CAC: Requires 3+ orders to break even (risky)

### 2.2. LTV - Customer Lifetime Value

**LTV = How much customer worth over their entire relationship with you**

**Calculation method 1: Actual historical**

```
Sum all revenue from customer / Total customers

Example: 1,000 customers spent total 2,000,000,000đ
LTV = 2,000,000,000 / 1,000 = 2,000,000đ per customer

Reality: Not all customers are equal
- Champions: LTV = 10,000,000đ (20% of customers)
- Loyal: LTV = 3,000,000đ (30% of customers)
- Potential: LTV = 1,000,000đ (25% of customers)
- New: LTV = 500,000đ (25% of customers)

Weighted average:
(0.2 × 10M) + (0.3 × 3M) + (0.25 × 1M) + (0.25 × 500K) = 4,000,000đ
```

**Calculation method 2: Forward-looking projection**

```
Formula: AOV × Purchase frequency × Customer lifespan × (1 - Churn rate)

Example:
- AOV: 500,000đ (average order value)
- Frequency: 3x per year
- Lifespan: 3 years (before customer stops buying)
- Churn rate: 30% (lose 30% of customers annually)

LTV = 500K × 3 × 3 × (1 - 0.3) = 500K × 3 × 3 × 0.7 = 3,150,000đ
```

**LTV by segment:**

```
Champions (top 20% spenders):
- AOV: 1,000,000đ (buy premium)
- Frequency: 6x per year (very frequent)
- Lifespan: 5 years (loyal long-term)
- Churn: 5% (very low)
- LTV = 1M × 6 × 5 × 0.95 = 28,500,000đ per champion!

Loyal:
- AOV: 500,000đ
- Frequency: 4x per year
- Lifespan: 3 years
- Churn: 20%
- LTV = 500K × 4 × 3 × 0.8 = 4,800,000đ

Potential:
- AOV: 300,000đ
- Frequency: 2x per year
- Lifespan: 2 years
- Churn: 50%
- LTV = 300K × 2 × 2 × 0.5 = 600,000đ
```

**Action: Increase LTV**

```
1. Increase AOV (sell more per order):
   - Upsell: "Bundle 3, get 20% off" (300K → 360K AOV +20%)
   - Cross-sell: "Pair with toner, 15% discount"
   - Premium products: Offer luxury line alongside budget

2. Increase Frequency (make them buy more often):
   - Subscription: "Get serum monthly, save 15%"
   - Loyalty: "3 more purchases = free gift"
   - Reminder: Email after 30 days "Time to reorder?"

3. Extend Lifespan (keep them longer):
   - Better service: Reduce churn from 30% to 20%
   - Loyalty program: Tier benefits keep them engaged
   - New products: Keep launching new items to re-excite

4. Reduce Churn (keep them coming back):
   - Target: Reduce 30% churn to 15%
   - Action: Win-back campaigns, loyalty rewards, VIP treatment
   - Impact: Would increase LTV from 3M to 4.2M (+40%!)
```

### 2.3. LTV:CAC Ratio

**Most important metric for unit economics**

```
Formula: LTV / CAC
Target: 3:1 or higher

Example:
- LTV: 3,000,000đ
- CAC: 41,500đ
- Ratio: 3,000,000 / 41,500 = 72:1 ✓✓✓ (EXCELLENT!)

Interpretation:
- For every 41.5K you spend acquiring customer
- They return 3M in lifetime value
- Profit: 3M - 41.5K = 2,958,500đ profit per customer
- ROI: 2,958,500 / 41,500 = 71:1 return!

Breakeven ratio (minimum acceptable):
- Below 1:1 = You lose money per customer (❌ Stop!)
- 1:1 = Break even (⚠️ Not sustainable)
- 2:1 = Barely profitable, risky (⚠️)
- 3:1 = Good, sustainable (✓)
- 5:1 = Very healthy (✓✓)
- 10:1 = Exceptional (✓✓✓)

If ratio is bad:
1. Option A: Reduce CAC (cheaper marketing)
2. Option B: Increase LTV (sell more per customer)
3. Option C: Improve retention (longer customer lifetime)
```

### 2.4. Payback Period

**How many days/months until you recoup customer acquisition cost**

```
Formula: CAC / (Daily profit per customer)

Example:
- CAC: 41,500đ
- First purchase revenue: 500,000đ
- COGS + platform fee: 250,000đ (50% of revenue)
- Profit from first purchase: 250,000đ
- Payback days: 41,500 / 250,000 = 0.166 days = 4 hours! ✓

(You recoup your acquisition cost within FIRST ORDER!)

Interpretation:
- If payback <30 days: Excellent (can scale aggressively)
- If payback 30-90 days: Good (can scale moderately)
- If payback 90-180 days: Risky (need strong retention)
- If payback >180 days: Dangerous (customer must buy 2+ times)
```

---

### 2.5. Contribution Margin per Order

**How much profit you make per order (after COGS + direct costs)**

```
Formula: (AOV - COGS - Direct variable costs) / AOV × 100

Example:
- AOV: 500,000đ
- COGS: 200,000đ (40%)
- Direct variable costs:
  - Shipping: 25,000đ
  - Packaging: 5,000đ
  - Payment gateway (1.5%): 7,500đ
  - Platform fee (5%): 25,000đ
  - Total direct: 62,500đ

Contribution margin: (500K - 200K - 62.5K) / 500K = 47.5%
Profit per order: 237,500đ

Interpretation:
- Out of 500K order, 237.5K is "contribution"
- This covers marketing, salaries, rent, and profits
- High contribution margin = More sustainable business
```

### 3.2. Cost reduction strategies

**Where to cut costs:**

```
1. REDUCE COGS (Biggest impact)
   Current: 35% → Target: 32% (-3% points = 30K per 1M revenue)
   How:
   - Negotiate better rates with suppliers
   - Bulk purchase (commit to 1000 units, save 5-10%)
   - Find alternative suppliers
   - Reduce packaging cost (simpler box)
   Impact: -3% = Extra 30K profit per 1M revenue

2. REDUCE PLATFORM FEES (Limited control)
   Current: 6% → Target: 5.5% (by joining Mall)
   How:
   - Join Shopee Mall (get 0.5% fee reduction)
   - Use own website (0% fees, but harder to drive traffic)
   Impact: -0.5% = 5K extra per 1M revenue

3. REDUCE SHIPPING (30-50% is negotiable)
   Current: 3% → Target: 2% (by partnering with logistics)
   How:
   - Negotiate with GHN/Giao hàng nhanh for bulk rates
   - Use zone-based pricing (cheaper for nearby areas)
   - Offer customer pickup points
   Impact: -1% = 10K extra per 1M revenue

4. OPTIMIZE MARKETING (Most flexible)
   Current: 15% → Target: 12% (by improving efficiency)
   How:
   - Cut low-ROI channels (>1% CAC:LTV ratio)
   - Double-down on high-ROI channels
   - Improve conversion rate (less traffic needed)
   Impact: -3% = 30K extra per 1M revenue

5. REDUCE OVERHEAD (Small but easy)
   Current: 5% → Target: 3% (squeeze efficiency)
   How:
   - Work from home (no rent)
   - Automate customer service (chatbot)
   - Reduce team size / freelance
   Impact: -2% = 20K extra per 1M revenue

Total opportunity: -9% of revenue (90K per 1M)
From 25% profit margin → 34% profit margin!
```

## PHẦN 4: CASH FLOW MANAGEMENT

### 4.1. Cash flow cycle

**Key insight: Revenue ≠ Cash in hand!**

```
Timeline of money movement:

DAY 0: Customer buys on Shopee
- Revenue recorded: 500,000đ ✓
- Cash in your account: 0đ ❌

DAY 1: You ship product
- You pay logistics: -25,000đ (out of pocket!)
- Cash position: -25,000đ ❌

DAY 5: Customer receives product
- Revenue still: 500,000đ
- Cash still: -25,000đ ❌

DAY 7: Shopee pays you
- Shopee sends: 500,000 - (5% platform fee) = 475,000đ
- Cash position: +450,000đ ✓ (Net of logistics paid earlier)

RESULT: 7-day lag between sale and cash receipt!

For 1000 sales/day:
- Daily revenue: 500M
- Shopee payment 7 days later: 475M
- But you need cash TODAY for inventory, shipping, payroll!
- Cash gap: 7 days × 500M / 30 days = 116M working capital needed!
```

**Payment cycles from sàn:**

```
Shopee:
- Standard: 2-7 days (varies by shop tier)
- Premium shop: 1-2 days
- Settlement frequency: Usually 2-3x per week
- If you have 20M in pending orders, might wait 5 days = 20M tied up

Lazada:
- Standard: 3-7 days
- Settlement: Usually once per week
- Same issue: If 20M pending, up to 7 days wait

TikTok Shop:
- Usually: 3-5 days
- Settlement: Daily or weekly (varies)

Own website (if you have):
- If credit card: 2-3 days (payment processor hold)
- If bank transfer: 1-2 days
- If COD: 5-15 days (logistics collect, then pay you)
```

**COD (Cash on Delivery) impact:**

```
COD challenge:
- Customer pays logistics on delivery
- Logistics pays you 5-7 days later
- Your cash lag: 5-7 days PLUS you pay for inventory/shipping upfront!

Example with 100 COD orders/day at 500K each:
- Daily revenue: 50M
- But you paid for inventory yesterday: -20M (COGS)
- You paid for shipping: -2.5M
- Cash gap day 1: -22.5M (negative!)
- Logistics pays you 7 days later: +47.5M
- Finally: +25M cash

COD is cash flow killer if >50% of orders!
Best practice: Mix COD (40%) + prepaid (60%)
```

### 4.3. Seasonal cash flow

**Plan for seasonal peaks & troughs:**

```
Typical e-commerce seasonal pattern (Vietnam):

Jan-Feb (Tết): ⬆️⬆️⬆️ Peak sales
- Valentine: Mid-Feb peak
- Women's day March 8: Peak
- Tet (Feb): People have bonus, spend money

March-April: ➡️ Normal

May-June: ⬇️ Slow (summer, students on break, less spending)

July-Aug: ➡️ Slight rise (back to school)

Sept-Oct: ➡️ Normal

Nov-Dec: ⬆️⬆️ Peak (Black Friday, Christmas)

Pattern: 2 major peaks (Tết + Black Friday)
         1 low valley (summer)
         Rest = baseline

Cash flow implication:

Baseline revenue: 80M/month
Tết season: 150M/month (87% increase!)
Summer: 50M/month (37% decrease)
Black Friday: 160M/month

Cash planning:
- Jan-Feb: Accumulate 100M cash (from peak sales)
- May-June: Use accumulated cash to cover low sales
- Nov-Dec: Another accumulation period

If you don't plan:
- High season: Too much cash, stuck in inventory
- Low season: Not enough cash to pay suppliers
```

```

Calculation:

- Net profit: 400M
- Tax: 400M × 10% = 40M

Payment:

- Annual filing: Jan 1 - Dec 31, file by Feb 28 next year
- Payment method: Bank transfer, tax office, or intermediary
```

### 6.2. Thuế GTGT (VAT)

**If revenue >100M/year (must register):**

```
VAT rate: 10% (main rate) or 0% (exports, some services)

Calculation:
- Input VAT (paid to suppliers): 40M (roughly 35% × 1,205M × 10%)
- Output VAT (charged to customers): 120.5M (10% × 1,205M)
- VAT to pay: 120.5M - 40M = 80.5M per year
- Monthly: 6.7M

Or can use simplified: 5% of gross if under 200M revenue/year

Payment:
- Monthly or quarterly (choose 1)
- File by 30th of next month
```

**Reality in Vietnam (sàn TMĐT):**

```
Most sellers use simplified tax:
- Pay 5% of gross revenue as "personal income"
- Don't worry about VAT separately
- Much easier, don't need accounting

Example:
- Monthly revenue: 100M
- Tax owed: 100M × 5% = 5M (simple!)
- Vs precise calculation: could be 4-6M anyway
```

## PHẦN 7: PRICING & MARGIN OPTIMIZATION

### 7.1. Minimum price formula

**Never sell below this:**

```
Minimum price = COGS + All direct costs + Target margin

Example:
- COGS: 200,000đ
- Shipping cost (you absorb): 25,000đ
- Platform fee (5%): Assume 25,000đ (apply after price known)
- Packaging: 5,000đ
- Payment processing (1.5%): Assume 7,500đ
- Direct variable total: 62,500đ
- Target margin: 50,000đ (10% of COGS, profit)

Raw minimum: 200K + 62.5K + 50K = 312.5K

But this assumes 5% platform fee on unknown final price!

Solve:
- Let P = final price
- Platform fee = 5% × P
- Price - COGS - direct costs - platform fee = 50K profit
- P - 200K - 37.5K (non-platform) - 0.05P = 50K
- 0.95P = 287.5K
- P = 302,632đ

Round to: 305,000đ or 309,000đ (psychological price point)

Verification:
- Price: 305K
- Platform fee (5%): 15.25K
- COGS: 200K
- Other direct: 37.5K
- Profit: 305K - 15.25K - 200K - 37.5K = 52.25K ✓
```

---

### 7.2. Margin by product category

**Different categories support different margins:**

```
HIGH MARGIN (40-60% margin acceptable):
- Beauty/cosmetics: 50-60% (COGS low, customers willing to pay)
- Accessories: 40-50%
- Digital products: 90%+ (no COGS!)

MEDIUM MARGIN (25-40%):
- Fashion: 35-45% (bulk COGS, import)
- Home décor: 30-40%
- Books: 25-35%

LOW MARGIN (10-25%):
- Electronics: 10-20% (high COGS, competitive)
- Groceries: 15-25%
- Bulk goods: 10-20%

Strategy:
- Sell low-margin items to build volume + loyalty
- Sell high-margin items for profit
- Mix = Sustainable business (e.g., 60% low-margin + 40% high-margin)
```

---

### 7.3. Promotional discounting formula

**When to discount safely:**

```
Rule: Don't discount more than (Margin % - 10%)

Example:
- Product margin: 60%
- Safe discount: Maximum 50% off
- Reason: After 50% off, you still have 10% margin to cover other costs

Practical:
- Normal price: 500K
- Margin: 60% (300K profit per unit)
- Flash sale: 250K (50% off)
- New margin: 10% (50K profit)
- Just enough to cover admin/overhead

Too aggressive:
- Super sale: 200K (60% off)
- Negative margin: You lose 100K per unit!
- Avoid!

Discounting psychology:
- 5-10% off: "Small discount, still good profit"
- 20-30% off: "Good sale, can afford this"
- 40-50% off: "Major clearance, acceptable if temporary"
- 60%+ off: "Loss-leader, only for volume"

Time-limited rule:
- Weekly 10% discount: OK (frequent, small)
- Monthly 30% sale: OK (big but rare)
- Daily 50% off: DEATH (unsustainable, trains customers to wait)
```

## PHẦN 10: SUMMARY & ACTION PLAN

**Quick financial checklist:**

```

  ☐ Create P&L template (Google Sheets)
  ☐ Calculate current month: Revenue, COGS, profit

  ☐ Identify top 3 cost reduction opportunities
```
