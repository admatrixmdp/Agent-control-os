# CHIẾN LƯỢC TIKTOK MARKETING & TMĐT VIỆT NAM

## 1. TIKTOK ORGANIC STRATEGY

### 1.1 Content Pillars cho TMĐT trên TikTok

**Pillar 1: Product Showcase (25% of content)**
- Purpose: Display products in lifestyle context
- Formats: Unboxing, product features, color variations
- Tone: Casual, authentic
- Goals: Product awareness, engagement

**Pillar 2: Customer Stories & Testimonials (20%)**
- Purpose: Build trust through real customers
- Formats: Before-after, customer review, use case
- Tone: Authentic, emotional
- Goals: Social proof, conversion intent

**Pillar 3: Behind the Scenes (15%)**
- Purpose: Show brand humanity
- Formats: Warehouse tour, team introduction, product creation
- Tone: Fun, transparent
- Goals: Brand connection, audience loyalty

**Pillar 4: Trend + Meme (25%)**
- Purpose: Entertain, stay relevant
- Formats: Trending sounds, challenges, parodies
- Tone: Fun, relatable, funny
- Goals: Viral potential, algorithm favor

**Pillar 5: Educational (15%)**
- Purpose: Provide value
- Formats: Tutorial, tips, how-to, industry insights
- Tone: Expert, helpful
- Goals: Authority, engagement, watch time

### 1.2 Video Format Guide

**POV (Point of View)**
- Frame: First-person perspective (as if viewer doing action)
- Script: "You when...", "Me when..."
- Duration: 15-30s
- Performance: High relatability, viral potential
- VN example: "Bạn khi đi mua áo mới" (when you go buy new shirt)
- Hook: Visual first 1s must be interesting

**Tutorial/How-to**
- Frame: Overhead or front camera
- Content: Step-by-step instructions
- Duration: 15-45s
- Graphics: Text overlays, arrows, zoom effects
- VN example: "Cách chọn size áo phù hợp" (how to pick right shirt size)
- Best for: Educational content pillar

**Review + Unboxing**
- Frame: Hands close-up, product handling
- Process: Open, show, demo, final thoughts
- Duration: 20-45s
- Tone: Genuine reactions, honest opinion
- VN example: "Unboxing Samsung Galaxy A12"
- Hook: First 3s show dramatic moment (opening, price reveal)

**Before/After Transformation**
- Frame: Split screen or video transition
- Content: Result showcase
- Duration: 15-30s
- Caution: Avoid health claims without evidence
- Good for: Fashion, furniture, skincare (safe)
- Hook: Show extreme difference in first 2s

**Trend/Sound integration**
- Use trending sounds from TikTok
- Customize for your product
- Duration: Must fit sound (usually 15-45s)
- Algorithm: Trending sounds get 3-5x more reach
- VN example: Use trending music + product placement

**Story/Narrative**
- Frame: Mixed angles, story progression
- Format: Setup → Problem → Solution → Result
- Duration: 30-60s
- Tone: Emotional, engaging
- Goal: Deep watch time, emotional connection
- VN example: "Cái áo này thay đổi cuộc sống em"

**Behind the Scenes**
- Frame: Casual, sometimes shaky camera (authentic)
- Content: Day in the life, workspace, team moments
- Duration: 15-30s
- Tone: Fun, transparent, relatable
- Algorithm: People love authentic, non-polished content
- VN example: "Ngày làm việc tại cửa hàng" (work day at shop)

### 1.3 Hook Formulas (Critical: 3 giây đầu)

**Hook Formula 1: Question**
- Structure: Ask engaging question
- Duration: 1-2 seconds
- Example: "Bạn có biết cách chọn điện thoại cũ không?" (Do you know how to pick a used phone?)
- Impact: User wants to see answer

**Hook Formula 2: Shocking Claim**
- Structure: Start with surprising statement
- Duration: 1-2 seconds
- Example: "Áo này có thể mặc 100 lần mà vẫn đẹp" (This shirt lasts 100 wears)
- Impact: User curious about truth

**Hook Formula 3: Statistics/Number**
- Structure: Share impressive data
- Duration: 1-2 seconds
- Example: "10,000 khách hàng yêu thích sản phẩm này" (10k customers love this)
- Impact: Authority, social proof

**Hook Formula 4: Trend/Sound**
- Structure: Use viral sound, surprising visual match
- Duration: Match sound transition
- Example: Sound plays → Product appears on beat
- Impact: Algorithm favors trending sounds, natural sharing

**Hook Formula 5: Pattern Interrupt**
- Structure: Unexpected visual/color/movement
- Duration: 0.5-1 second
- Example: Bright color flash, quick scene change, unexpected action
- Impact: Stop scrolling, catch attention

**Hook Formula 6: Relatability**
- Structure: "Me when..." scenario
- Duration: 1-2 seconds
- Example: "Me when I find an item in my perfect size 🎉"
- Impact: Instant emotional connection

**Hook Formula 7: Promise of Benefit**
- Structure: "Learn how to..." or "I'll show you..."
- Duration: 1-2 seconds
- Example: "I'll show you 5 ways to style this shirt"
- Impact: Curiosity, watch time

### 1.4 Script Templates

```

[10-13s] CALL-TO-ACTION: Price, where to buy, link

```

"Tôi mua áo này từ 2 tháng trước và tôi yêu nó"

"Chất vải mềm, không bai, fit vừa vặn, giá rất tốt"

[15-25s] FINAL REACTION: Honest opinion, rate, recommend
"Mình đánh giá 10/10, khuyên mọi người nên mua"

[25-30s] CTA: Link, code, where to buy
"Buy now, link in bio, code SAVE10 for 10% off"

**45-second Tutorial**

```

"Nhiều bạn hỏi cách chọn size áo phù hợp, hôm nay mình hướng dẫn"

"Bước 1: Đo chiều dài từ vai xuống" (with visual guide)

[12-25s] STEP 2: Show width measurement
"Bước 2: Đo chiều rộng từ vai sang vai"

[25-35s] STEP 3: Compare to chart
"Bước 3: So sánh với bảng size của shop" (show chart)

"Kết quả: Áo fit vừa vặn như vậy là đúng size"

[42-45s] CTA: Shop link, size guide
"Xem bảng size đầy đủ ở link bio"
```

**60-second Story/Transformation**

```
[0-5s] HOOK + SETUP: Introduce problem
"Cô gái này không có áo để mặc đi phỏng vấn việc"

[5-15s] PROBLEM: Show situation
"Có rất nhiều áo nhưng không cái nào hợp lắm"

[15-35s] SOLUTION: Show discovery
"Rồi cô ấy tìm được áo này trong cửa hàng chúng tôi"

[35-50s] TRANSFORMATION: Show result
"Cô gái từng tự tin, đẹp, và cuối cùng cô ấy được nhận việc"

[50-55s] EMOTION: Reaction, smile, success
Show happy customer

[55-60s] CTA: Inspire action
"Tìm áo của bạn hôm nay, link bio"
```

### 1.5 Posting Schedule tối ưu VN

**Research findings:**
- Vietnam peak time: 12-13h (lunch break), 18-20h (evening), 21-23h (late night)
- Weekdays vs weekends: Similar engagement
- Best days: Friday-Sunday slightly higher
- Algorithm freshness: Post when followers most active

**Optimal VN schedule:**

| Time | Audience | Post | Frequency |
|------|----------|------|-----------|
| 12-13h | Office workers, lunch break | 1 video | Daily |
| 18-20h | After work, dinner prep | 1 video | Daily |
| 21-23h | Pre-sleep scrolling | 1 video | Daily |
| 9-10h | Morning commute | 1-2 videos | 5x/week |
| 14-15h | Afternoon break | 0-1 video | 3x/week |

**Consistency rule:**
- Daily: 3-5 videos per day (optimal)
- Weekly: 15-21 videos per week minimum
- Never: Post same content to followers (boring)
- Fresh content daily essential for growth

**Strategic timing:**
- Tuesday: Test new creative
- Wednesday: Analyze performance
- Thursday: Scale what works
- Friday-Saturday: Boost timeless content
- Sunday: Community engagement, replies

**How to find trending sounds:**

**VN-popular sound categories:**

- Vietnamese rap/hip-hop
- Feel-good pop music
- Viral sound effects
- Comedy dialogues

**Sound strategy:**
- Use trending sounds in first 1-2 weeks

- Keep post: Evergreen sounds (don't age out)

**Licensing tip:**

### 1.8 Duet & Stitch Strategy

**Duets** (Side-by-side response video)
- Use when: Responding to customer question, trend
- Length: 15-60s per side
- Performance: Duets get 30-50% more reach
- VN example: Customer asks "How to style this?" → You duet with styling video
- Process:
  1. Find video to duet
  2. Record your response side-by-side
  3. Post with credit to original

**Stitches** (Clip from another video + your commentary)
- Use when: Reacting to feedback, news, trend
- Clip length: Up to 5 seconds
- Your addition: 15+ seconds
- Performance: Stitches get 20-30% more reach
- VN example: Customer review clip → You stitch and respond
- Process:
  1. Find video
  2. Clip relevant 5s section
  3. Record your response (15s+)
  4. Post with credit

**Growth benefit:**
- Duets/stitches visible on original video
- Original creator's followers see you
- Cross-pollination = more followers
- Engagement signals boost algorithm

### 1.9 Engagement Tactics

**Caption strategy:**
- Ask questions: "Bạn thích color nào?" (Which color?)
- Create urgency: "Comment để nhận discount"
- Encourage action: "Tag bạn bè của bạn"
- Use emojis: 3-5 relevant emojis
- Length: 100-300 characters optimal

**Comment engagement:**
- Reply to every comment first 1 hour
- Response time: <15 minutes ideal
- Tone: Friendly, thank them, answer fully
- Pin best comments: Boost engagement
- Create threads: Encourage replies to replies

**Call-to-action (CTA):**
- Explicit: "Follow for more", "Share with friends"
- Implicit: "Guess the price", "Tell me your favorite"
- Video end CTA: Show text overlay with action
- Bio CTA: Link to shop, link guide

## 2. TIKTOK SHOP SETUP & STRATEGY

### 2.1 TikTok Shop Setup Checklist

**Prerequisites:**
- [ ] TikTok account (min 5000 followers recommended for Shop)
- [ ] Business registration (Doanh nghiệp or Individual VN)
- [ ] Tax ID (Mã số thuế)
- [ ] Bank account (for payouts)
- [ ] Product inventory ready

**Setup steps:**

Step 1: Enable Shop
- Profile → Creator Marketplace → TikTok Shop
- Or request access (usually instant if 5k+ followers)
- Authorize TikTok Shop access

Step 2: Complete verification
- Business information: Name, ID, tax code
- Bank account: For fund transfers
- Address: Warehouse/office address

Step 3: Setup shipping
- Shipping partners: Choose ViettelPost, GHN, GHTK
- Rates: Set per zone (HCM, Hanoi, other)
- Estimated delivery time: 1-2 days HCM, 2-3 days others

Step 4: Payment integration
- Payment method: Bank, e-wallet
- Payout schedule: Daily or weekly

Step 5: Product upload
- Create products (manually or bulk)
- Add images (5+), description, price, SKU
- Categories: Assign proper categories

### 2.2 Product Listing Optimization

**Product title best practices:**
- Format: [Brand] [Category] [Key attribute] [Size/Color]
- Length: 30-60 characters (full display)
- Include trending keyword
- Avoid repetition, all caps
- Examples:
  - "ZARA White Cotton Shirt - Size M" (Good)
  - "SHIRT SHIRT SHIRT WHITE ZARA" (Bad)

**Product description:**
- Paragraph 1: Brand + product essence
- Paragraph 2: Key features, benefits
- Paragraph 3: Specifications (size, material, care)
- Paragraph 4: Warranty, shipping info
- Include emojis: 3-5 relevant emojis
- Length: 200-500 characters (more = better indexing)

**Product images strategy:**
- Image 1: Main product (white background, centered)
- Image 2: Lifestyle/wearing
- Image 3: Detail/texture
- Image 4: Size comparison or model
- Image 5: Features/benefits highlight
- Specs: 800x800px minimum, 1200x1200px ideal
- Avoid: Watermarks, logos, busy backgrounds

**Pricing strategy:**
- Base price: Retail price - 10-20% (make profitable)
- Seasonal: Increase during peak (Tet, 11/11)
- Flash sale: 30-40% discount for promotions
- Bundle: Offer 2-3 products together at discount
- Tiered: If buying 3+, discount increases

**Categories & tags:**
- Select correct category (affects discoverability)
- Add tags: Color, size, material, brand, style
- Use VN keywords: Men's, Women's, Unisex, Kids
- Example tags: "Áo nam", "Cotton", "Casual", "Black"

### 2.3 Affiliate Program Strategy

**Purpose:** Leverage TikTok creators to promote your products

**Setup:**
- TikTok Shop → Commission Manager
- Set commission rate: 5-20% per sale
- Higher commission = more creators interested
- VN benchmark: 10-15% is standard

**Recruitment:**
- Invite nano-influencers (1-10K followers)
- Commission-based (don't pay upfront)
- They use affiliate link to earn commission
- Low barrier to entry, high volume potential

**Performance metrics:**
- Track: Clicks, conversions, revenue from each affiliate
- Top affiliates: Consider offering bonuses
- Poor performers: Deactivate after 2 weeks no sales
- Monthly payouts: Creators paid via TikTok Wallet

**Affiliate best practices:**
- Vet creators: Check content quality, audience fit
- Provide brief: Show product, key selling points
- Authentic promotion: Creators promote naturally
- Commission timing: Pay within 7-14 days of sale

### 2.5 Flash Sale Strategy

**Best times for flash sales:**
- Peak hours: 12-13h, 18-20h, 21-23h
- Weekends: Friday night, Saturday evening
- Seasonal: Tet (end Jan/Feb), 11/11, 12/12, Mid-Autumn

**Discount strategy:**
- Standard sale: 15-25% off (test profitability)
- Flash sale: 30-50% off (limited quantity/time)
- Bundle sale: Buy 2 get discount, buy 3 get bigger
- Time-limited: Flash sale 30-60 min only

**Volume impact:**
- 20% discount: 1.5x volume increase expected
- 30% discount: 2-3x volume increase
- 50% discount: 4-5x volume increase
- Profitability: Monitor margin, don't go too low

**Promotion tactics:**
- Announce: Before sale starts, build anticipation
- Scarcity: "Only 100 items at this price"
- Countdown: "Sale ends in 10 minutes!"
- Social proof: "200 people buying right now!"
- Urgency: "Limited stock, grab yours!"

## 3. TIKTOK ADS STRATEGY

### 3.1 Campaign Objectives

**Objective 1: Traffic**
- Goal: Drive users to website or TikTok Shop
- Optimization: Maximize clicks
- Budget: 2-5M VND/day
- Best for: Product discovery, new launch
- Expected metrics:
  - CPC: 2-5k VND
  - CTR: 1.5-3%
  - ROAS: 1.5-2.5x

**Objective 2: Conversion**
- Goal: Drive purchases in external shop
- Optimization: Maximize conversions
- Budget: 5-15M VND/day
- Requires: Pixel installed, purchase event tracking
- Expected metrics:
  - CPA: 50-150k VND
  - Conversion rate: 1-3%
  - ROAS: 2-4x

**Objective 3: Product Sales**
- Goal: Sell directly through TikTok Shop
- Optimization: Maximize sales value
- Budget: 5-15M VND/day
- Requirement: TikTok Shop setup
- Expected metrics:
  - CPA: 30-100k VND (lower since no landing page friction)
  - ROAS: 2.5-5x (highest)
  - AOV: Usually 200-500k VND

### 3.2 Ad Formats

**In-Feed Ads**
- Format: Regular TikTok video in user feed
- Duration: 9-30 seconds (9-15s optimal)
- CTA: Clickable (Link, Shop button, Download)
- Performance: Highest CTR, native feel
- Cost: Medium CPM (6-15k VND)
- Best for: Product showcase, traffic, conversions

**TopView Ads**
- Format: Plays at top of FYP when app opens
- Duration: Up to 60s
- First ad user sees = premium placement
- Performance: Highest impression cost, captive audience
- Cost: High CPM (20-50k VND) - Premium
- Best for: Major launches, budget available, brand awareness

**Branded Hashtag Challenges**
- Format: Campaign where users participate
- Participation: Create video with branded hashtag
- Reward: Can offer prizes, incentives
- Duration: 5-10 day campaigns
- Performance: High engagement, user-generated content
- Cost: 50k - 500k VND per campaign (varies)
- Best for: Viral campaigns, community building, awareness

**Spark Ads**
- Format: Promote existing organic TikTok video (boosted post)
- CTA: Same as original (Link, Shop, etc.)
- Performance: Most authentic feel, leverages organic engagement
- Cost: Lower CPM than In-Feed ads (3-10k VND)
- Best for: Leverage viral videos, cost-efficient scaling

### 3.3 Audience Targeting for TikTok VN

**Demographic targeting:**
- Age: 16-40 (primary), expand to 40-60 if product fit
- Gender: Flexible or specific (women for fashion, both for electronics)
- Location: Vietnam (all or specific cities: HCM, Hanoi, Da Nang)

**Interest targeting:**
- In-app: Select interests during campaign setup
- VN-relevant interests:
  - Fashion & apparel
  - Beauty & makeup
  - Electronics & gadgets
  - Home & living
  - Food & dining
  - Sports & fitness

**Audience segments:**
- New audiences: Broad targeting, let TikTok learn
- Existing audiences: Retarget website visitors, app users
- Similar audiences: TikTok's lookalike, finds similar users

**Placement:**
- Newsfeed (organic feed) - Primary, highest reach
- FYP (for you page) - Recommended, good performance
- Messenger ads - Secondary option
- Search ads - Ad appears in search results

### 3.4 Creative Best Practices for TikTok Ads

**Hook (0-3 seconds) - CRITICAL**
- Use trending sounds immediately
- Visual motion/color change
- Text overlay: Question or claim
- Human face recommended
- Avoid: 3-second static intro

**Body (3-20 seconds)**
- Show product in context
- Demonstrate benefit
- Use quick cuts/transitions
- Captions overlay explaining
- Keep pace quick (TikTok audience fast-paced)
- Include emotional moment or humor

**CTA (20-30 seconds)**

- Sense of urgency: "Limited time", "Only few left"

**Audio strategy:**
- Trending sounds: Use sounds from top videos this week
- Music that fits: Upbeat for fashion, dramatic for tech

**Visual requirements:**
- Vertical video (9:16): Optimized for mobile
- Resolution: 1080x1920px minimum
- Duration: 9-15s optimal (up to 30s allowed, but retention drops)
- Lighting: Bright, clear, professional but not overly polished

**VN-specific creative tips:**

## 4. KOL/KOC COLLABORATION STRATEGY

### 4.1 Creator Classification

**Nano Influencers (1K-10K followers)**
- Characteristics: Ultra-niche, highly engaged audience, authentic
- Followers: 1,000 - 10,000
- Engagement rate: 5-15% (very high)
- Cost per post: 500k - 3M VND
- Trust level: VERY HIGH (audience knows them personally)
- Best for: Community building, niche products, authenticity
- Example: A fashion blogger with 5K passionate followers in specific style niche

**Micro Influencers (10K-100K followers)**
- Followers: 10,000 - 100,000
- Engagement rate: 2-5%
- Cost per post: 2M - 10M VND
- Trust level: HIGH
- Best for: Product launches, wider reach, affordable
- Most cost-effective for TMĐT campaigns
- Example: Fashion vlogger with 50K followers

**Macro Influencers (100K-1M followers)**
- Followers: 100,000 - 1,000,000
- Engagement rate: 0.5-2%
- Cost per post: 10M - 50M VND
- Trust level: MEDIUM
- Best for: Mass awareness, brand building
- Higher cost, lower engagement %
- Example: Celebrity with 500K followers

**Mega Influencers (1M+ followers)**
- Followers: 1,000,000+
- Engagement rate: 0.1-0.5% (low %age but huge volume)
- Cost per post: 50M - 500M+ VND
- Trust level: LOW (influencer often distant from followers)
- Best for: Major brand launches, publicity
- Very expensive, accessibility limited
- Example: Famous actress with 5M followers

### 4.2 Creator Selection & Vetting

**Selection criteria:**

| Criteria | Weight | How to evaluate |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Audience fit** | 40% | Are their followers your target customer? |
| **Engagement rate** | 25% | Higher = more valuable (5%+ is good) |
| **Authenticity** | 20% | Does their content feel genuine? |
| **Content quality** | 10% | Professional or acceptable quality? |
| **Brand safety** | 5% | Any controversial past posts? |

**Evaluation process:**
1. Check 10 recent posts: Engagement, comments, shares
2. Analyze audience: Demographics, location, interests (if visible)
3. Review past partnerships: What brands worked with them
4. Fraud check: Use tools like HypeAudience or check for fake followers
5. Reach out: DM pitch, see responsiveness and professionalism

**Red flags to avoid:**
- High follower count, low engagement (likely fake followers)
- Posts not in native language (poor audience targeting)
- Excessive sponsored content (audience tunes out)
- No engagement from followers (zombie followers)
- Controversial past posts (brand safety risk)

## 6. TIKTOK ANALYTICS & METRICS

### 6.1 Key Performance Indicators

**Content metrics:**

| Metric | Meaning | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Views** | How many people saw video | 10K+ per post |
| **Watch time (AVG)** | Average watch duration | 50%+ of video length |
| **Completion rate** | % people watch to end | 40%+ |
| **Shares** | How many times shared | 2-5% of views |
| **Comments** | Number of comments | 1-3% of views |
| **Likes** | Number of likes | 5-10% of views |

**Engagement rate calculation:**
```
Engagement rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares) / Views * 100

Example:
(500 likes + 50 comments + 20 shares) / 10,000 views * 100 = 5.7%
```

**Healthy ranges:**
- Under 1%: Low engagement, adjust content
- 1-3%: Average, room to improve
- 3-5%: Good, consistent growth
- 5-10%: Excellent, viral potential
- 10%+: Outstanding, leverage for brand deals

### 6.2 Shop-specific metrics

**For TikTok Shop only:**

| Metric | Meaning | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Shop visits** | People clicked into shop | Track weekly trend |
| **Favorites** | Users saved products | 5-10% of visits |
| **Conversion rate** | % visitors who bought | 1-3% |
| **Add to cart** | Products added to cart | 10-20% of visits |
| **GMV** | Gross merchandise value (sales) | Depends on budget |
| **AOV** | Average order value | 200K-500K VND |

### 6.4 Optimization based on Analytics

**Weekly analysis:**
- Top 3 videos: Analyze what worked (hook, format, sound, topic)
- Bottom 3 videos: What didn't work?
- Engagement rate: Trending up or down?
- Follower growth: Rate consistent?

**Monthly strategy adjustment:**
- Double down on: Content formats/topics with highest engagement
- Stop: Content getting <1% engagement
- Experiment: 1-2 new content pillars monthly
- Audience shift: Growing younger? Older? Different geography?
