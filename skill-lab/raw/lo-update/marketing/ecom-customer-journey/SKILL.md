# CUSTOMER JOURNEY MAP CHO TMĐT VIỆT NAM

## 1. OVERVIEW: 5 GIAI ĐOẠN CUSTOMER JOURNEY

Khách hàng trải qua 5 giai đoạn chính từ lúc không biết tới brand, đến lúc trở thành
người ủng hộ trung thành:

```
AWARENESS → CONSIDERATION → PURCHASE → RETENTION → ADVOCACY

(Nhận biết)  (Cân nhắc)      (Mua)      (Giữ chân)  (Quảng bá)
```

### 1.1 Giai Đoạn 1: AWARENESS (Nhận Biết)

**Định nghĩa**: Khách hàng chưa biết tới brand/sản phẩm của bạn. Nhiệm vụ là làm họ biết đến.

**Customer mindset**: "Mình không biết cái này tồn tại"

**Mục tiêu**:
- Tăng brand awareness
- Tiếp cận target audience
- Xây dựng initial impression

**Duration**: 1 ngày - vài tuần (tùy thuộc vào customer search behavior)

### 1.2 Giai Đoạn 2: CONSIDERATION (Cân Nhắc)

**Định nghĩa**: Khách hàng đã biết sản phẩm, đang quyết định có nên mua hay không.

**Customer mindset**: "Cái này có tốt không? Có đáng mua không? So sánh với cái khác"

**Mục tiêu**:
- Cung cấp thông tin sản phẩm
- Xây dựng trust (đánh giá, review, proof)
- Giúp so sánh với competitor

**Duration**: 3-14 ngày (nhiều người dành thời gian research)

### 1.3 Giai Đoạn 3: PURCHASE (Mua)

**Định nghĩa**: Khách hàng quyết định mua. Điểm tới hạn - convert to customer.

**Customer mindset**: "Mình sẽ mua cái này"

**Mục tiêu**:
- Tối ưu hóa conversion rate
- Giảm cart abandonment
- Làm mua hàng dễ dàng

**Duration**: Vài phút - vài giờ (quyết định nhanh)

### 1.4 Giai Đoạn 4: RETENTION (Giữ Chân)

**Định nghĩa**: Khách hàng đã mua. Nhiệm vụ là làm họ thỏa mãn, muốn mua lại.

**Customer mindset**: "Sản phẩm có tốt không? Có mua lại được không?"

**Mục tiêu**:
- Tăng customer satisfaction
- Khuyến khích mua lại (repeat purchase)
- Tăng customer lifetime value (CLV)

**Duration**: Dài nhất (từ lúc mua đến khi lãng quên hoặc chuyển sang competitor)

### 1.5 Giai Đoạn 5: ADVOCACY (Ủng Hộ)

**Định nghĩa**: Khách hàng rất hài lòng, tự động giới thiệu brand cho người khác.

**Customer mindset**: "Sản phẩm này tuyệt vời! Mình phải kể cho bạn bè biết"

**Mục tiêu**:
- Khuyến khích review & rating
- Yêu cầu referral
- Xây dựng community

**Duration**: Lâu dài (trở thành brand advocate)

## 2. TOUCHPOINT MAPPING (Lập Bản Đồ Điểm Tiếp Xúc)

### 2.1 AWARENESS Stage Touchpoints

**Touchpoint 1: Social Media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)**
- Format: Organic posts, reels, stories, ads
- Content: Product showcase, behind-the-scenes, lifestyle
- Goal: Reach as many people as possible
- Metrics: Impressions, reach, engagement rate

**Touchpoint 2: Search Engines (Google)**
- Type: Organic search (SEO), paid search (Google Ads)
- What they search: "[Product category]", "[Problem] solution", "[Brand name]"
- Your goal: Appear in search results
- Metrics: Click-through rate, search volume

**Touchpoint 3: Word of Mouth**
- Who: Existing customers, friends, family
- What they say: "Mình vừa mua từ cửa hàng này, tốt lắm"
- Your role: Make product so good people talk about it
- Metrics: Referral rate, NPS (Net Promoter Score)

**Touchpoint 4: KOL / Influencer**
- Who: Content creators, celebrities, micro-influencers
- What they do: Review, feature product, create buzz
- Your partnership: Provide free product for review
- Metrics: Reach, engagement, mentions

**Touchpoint 5: Ads (Facebook, Google, TikTok)**
- Format: Video ads, image ads, carousel ads
- Placement: In-feed, stories, search results
- Goal: Targeted reach to relevant audience
- Metrics: Impressions, cost per impression, clicks

**Touchpoint 6: Viral Content / UGC**
- What: Customer posts about product (genuine, not paid)
- Format: TikTok videos, Instagram reels, Facebook posts
- Your role: Encourage, re-share, amplify
- Metrics: Shares, comments, saves

**Touchpoint 7: PR / News Coverage**
- Type: Articles, blog posts, news coverage
- Goal: Credibility, third-party endorsement
- Metrics: Media mentions, backlinks

---

### 2.2 CONSIDERATION Stage Touchpoints

**Touchpoint 1: Website Visit**
- Where: Homepage, product category pages
- What customer does: Browse, explore, read about brand
- Your goal: Provide clear, compelling information
- Metrics: Bounce rate, time on page, pages per session

**Touchpoint 2: Product Page**
- Key elements:
  - Product images (multiple angles, high quality)
  - Product description (benefits, specs)
  - Price & availability
  - Reviews & ratings (social proof)
  - Related/recommended products
  - Call-to-action (Add to cart)

**Touchpoint 3: Customer Reviews & Ratings**
- 93% of Vietnamese consumers read reviews before buying
- Types: Star ratings, detailed written reviews, photos/videos from real customers
- Your role: Encourage customers to review, respond to feedback
- Metrics: Average rating, review volume, review sentiment

**Touchpoint 4: Comparison Shopping**
- Customer compares your product vs. competitor
- Touchpoint locations: Your website, competitor sites, comparison tools
- Your strategy: Highlight unique benefits, competitive advantages
- Messaging: "Why choose us?" section

**Touchpoint 5: Live Chat / Customer Service**
- Customer has questions: "Có kích thước khác không?", "Giao hàng mất bao lâu?"
- Response time: Fast (ideally <2 minutes) = higher conversion
- Your goal: Answer questions, build trust
- Metrics: Chat response time, satisfaction rating

**Touchpoint 6: Email**
- Type: Abandoned cart emails, product recommendation emails
- Goal: Re-engage, provide additional info, create urgency
- Content: Product benefits, reviews from others, limited-time offer
- Metrics: Open rate, click rate, conversion rate

**Touchpoint 7: Video Content**
- Type: Product demo videos, tutorial, unboxing videos
- Length: 30 seconds to 2 minutes
- Goal: Show product in action, build confidence
- Metrics: Views, watch time, comments

**Touchpoint 8: FAQ / Knowledge Base**
- Type: Frequently asked questions, how-to guides
- Common Q's: Shipping times, return policy, size guides
- Your goal: Remove objections, answer concerns
- Metrics: Page views, bounce rate

---

### 2.3 PURCHASE Stage Touchpoints

**Touchpoint 1: Cart Page**
- What's visible: Items in cart, quantities, total price
- Pain points: Unexpected shipping cost, complex process
- Your optimization: Show shipping cost early, offer promo code, save cart for later
- Metrics: Cart abandonment rate (should be <70%)

**Touchpoint 2: Checkout Process**
- Steps:
  1. Shipping address
  2. Shipping method selection
  3. Payment method
  4. Order review
  5. Confirm order
- Pain points: Too many steps, forced registration, unclear costs
- Optimization: Minimize steps (3-4 max), guest checkout option, progress indicator
- Metrics: Checkout abandonment rate (track which step people drop off)

**Touchpoint 3: Payment Gateway**
- Options: Credit card, debit card, e-wallet (Zalopay, Momo), bank transfer, COD
- Pain point: Limited payment options = lost sales
- Optimization: Offer multiple options, clearly show accepted methods
- Metrics: Payment failure rate, successful transactions

**Touchpoint 4: Order Confirmation Email**
- What to include:
  - Order number (for reference)
  - Ordered items & quantities
  - Total amount paid
  - Shipping address
  - Expected delivery date
  - Tracking link (when available)
  - Return policy
  - Customer service contact
- Goal: Reassure customer, set expectations
- Tone: Professional but friendly

**Touchpoint 5: SMS Confirmation**
- Send SMS with: Order #, estimated delivery, track link
- When: Immediately after purchase
- Goal: Quick confirmation, accessible info (SMS easier than email to access)

**Touchpoint 6: Post-Purchase Landing Page**
- What: "Order confirmed" page after checkout completion
- Include: Order details + cross-sell offer ("Wait, add this for $X")
- Goal: Last-minute upsell
- Metrics: % who click cross-sell, cross-sell revenue

---

### 2.4 RETENTION Stage Touchpoints

**Touchpoint 1: Shipping & Delivery**
- Tracking updates: SMS/email updates as package moves
- Timing: "Shipped", "In transit", "Out for delivery", "Delivered"
- Goal: Keep customer informed, reduce anxiety
- Metrics: Delivery time, on-time delivery rate

**Touchpoint 2: Unboxing Experience**
- What matters: Packaging quality, inclusion of extras (freebies, notes)
- Goal: Create Instagram-worthy moment → UGC
- Tactic: Include thank-you card, small freebie, QR code for review
- Metrics: Unboxing posts on social media

**Touchpoint 3: Product Usage Support**
- How-to videos, guides, tutorials
- FAQs about usage, care, maintenance
- Goal: Help customer get maximum value from product
- Metrics: Video views, guide downloads

**Touchpoint 4: Customer Service / Support**
- Channels: Email, chat, phone
- Response time: <24 hours for non-urgent, <2 hours for urgent
- Common issues: Defective product, need for refund, advice
- Goal: Resolve issues quickly, maintain satisfaction
- Metrics: Support response time, satisfaction rating, resolution rate

**Touchpoint 5: Product Review Request Email**
- When: 3-7 days after delivery (customer has used product)
- Message: "How did you like your purchase? Share your thoughts"
- Incentive: "Leave a review, get 10% off next order"
- Goal: Collect reviews → build social proof
- Metrics: Review collection rate, sentiment

**Touchpoint 6: Loyalty Program**
- Structure: Points per purchase → Redeem for discount/free item
- Benefit: 1% cashback, 2x points on birthdays, VIP early access
- Goal: Encourage repeat purchases, increase CLV
- Metrics: Loyalty program enrollment, repeat purchase rate

**Touchpoint 7: Email Marketing**
- Frequency: 1-2 emails per week (not overwhelming)
- Content: Product tips, new arrivals, personalized recommendations
- Goal: Keep top of mind, encourage repeat purchase
- Metrics: Email open rate, click rate, revenue per email

**Touchpoint 8: SMS Marketing**
- Frequency: 1-2 SMS per month (be respectful)
- Content: Flash sale alerts, exclusive member discounts
- Goal: Drive urgent action (flash sales are time-sensitive)
- Metrics: SMS open rate, click rate, conversion

**Touchpoint 9: Community / Social Groups**
- What: Facebook group, Discord, Line group for brand community
- Content: Tips, product discussions, member-only sales, events
- Goal: Build community, increase engagement, retention
- Metrics: Group membership, engagement rate, repeat purchase rate

---

### 2.5 ADVOCACY Stage Touchpoints

**Touchpoint 1: Review & Rating Platform**
- Where: Your website product page, Google Reviews, Shopee, Lazada, Facebook
- Goal: Customer leaves positive review (ideally with photo/video)
- Incentive: Discount code for next purchase
- Metrics: Review count, average rating, review sentiment

**Touchpoint 2: Referral Program**
- Structure: "Refer a friend, both get $10 off"
- How: Customer gets unique referral link, shares with friends
- Benefit: New customer acquisition, customer retention
- Metrics: Referral links clicked, new customers from referrals, referral conversion rate

**Touchpoint 3: UGC (User Generated Content)**
- What: Customer posts about product on social media (organic)
- Tactic: Repost their content, tag them, give them shout-out
- Goal: More exposure, create buzz, build trust
- Metrics: UGC posts featuring your product, reach of UGC content

**Touchpoint 4: Brand Ambassador Program**
- Who: Highly satisfied customers, micro-influencers
- What they do: Regularly feature product, provide feedback, represent brand
- Benefit: Free products, commission on sales, exclusive perks
- Metrics: Ambassador-driven sales, engagement rate

**Touchpoint 5: Testimonial / Case Study**
- Format: Written quote, video testimonial, before-after photos
- Goal: Use real customer stories to convince new customers
- Incentive: $50-$100 gift card for participation
- Metrics: Testimonials collected, usage on marketing materials

**Touchpoint 6: Event / Meetup**
- Type: Product launch, customer appreciation event, workshop
- Goal: Strengthen community, deepen customer relationship
- Metrics: Event attendance, social media mentions, post-event sales

**Touchpoint 7: Exclusive Access / VIP Benefits**
- Perks: Early access to new products, VIP-only sales, special pricing
- Goal: Make top customers feel special
- Metrics: VIP program participation, VIP repeat purchase rate

## 3. CUSTOMER PERSONA TEMPLATES (MẪU NGƯỜI MUA)

### 3.1 Persona 1: Chị Em Mua Sắm Online (Online Shopping Mom)

**Demographics**:
- Age: 25-40 years old
- Gender: Female
- Income: $500-1,500/month
- Education: High school to college
- Family status: Married with children

**Platforms Preferred**:
- Facebook: 4+ hours/day
- Shopee: Primary shopping platform
- Zalo: Communication
- Instagram: Browsing

**Behavior**:
- Shops on mobile (phone)
- Prefers convenience over premium
- Buys household items, kids products, fashion
- Highly influenced by friends' recommendations
- Reads Shopee reviews before buying (93% do)
- Uses vouchers/discount codes aggressively
- Watches TikTok while shopping

**Pain Points**:
- Limited time (busy with kids, work)
- Budget conscious
- Worried about product quality
- Concerned about shipping times
- Needs reliable customer service

**Buying Triggers**:
- Friend recommendation: "Chị mua cái này ở Shopee, tốt lắm"
- Seasonal sales (11.11, 12.12)
- Flash deals & vouchers
- Free shipping
- High reviews (4.8+ stars)

**Content that Resonates**:
- "Mom hacks" tips
- Product reviews from real users
- Before-after transformations
- Affordable + quality messaging
- Social proof (5,000+ reviews)

**Communication**:
- Language: Casual, friendly, use emojis
- Channel: Facebook, Zalo, SMS
- Frequency: Daily is okay (they're active)
- Tone: Friend-like, not corporate

**Purchase Journey Timeline**:
- Awareness: Friend recommendation (fastest)
- Consideration: Read 10-20 reviews
- Purchase: Make decision same day
- Retention: Repeat buy in 3-6 months
- Advocacy: Tell friends, leave review if very satisfied

---

### 3.2 Persona 2: Gen Z Shopaholic (Thích Mua Sắm)

**Demographics**:
- Age: 18-25 years old
- Gender: Primarily female
- Income: $200-800/month (students or entry-level jobs)
- Education: High school to college
- Family status: Single, living with parents or roommates

**Platforms Preferred**:
- TikTok: #1 platform (4+ hours/day)
- Instagram: Reels, stories
- Shopee: Shopping
- Reddit/Twitter: Trends, discussions

**Behavior**:
- Shops on mobile exclusively
- Influenced by TikTok trends, viral products
- Buys fashion, cosmetics, trendy items
- FOMO-driven (fear of missing out)
- Follows trends obsessively
- Values aesthetic/looks over function
- Shares purchases on social media

**Pain Points**:
- Wants trending products FAST
- Limited budget (saving money matters)
- Worried about authenticity/counterfeits
- Needs instant gratification
- Values packaging (Instagram-worthy)

**Buying Triggers**:
- TikTok viral video: "POV: You see this product on TikTok"
- Limited edition products
- Cute packaging
- Flash sales (speed matters)
- Influencer endorsement
- Trending hashtag

**Content that Resonates**:
- Short-form videos (15-60 seconds)
- Trending audio/sounds
- Aesthetic unboxing videos
- Influencer partnerships
- Limited edition messaging
- "Only 50 left!" scarcity

**Communication**:
- Language: Casual, slang, lots of emojis, Gen Z speak
- Channel: TikTok, Instagram, Zalo
- Frequency: Daily content (expect engagement)
- Tone: Cool, trendy, relatable, humor

**Purchase Journey Timeline**:
- Awareness: TikTok algorithm (5 seconds)
- Consideration: Check comments, see other opinions (30 seconds)
- Purchase: Impulse buy (1-5 minutes decision)
- Retention: Unboxing, post on social (same day)
- Advocacy: Create content, challenge others

### 3.3 Persona 3: Ông Bố Bà Mẹ (Convenience-Focused Parent)

**Demographics**:
- Age: 35-50 years old
- Gender: Both male and female
- Income: $1,000-2,500/month
- Education: College educated
- Family status: Married with school-age or older children

**Platforms Preferred**:
- Facebook: Main platform
- Email: Prefer traditional communication

- WhatsApp: Communication

**Behavior**:
- Not heavy social media user
- Shops online for convenience (not entertainment)
- Buys practical items: home, electronics, health
- Values reliability and safety
- Less price-sensitive than younger cohorts
- Prefers established brands
- Reads detailed product specs
- Concerned about authenticity

**Pain Points**:
- Not tech-savvy, may need help
- Worried about scams/counterfeits
- Needs clear, simple information
- Concerned about child safety (if relevant)
- Wants trustworthy brands
- Values good warranty/support

**Buying Triggers**:
- Recommendation from trusted friend
- Established brand they know
- Good warranty/support
- Clear specifications
- Free returns (peace of mind)
- Safety certifications

**Content that Resonates**:
- Detailed product specifications
- Safety & quality assurance info
- Expert reviews (not just user reviews)
- Warranty information
- Professional testimonials
- Brand heritage/history

**Communication**:
- Language: Formal, clear, professional
- Channel: Email, phone, Facebook messenger
- Frequency: Not daily (respect their time)
- Tone: Professional, trustworthy, clear

**Purchase Journey Timeline**:
- Awareness: Friend recommendation or search
- Consideration: Read detailed specs, compare warranties (days/weeks)
- Purchase: Deliberate, informed decision
- Retention: After-sales service matters most
- Advocacy: Recommend to friends based on experience

## 4. PAIN POINTS & EMOTIONS AT EACH TOUCHPOINT

### 4.1 Awareness Stage

**Emotional State**: Curious but skeptical, unaware of brand

**Pain Points**:
- Too many options (information overload)
- Can't find what they're looking for
- Unclear value proposition (why should I care?)
- Doubts credibility

**Emotional Needs**:
- Clarity: "What is this?"
- Trust: "Can I trust this brand?"
- Relevance: "Is this for me?"

**How to Address**:
- Clear, concise messaging
- Show social proof early (reviews, follower count)
- Explain problem you solve
- Use relatable language

---

### 4.2 Consideration Stage

**Emotional State**: Evaluating, comparing, hesitant

**Pain Points**:
- Uncertainty about product quality
- Fear of making wrong choice
- Price seems high
- Don't know if it's best option
- Worried about scams/counterfeits

**Emotional Needs**:
- Confidence: "This is the right choice"
- Proof: "Real people use and love this"
- Security: "I won't regret this"
- Value: "It's worth the price"

**How to Address**:
- Abundant reviews and ratings
- Before/after photos from real customers
- Detailed product info
- FAQ addressing concerns
- Money-back guarantee
- Live chat for questions

---

### 4.3 Purchase Stage

**Emotional State**: Committed but anxious, final decision point

**Pain Points**:
- Checkout process is complex
- Unexpected costs (shipping)
- Payment failure
- Too many steps
- Can't use preferred payment method

**Emotional Needs**:
- Simplicity: "Make this easy"
- Clarity: "What's the total cost?"
- Security: "My payment is safe"
- Speed: "I'm ready to buy now"

**How to Address**:
- 3-4 step checkout max
- Show total price including shipping early
- Multiple payment options
- Progress indicator (Step 1 of 3)
- Trust badges (SSL, payment processor logos)
- Guest checkout option

---

### 4.4 Retention Stage

**Emotional State**: Post-purchase excitement then curiosity

**Pain Points**:
- "Did I make the right choice?"
- "When will it arrive?"
- "How do I use this?"
- "What if it doesn't work?"
- Forgotten after initial excitement

**Emotional Needs**:
- Reassurance: "You made a good choice"
- Support: "We're here to help"
- Value: "Get the most from your purchase"
- Connection: "We care about you"

**How to Address**:
- Order confirmation email within minutes
- Tracking updates (SMS/email)
- Post-delivery check-in
- How-to guides/videos
- Community/support resources
- Regular relevant email (not spammy)

---

### 4.5 Advocacy Stage

**Emotional State**: Satisfied, willing to promote, feel like part of community

**Pain Points**:
- Not asked for review/referral
- Don't know how to share
- No incentive to put effort in
- Fear of judgment if recommend

**Emotional Needs**:
- Recognition: "Your opinion matters"
- Community: "You're part of something"
- Reward: "We appreciate you"
- Empowerment: "Help others discover this"

**How to Address**:
- Easy review process (3 clicks)
- Share referral link easily
- Incentivize reviews (discount on next purchase)
- Feature customer stories
- Create brand community
- VIP treatment for top advocates

---

## 5. CONTENT STRATEGY BY STAGE

### 5.1 Awareness Stage Content

**Goal**: Reach target audience, introduce brand/problem

**Content Types**:

1. **Social Media Posts**
   - Instagram carousel: "5 reasons why [problem]"
   - TikTok video: Trending audio + product hook
   - Facebook post: Story about how you solved problem
   - Length: Short (15-60 seconds for video)

2. **Blog Posts**
   - Title: "7 Ways to [Solve Problem]" (SEO-optimized)
   - Format: How-to, listicle, comparison
   - Length: 1,500-2,500 words
   - Goal: Rank on Google → organic traffic

3. **Video Content**
   - YouTube channel: Problem/solution videos
   - Format: Educational, entertaining
   - Length: 5-10 minutes (YouTube), 15-60 seconds (social)

4. **Influencer Content**
   - Partner with micro-influencers (10k-100k followers)
   - Format: Authentic reviews, use-case showcases
   - Reach: Their audience → awareness

5. **Ads**
   - Facebook/Instagram ads: Carousel ads showing benefits
   - TikTok ads: Native-looking content (not obvious ad)
   - Google ads: Text ads for keyword searches

6. **PR / Media**
   - Press release about new product launch
   - Pitch to blogs/journalists in your niche
   - Goal: Earned media coverage

---

### 5.2 Consideration Stage Content

**Goal**: Build trust, educate, differentiate from competitors

**Content Types**:

1. **Product Pages**
   - Clear product description (benefits, not just specs)
   - Multiple high-quality images (8-10 angles)
   - Video demo (30-60 seconds showing product in action)
   - Technical specs (size, materials, etc.)
   - Use case examples
   - FAQ section

2. **Reviews & Testimonials**
   - Showcase 5-star reviews prominently
   - Encourage photo/video reviews (more credible)
   - Respond to negative reviews professionally
   - Create case study from happy customer

3. **Comparison Content**
   - "Our product vs. competitor X" comparison chart
   - Highlight unique selling points
   - Address common objections

4. **Educational Content**
   - "Buyer's guide: How to choose [product]"
   - Video tutorials: How to use product
   - Blog: Tips for getting maximum value
   - Webinar: Live Q&A with expert

5. **Email Content**
   - Abandoned cart email: "Did you find what you're looking for?"
   - Educational series: "5 benefits of [product]"
   - Case study: How customer X got result Y

6. **Live Chat / Customer Service**
   - Proactive engagement: "Do you have questions?"
   - Quick answers to common questions
   - Personalized product recommendations

7. **Video Testimonials**
   - Real customer talking about experience
   - Before/after results
   - Authentic (not scripted-sounding)

---

### 5.3 Purchase Stage Content

**Goal**: Remove final objections, encourage conversion

**Content Types**:

1. **Checkout Page**
   - Trust indicators (SSL badge, payment logos)
   - "Why buy from us" section
   - Money-back guarantee
   - Return policy summary
   - Shipping info (cost, time, free over $X)

2. **Exit-Intent Popup**
   - "Wait! Here's 10% off your first order"
   - Creates urgency (limited-time offer)
   - Works as last conversion attempt

3. **Limited-Time Offer**
   - "Flash sale ends in 24 hours"
   - Creates FOMO (fear of missing out)
   - Scarcity messaging ("Only 10 left in stock")

## 7. METRICS BY STAGE

### 7.1 Awareness Stage Metrics

**Traffic Metrics**:
- Website visits: Target 10,000+/month (depends on size)
- Impressions: # of times ad/content shown
- Reach: # of unique people who saw ad/post
- Click-through rate (CTR): # of clicks / impressions
  - Google ads CTR: 2-5%
  - Facebook ads CTR: 1-3%
  - Organic social CTR: 0.1-1%

**Social Media Metrics**:
- Followers: Growth rate
- Engagement rate: (likes + comments + shares) / followers
  - Good rate: 3-5%
- Impressions
- Reach

**SEO Metrics**:
- Keyword rankings: Where do you rank for target keywords?
  - Target: Page 1 of Google (top 10)
- Organic traffic: Visitors from search engines

**Cost Metrics**:
- Cost per impression (CPM)
- Cost per click (CPC): Cost of ad / Clicks
  - Target: $0.50-$2.00 depending on product

---

### 7.2 Consideration Stage Metrics

**Engagement Metrics**:
- Time on page: How long do people read?
  - Target: 2+ minutes (page is engaging)
- Bounce rate: % who leave after 1 page
  - Target: <50% (content is relevant)
- Pages per session: # of pages visited
  - Target: 3+ (exploring multiple items)

**Review Metrics**:
- Average rating: Aim for 4.5+/5.0
- # of reviews: More reviews = more social proof
- Review sentiment: Are they positive or negative?

**Conversion Metrics**:
- Add-to-cart rate: % who click add to cart
  - Target: 5-10%
- Wishlist/save rate: % who bookmark item

**Email Metrics**:
- Open rate: % who open email
  - Target: 20-30%
- Click rate: % who click link in email
  - Target: 2-5%
- Conversion rate (from email): % who buy after email
  - Target: 1-3%

---

### 7.3 Purchase Stage Metrics

**Conversion Metrics**:
- Conversion rate: # of purchases / visitors
  - E-commerce average: 1-2%
  - Target: 2-3%

- Cart abandonment rate: % who add to cart but don't buy
  - Average: 70-80%
  - Target: <70%

- Checkout completion rate: % who complete checkout
  - Target: 80%+ (low friction)

**Transaction Metrics**:
- AOV (Average Order Value): Total revenue / # orders
- Order value range: Is it concentrated or diverse?
- Orders per day: Traffic-to-order conversion

**Payment Metrics**:
- Payment failure rate: % whose payment fails
  - Target: <5%
- Payment methods used: Track which methods work best

---

### 7.4 Retention Stage Metrics

**Satisfaction Metrics**:
- Product rating: Post-purchase, 1-5 stars
  - Target: 4.5+/5.0
- NPS (Net Promoter Score): "Would you recommend?"
  - Formula: (Promoters - Detractors) / Total × 100
  - Target: 50+

**Email Metrics**:
- Email open rate: % who open retention emails
  - Target: 20%+
- Unsubscribe rate: % who opt out
  - Target: <0.5%

**Repeat Purchase Metrics**:
- Repeat purchase rate: % who buy again
  - Target: 15-30% (depending on product)
- Time to repeat purchase: How long until 2nd buy?
  - Target: 30-90 days
- Repeat purchase frequency: Avg # purchases per customer per year

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**:
```
CLV = (Average purchase value) × (Repeat purchase frequency) × (Customer lifespan)

Example:
- Avg purchase: $50
- Repeat frequency: 2x per year
- Lifespan: 5 years
- CLV: $50 × 2 × 5 = $500

How to improve CLV:
1. Increase average purchase value (upsell/cross-sell)
2. Increase repeat frequency (retention marketing)
3. Extend lifespan (build loyalty, reduce churn)
```

**Retention Cohort Analysis**:
```
Track: Of customers who bought in January, how many bought again in Feb, Mar, Apr?

Cohort: Jan 2024
Jan: 100% (1,000 customers)
Feb: 25% bought again (250)
Mar: 18% (180)
Apr: 12% (120)
May: 8% (80)

This tells you: Retention is declining → Need better retention strategy
```

---

### 7.5 Advocacy Stage Metrics

**Review Metrics**:
- Review collection rate: % who leave review
  - Target: 10-20% (of customers)
- Review star rating: 4.5+/5.0
- Review volume: # of reviews (more = better social proof)

**Referral Metrics**:
- Referral rate: % who refer a friend
  - Target: 5-10%
- Referred customer conversion rate: % of referred who buy
  - Usually higher than cold traffic (30-50%)
- Cost per acquisition via referral: Often lowest cost channel

**UGC Metrics**:
- UGC volume: # of customer posts with brand hashtag/mention
- UGC engagement: Avg engagement per UGC post
- UGC conversion: % of viewers who buy after seeing UGC

**Community Metrics**:
- Community size: Members in brand community
- Engagement: Posts, comments, interactions per day
- Community sentiment: Are discussions positive?

Modern customers shop across multiple channels. Journey might look like:

```
Day 1: AWARENESS
- Sees TikTok video about product (discovery)
- Intrigued but not ready to buy

Day 2: CONSIDERATION
- Googles product name
- Reads reviews on website and Shopee
- Joins brand's Discord community (asks question)

Day 3: PURCHASE
- Still deciding between brands A & B
- Gets retargeting ad on Facebook
- Decides: Buys on Shopee (cheaper, more reviews)

Day 5: RETENTION

- Posts unboxing video on TikTok (organic UGC)

Day 7: RETENTION
- Leaves positive Shopee review with photo

Day 30: RETENTION
- Uses product regularly, loves it

Day 35: ADVOCACY
- Friend buys (referred by day 30 customer)

Timeline: 35 days from "never heard of brand" to "satisfied customer advocating"
```

## 10. CUSTOMER JOURNEY OPTIMIZATION FRAMEWORK

### Step 1: Identify Gaps

**Analyze each stage**:
- Where are customers dropping off?
- Awareness: Low traffic? Increase marketing budget
- Consideration: Bounce high? Improve website clarity
- Purchase: Cart abandonment? Reduce friction, add urgency
- Retention: Low repeat rate? Improve email, create loyalty program
- Advocacy: Low reviews? Make review process easier

**Ask critical questions**:
- What % of customers progress to each stage?
- Where do most customers drop off?
- Which touchpoint has lowest satisfaction?

### Step 2: Prioritize

**Focus on highest-impact improvements**:
- High volume + high impact: Biggest wins
- Example: If 70% abandon cart at shipping cost, fix that

**Quick wins first**:
- Low effort, high impact changes
- Examples: Better product photos, faster response time, clearer return policy

**Long-term improvements**:
- Takes time/resources but significant payoff
- Examples: Building community, brand positioning

### Step 3: Test

**A/B test improvements**:
- Original: Shipping cost shown after selecting items
- Variant: Show total cost (including shipping) before add to cart
- Measure: Does showing cost earlier reduce overall cart abandonment?

**Measure incrementally**:
- 1-2 changes at a time (know which change drove improvement)
- Run tests for 7-14 days minimum (statistical significance)

### Step 4: Implement

**Roll out winners**:
- If variant wins clearly (15%+ improvement), implement permanently
- Celebrate the win (improved metrics = better business)

**Document and scale**:
- Record what worked
- Scale to similar scenarios
- Share learnings across team

### Step 5: Measure Impact

**Calculate ROI**:
```
Example:
Old cart abandonment rate: 75%
New cart abandonment rate: 70% (5% improvement)
Average order value: $100
Daily visitors: 1,000

Daily impact:
Old: 1,000 × 25% conversion = 250 orders = $25,000 revenue
New: 1,000 × 30% conversion = 300 orders = $30,000 revenue
Incremental: +$5,000/day = +$150,000/month

ROI is huge!
```

**Metrics to track ongoing**:
- Customer journey completion rate (% progressing to each stage)
- Conversion rate at each stage
- Stage-to-stage drop-off rate

## CONCLUSION

A well-designed customer journey:
1. **Maps all touchpoints** - Know where customers interact with brand
2. **Understands personas** - Different people need different approaches
3. **Addresses pain points** - Remove friction at each stage
4. **Delivers right content** - Right message, right place, right time
5. **Uses automation** - Trigger-based actions scale personalization
6. **Measures constantly** - Data shows what's working, what needs fixing
7. **Optimizes iteratively** - Continuous small improvements compound
