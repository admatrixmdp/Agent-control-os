# CHIẾN LƯỢC REVIEW & USER GENERATED CONTENT (UGC)

## 1. TẠI SAO REVIEW & UGC QUAN TRỌNG TRONG TMĐT VIỆT NAM?

### 1.1 Con Số Chỉ Ra Sức Mạnh Của Review

**Thực tế về Việt Nam**:
- **93% người Việt đọc review trước khi mua hàng online**
- 73% người mua chỉ tin vào sản phẩm có 4.5+ sao
- 68% sẽ không mua nếu product rating dưới 4 sao
- Sản phẩm có ảnh/video review bán 30% nhiều hơn không có ảnh

**Psychological Impact**:
- Review = "chứng cứ" từ người khác (không phải marketing của shop)
- Social proof: "Nếu người khác thích, mình cũng sẽ thích"
- Trust builder: Sợ bị lừa, review giảm lo lắng

**Business Impact**:
- Conversion rate tăng 40% (với review tích cực)
- AOV tăng 10-20% (khách tin sản phẩm → mua thêm)
- Repeat purchase tăng 25% (hài lòng từ lần đầu)
- Word-of-mouth tăng (khách recommend)

### 1.2 Sự Khác Biệt: Review vs. UGC

**Review**: Phản hồi cấu trúc về sản phẩm
- Hình thức: Star rating (1-5 sao) + Text feedback (tuỳ chọn) + Photos/Videos
- Nơi: Product page, Shopee, Lazada, website
- Mục đích: Informative (giúp khách hàng khác decide)
- Formal: Có cấu trúc, moderators kiểm duyệt

**UGC (User Generated Content)**: Content organic từ customers
- Hình thức: Posts, videos, photos, stories (tự do)
- Nơi: Social media (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook)
- Mục đích: Entertaining/authentic (không marketing-y)
- Organic: Spontaneous, feels real (high trust)

**Quick comparison**:
```
REVIEW                          | UGC
- Structured, formal            | - Organic, casual
- On platform (Shopee, etc)    | - On social media
- Helps purchase decision       | - Builds desire/engagement
- Rating + text + photos       | - Any format
- Moderate to reach many       | - Viral potential
```

### 1.3 Impact Trên Revenue

**Without positive reviews**:
- Conversion rate: 1%
- 1,000 visitors → 10 orders → $1,000 revenue

**With 4.5+ star reviews & UGC**:
- Conversion rate: 2.5% (2.5x improvement)
- 1,000 visitors → 25 orders → $2,500 revenue
- Additional $1,500 revenue just from reviews!

**Cumulative effect over 1 year**:
- 30,000 visitors × $1,500 additional revenue = +$45,000
- If margin is 30%, that's +$13,500 additional profit

**ROI**: Investment to build review/UGC strategy pays for itself quickly

## 2. CHIẾN LƯỢC THU THẬP REVIEW

### 2.1 Post-Purchase Email Review Request (Most Effective)

**Timing**: 3-7 ngày sau khi khách nhận hàng

**Why this timing**:
- Too early (1-2 days): Customer hasn't used product yet
- Perfect (3-7 days): Used product, has opinion, still engaged
- Too late (14+ days): Forgotten, less engaged

**Email Template**:

```
Subject: "Hãy chia sẻ trải nghiệm của bạn với [product name]"
(or more casual: "Chị/anh thích sản phẩm mình mua không? 😊")

Hi [Customer Name],

Mình hy vọng bạn đã nhận được [product] và yêu thích nó!

Bây giờ bạn đã dùng thử, mình rất muốn biết:
- Sản phẩm có tốt không?
- Chất lượng như mong đợi không?
- Bạn có recommend cho bạn bè không?

Hãy chia sẻ nhận xét của bạn + ảnh/video nếu có!

[ONE-CLICK LINKS TO LEAVE REVIEW]:
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Tuyệt vời! Recommend cho bạn bè
⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Tốt, nhưng cần cải thiện
⭐⭐⭐☆☆ Bình thường
⭐⭐☆☆☆ Không như quảng cáo
⭐☆☆☆☆ Rất tệ, muốn trả lại

[LINK TO DETAILED REVIEW FORM]

Bonus: "Để lại ảnh/video review, nhận code giảm 10% cho lần mua tiếp theo!"

Thanks,
[Your Brand Name]
```

**Why one-click links work**:
- Low friction: Just click, no form to fill
- Fast: Captures opinion immediately
- High response rate: 5-15% vs. 1-2% without one-click

**Key elements**:
- Personalization: Use customer's name
- Specific question: Ask about real aspects
- Incentive: Discount for detailed review
- Easy: Multiple click options + link

### 2.2 SMS Review Request

**When**: If you have SMS list, send 2-3 days after delivery

**Message**:
```
"Hi Chị! Sản phẩm có tốt không? 😊 Chia sẻ đánh giá 5 sao để nhận
code giảm 10% lần sau: [short link to review form]"
```

**Why SMS works**:
- Higher open rate than email (98% vs. 20-30%)
- More personal, direct
- Mobile-native (easy to act)

**Trade-off**:
- Limited characters
- Higher cost (if using SMS provider)
- Don't overuse (customers get annoyed)

### 2.4 Follow-up for Incomplete Reviews

**Sequence**:
```
Day 7: First email → 5% review rate
Day 14: Follow-up SMS → "+3% additional (total 8%)
Day 21: Final email "Last chance" → "+2% (total 10%)
```

**Key**: Don't be aggressive
- Max 3 touchpoints
- Friendly tone
- Optional (easy opt-out)

**Example follow-up**:
```
Subject: "We're still waiting for your feedback 💭"

Hi [name],

It seems you haven't shared your review yet. We'd love to hear:
- What you loved
- What could improve
- Would you recommend?

Share your honest feedback: [link]
Bonus: All reviews get 10% off code

[UNSUBSCRIBE from review emails]
```

### 3.2 Quy Trình Xử Lý Review Tiêu Cực (4-Step Framework)

**Step 1: ACKNOWLEDGE (Ghi Nhận)**
- Respond within 24 hours (ngay lập tức, before others pile on)
- Show you read carefully: "You mentioned the product arrived broken..."
- Don't dismiss or defend

**Step 2: APOLOGIZE (Xin Lỗi)**
- "Chúng tôi rất tiếc khi bạn có trải nghiệm tồi tệ"
- Be sincere (không sound corporate/robotic)
- Take responsibility (even if not your fault): "We failed to deliver the quality you expected"

**Step 3: ACT (Hành Động)**
- Provide solution: Replace, refund, discount
- Make it easy: "DM me your order #, I'll process replacement today"
- Empower your team: Let CS take action without approval delay

**Step 4: FOLLOW-UP (Theo Dõi)**
- After resolution: "Did the replacement arrive? Is it better now?"
- Request to update review (if they're happy): "If issue is solved, would you update your review?"
- Don't pressure: They can keep it if they want

### 3.3 Biến Review Xấu Thành Cơ Hội

**Psychology**:
- Customers EXPECT brands to be perfect
- When you respond well to complaint = exceeds expectation
- Bad review + great response = **better than no complaint** (shows you care)

**Conversion Example**:
```
Scenario 1: Perfect product, no complaints
- Customer: 4 stars
- Reason: "Good product, nothing wrong"

Scenario 2: Broken product, great response
- Customer initially: 1 star "Product arrived broken"
- You respond: Replace immediately, nice follow-up
- Customer updates: 5 stars "Product is great, customer service is even better!"

Scenario 2 wins: Trust built, more powerful endorsement
```

**How to approach**:

1. **Listen deeply**: What's the real problem?
   - Surface: "Product broken"
   - Real issue: "I was disappointed, feel scammed"

2. **Exceed expectation**:
   - Expected: Refund or replacement
   - Exceed: Replacement + $20 voucher + apology gift
   - Result: Customer tells friends "They were amazing!"

3. **Turn into story**:
   - Worst scenario: Complaint sits there, more people see it
   - Best scenario: You resolve, customer updates review to positive
   - Others read: "This brand cares, even when things go wrong"

### 3.4 Report/Remove Fake Reviews

**Types of Fake Reviews**:
- Competitor posting false negative reviews
- Bot/fake account leaving reviews (spam)
- Review written by someone who didn't buy product

**How to identify**:
- Same email/account posting multiple reviews
- Review doesn't make sense: "Great product!" with 1 star
- Review content unrelated: Talk about something not in listing
- New account, suspicious pattern

**How to remove** (platform-specific):

**Shopee**:
1. Go to Product → Reviews
2. Click on suspicious review → Report icon
3. Select reason: "Fake review", "Spam", "Inappropriate"
4. Shopee investigates (24-48 hours)
5. If confirmed fake, removed

**Lazada**:
1. Similar process: Product → Ratings → Report
2. Reason: "Suspicious activity", "Spam", "Not related"

**Your website**:
1. Moderate all reviews before publishing
2. Manual filter: Obvious spam/irrelevant
3. Report to review platform (Trustpilot, etc)

## 4. UGC (USER GENERATED CONTENT) STRATEGY

### 4.1 Định Nghĩa UGC

**UGC**: Content tạo bởi customer về brand/product
- **Organic**: Không phải là ads, customer tự tạo (feel real)
- **Authentic**: Video unboxing sloppy, captions casual (higher trust)
- **Powerful**: 92% consumers trust UGC more than branded ads

**Types**:
1. **Photos**: Unboxing, product styling, before-after
2. **Videos**: Unboxing, demo, testimonial, haul
3. **Social posts**: Instagram post, TikTok video, Facebook story
4. **Reviews**: Detailed text reviews with photos

### 4.2 Branded Hashtag Campaign

**Goal**: Create centralized way to find & amplify customer content

**Campaign Structure**:

```
HASHTAG: #[BrandName]Story (or #[BrandName]LoveStory)

How it works:
1. Announce campaign: "Share your [product] story with #[hashtag]"
2. Customers post: Photos/videos with hashtag
3. You track: Monitor hashtag for posts
4. You amplify: Repost best content on official channels
5. You reward: Feature customers, give them shout-outs

Timeline: Ongoing (permanent for brand)
```

**Examples**:
- #ShopeeBeautyCommunity
- #LazadaMustHave
- #MyTikTokShop

**Response rate**:
- First month: 20-50 posts with hashtag
- After 3 months (with consistent amplification): 100+ posts/month
- Quality: Most will have 100-1,000 views

### 4.3 UGC Contest on TikTok/Instagram

**Campaign**:

```
Challenge: "Show us your [product] haul/unboxing/styling"

Rules:
1. Film video (15-60 seconds)
2. Post on TikTok/Instagram
3. Use branded hashtag + sound
4. Tag @[brand] account
5. Deadline: [date]

Prizes:
- Winner (1): $100 product pack + shout-out
- Runner-up (2): $20 voucher + shout-out
- Participation: All videos get to access private code (10% off)

Why participate:
- Chance to win
- Get featured on official account
- 10% discount for all participants
- Community (see other people using product)
```

**Expected engagement**:
- Reach: Your followers + their followers (viral potential)
- UGC volume: 50-200 submissions
- Amplification: Best videos get re-posted on your account

### 4.4 Encourage Organic Unboxing

**Strategy**: Design packaging SO GOOD that unboxing becomes content

**Tactics**:
1. **Beautiful packaging**:
   - Branded colors
   - Premium materials (matte box, ribbon)
   - Unboxing experience (layers, surprise inside)
   - Cost: +$0.50-$1.00 per order (worth it for UGC)

2. **Branded tissue paper**:
   - Include colorful tissue
   - Branded stickers to close box
   - Customers naturally photograph before opening

3. **Surprise gift inside**:
   - Small freebie (sticker, sample, thank-you card)
   - Handwritten note (personalization)
   - Makes unboxing worth sharing

4. **QR code / hashtag included**:
   - Card: "Tag us @[brand] #[hashtag] for chance to be featured"
   - Makes it easy for customers to share + tag you

**Result**: 10-20% of boxes result in social media post

## 5. SỬ DỤNG UGC TRONG MARKETING

### 5.1 UGC trong Ads (Spark Ads TikTok, Testimonial Ads Facebook)

**Spark Ads (TikTok)**:

```
Strategy:
- Take best UGC videos (unboxing, testimonial, before-after)
- Promote them as ads on TikTok (instead of creating ads)
- Cost: CPM lower (native content = better engagement)

Setup:
1. Find best customer video on TikTok (high engagement)
2. Ask permission: "We love your video, can we promote it?"
3. They get: Compensation ($20-100) + exposure
4. You run: As Spark Ad (labeled "Promoted")
5. Result: Audiences see "real customer" not "branded ad"

Performance:
- Regular ad: 1-2% CTR
- Spark Ad (UGC): 3-5% CTR (2-5x improvement)
- Cost per click: 30-50% lower
```

**Facebook Testimonial Ads**:

```
Format:
- Video: Customer talking about product (30-60 sec)
- Interview style: Natural, not scripted
- Emotion: They're genuinely excited, not acting
- Proof: Show product, show results

Setup:
1. Record customer testimonial video
2. Upload to Facebook
3. Create ad campaign (boost post)
4. Target: Warm audience (website visitors, engaged users)
5. Messaging: "See what [customer name] thinks"

Cost:
- $5-10/day
- CPM: 10-15% lower than regular ads
- ROAS: 3-5x higher
```

## 6. KOC (KEY OPINION CONSUMER) PROGRAM

### 6.1 KOC vs. Influencer

**Influencer**:
- Has large following (10k+)
- Profession: Create content
- Motivation: Money/free products
- Trust: Audiences know they're "sponsored"
- Cost: $200-5,000+ per post

**KOC (Key Opinion Consumer)**:
- Micro-influencer (500-5k followers) or regular customer
- Profession: Regular person (student, working, mom)
- Motivation: Free product + small compensation + community
- Trust: Audience thinks "This is my friend's opinion"
- Cost: $20-200 per post (much cheaper)
- Authenticity: MUCH higher (regular person, not professional)

**Why KOCs are valuable**:
- Affordable
- Authentic (feel like peer advice, not ads)
- Relatable (look like the audience)
- Trusted: "If my friend likes it, I should consider it"

### 6.2 How to Find & Recruit KOCs

**Source 1: Your Existing Customers**
- Who has high engagement rate on their posts?
- Who has publicly said nice things about you?
- Who has 500-2,000 followers?

**Approach**:
```
DM them: "Hi [name], we noticed you have great taste in [category].
Would you be interested in collaborating? We'd love to send you
[product] & pay you [amount] to share your honest review."
```

**Source 2: Search Hashtags**
- Search #[productcategory] in TikTok/Instagram
- Find creators posting about category
- Review their style (is it aligned with you?)
- Reach out with offer

**Source 3: Hashtag Followers**
- People using your branded hashtag
- People commenting on competitors' posts
- Highly engaged customers

### 6.3 KOC Brief Template

**Content**: What to ask them to create

```
PRODUCT BRIEF FOR KOC

Product: [Product name]
Compensation: [Amount] + [free product]
Timeline: Post by [date]

Content Requirements:
1. Video OR Photos (you choose)
   - Format: TikTok (15-60 sec) / Instagram Reel / Post
   - Style: Authentic, casual (not polished)
   - Show: Unboxing, styling, using, review

2. Messaging guidelines:
   - Mention: Product name + key benefits
   - Hashtags: Use #[branded hashtag] + your own
   - Tag: @[brand account]
   - Tone: Natural, your own words (not scripted!)
   - Authenticity: Be honest (even mention negatives if any)

3. What NOT to do:
   - Don't sound salesy
   - Don't over-edit (feels inauthentic)
   - Don't lie or exaggerate
   - Don't forget to disclose sponsorship (law requirement)

3. Disclosure requirement:
   Add "#ad" or #sponsored to indicate it's paid content
   Legal: Required to tell audience it's paid partnership

Example caption:
"Just received [product] from @[brand]! #ad #sponsored

[Honest opinion about product]

[Hashtags] #[branded] @[brand]"

4. Approval:
   - Send draft 24h before posting (for approval)
   - We give feedback if needed
   - You handle final editing

Payment: Sent upon proof of posting
```

### 6.4 KOC Compensation Models

**Model 1: Free Product + Cash**
- Free product (usually $50-200 value)
- Cash payment: $20-100 (depends on follower count)
- Best for: Mid-size KOCs (1k-5k followers)
- Total cost: $70-300 per post

**Model 2: Free Product + Commission**
- Free product to try
- Commission on sales: 5-10% of sales they drive
- Tracking: Unique code "KOC_[name]" or affiliate link
- Best for: Ongoing relationships
- Cost: Variable (0 if no sales, or 5-10% if they drive sales)

**Model 3: Affiliate Only**
- KOC gets commission (5-15%) on all their referred sales
- No upfront payment
- Best for: High-performing KOCs
- Risk: If no sales, you pay nothing
- Reward: If they're good, unlimited earning

**Model 4: Product Only**
- No cash, just free product
- Entry-level for new KOCs
- Cost: $50-100 in product
- Risk: Motivation to perform might be lower

**Recommendation**:
- Start with Model 1 (Product + small cash)
- Proven performers → Model 2 (Commission)
- Top performers → Model 3 (Affiliate)

## 7. QUALITY CONTROL & MONITORING

### 7.1 Review Monitoring & Analytics

**What to track**:

```
Daily metrics:
- New reviews posted today
- Average rating trend (4.8 → 4.7 → 4.6 = watch out!)
- Sentiment: Positive % vs. Negative %
- Most common compliments
- Most common complaints

Weekly review:
- Review volume per day (is it increasing?)
- Customer response rate to review requests (%, track if declining)
- Common themes (same complaint repeatedly?)
- Competitor comparison (how do we rate vs. them?)

Monthly analysis:
- Total reviews added
- Rating trend (up or down?)
- Review sentiment breakdown
- Which products have lowest ratings? (fix)
- Which products drive most reviews? (scale)
```

**Tools**:
- Shopee Seller Center: Built-in review analytics
- Lazada Seller Dashboard: Review stats
- Google Sheets: Manual tracking
- Tools: Review monitoring software (optional)

### 7.2 UGC Monitoring

**What to track**:

```
Monthly UGC Metrics:
- # of posts with your branded hashtag
- Total reach of UGC posts
- Engagement on UGC (likes, comments, shares)
- Video vs. photo ratio
- Sentiment: Positive, neutral, negative

Track per source:
- TikTok: # of videos, total views, engagement rate
- Instagram: # of posts/reels, likes, engagement rate
- Facebook: # of posts, shares, comments
- Website reviews: # of photo/video reviews

Content themes:
- Most popular product? (which items get posted most)
- Most popular use case? (how are customers using?)
- Tone of UGC: Entertained, helpful, inspiring?
```

### 7.3 Sentiment Analysis

**Positive indicators**:
- Words: "Amazing", "Love", "Perfect", "Recommend", "Worth it"
- Emojis: Heart, star, thumbs up, happy face
- Phrases: "Better than expected", "Great quality"

**Negative indicators**:
- Words: "Disappointed", "Broken", "Fake", "Scam", "Not worth"
- Emojis: Angry face, thumbs down, X
- Phrases: "Waste of money", "Don't buy", "Poor quality"

**Action if sentiment declining**:
1. Investigate: What changed?
2. Product issue? Logistics issue? Customer service issue?
3. Fix root cause
4. Communicate: Respond to concerns publicly
5. Monitor: Does sentiment improve?

## 8. LEGAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR REVIEWS & UGC

### 8.1 Disclosure Requirements (Vietnam Law)

**FTC-equivalent in Vietnam**: Not as strict as US, but guidelines exist

**What you MUST disclose**:
1. Paid reviews: If you paid customer, MUST disclose
2. Free product reviews: If you gave free product for review, MUST disclose
3. Affiliate reviews: If you get commission, MUST disclose

**How to disclose**:
- Add text: "#ad", "#sponsored", "#ads", "#promotion"
- In caption: "This is a paid partnership"
- In video: Verbal disclosure ("This product was sent by...")

**Example compliant caption**:
```
"Just received this amazing product from @[brand]! #ad #sponsored
#promotion

[Honest review of product]

[Your opinion, even if some negatives]

Discount code: [if provided]

#[brandhashtag]"
```

**Consequence of non-disclosure**:
- Vietnam: Not strict enforcement, but Shopee/Lazada can remove
- Social media: Account can be suspended
- Brand reputation: If discovered, damages trust

### 8.2 Intellectual Property Rights

**When you repost customer content**:
1. Get written permission: "Can I repost your video on our official account?"
2. Credit them: Always tag/mention their account
3. Don't edit: Keep original intact (unless minor edits)
4. Respect: If they ask to remove, remove immediately

**Using content for ads**:
- Requires EXPLICIT written permission
- May need additional compensation
- Consider saying: "$100 if we use for ads" (add incentive)

### 8.3 Terms & Conditions for Reviews

**Your policy should state** (on product page or in review form):

```
REVIEW GUIDELINES

By submitting a review, you grant [brand] permission to:
- Display your review on our website/social media
- Edit for spelling/grammar (not content)
- Use in marketing materials (ads, emails, etc.)

You confirm:
- Review is your honest opinion
- You actually purchased & used product
- Content doesn't violate third-party rights
- You won't post defamatory content

We reserve right to remove reviews that:
- Contain offensive language
- Promote competing products
- Aren't related to product
- Violate our community guidelines
```

- Response time to reviews (respond in 24h)

2. Respond professionally to reviews
3. Use "Ask a Question" feature to build engagement
4. Monitor rating during sales (tends to be lower if you over-promise)
