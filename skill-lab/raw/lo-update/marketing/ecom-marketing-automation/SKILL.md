# MARKETING AUTOMATION - ĐA KÊNH TOÀN DIỆN

## TỔNG QUAN: TẠI SAO AUTOMATION QUAN TRỌNG

### Impact:
- 48% of people use marketing automation tools and find them effective
- Email automation: 3-5x higher ROAS than manual sending

## WORKFLOW 1: WELCOME SERIES (3-5 emails)

### Goal:
Build relationship, set expectations, prime first purchase

### Trigger:
Customer subscribes to email list / Creates account / First visit

### Flow:

**Email 1 (Sent immediately)**
```
Subject: "Welcome to [Brand]! 👋"

Body:
- Say thank you for joining
- What they can expect (deals, content, stories)
- Set tone (friendly, not salesy)

CTA: Soft (explore website, follow on social)
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: "Welcome to Shop Vanity! 👋"

Hi [Name],

Thank you for joining our beauty family!

Here's what you'll get from us:
✓ Exclusive deals before public launch
✓ Beauty tips & tutorials
✓ Stories from real customers
✓ Special birthday gifts

We're not your typical beauty brand – we're here to help you glow at any age.

Excited to have you!
[Name], Founder

[Button: Explore our collection]
```

**Email 2 (3 days later)**
```
Subject: "[Name], here's 30% off (just for you)"

Body:
- Introduce best sellers
- "Most popular" social proof
- Strong offer (time-limited)

CTA: Hard (buy now with coupon)
```

**Email 3 (7 days later)**
```
Subject: "[Name], how to get started with skincare"

Body:
- Educational content (not salesy)
- Beginner's guide
- Show authority

CTA: Moderate (read full guide, join challenge)
```

**Email 4 (14 days later)**
```
Subject: "Join our loyalty program (exclusive perks)"

Body:
- Explain loyalty benefits
- Easy to join
- Long-term value

CTA: Soft (learn more about rewards)
```

**Email 5 (21 days later)**
```
Subject: "[Name], what customers say about us"

Body:
- Video testimonials or text reviews
- Before/after stories
- Build confidence

CTA: Moderate (see more reviews, shop)
```

### Multi-channel (not just email):

**SMS (if they opt-in):**
- Email 1 (Welcome): "Welcome to Shop Vanity! Get 30% off → [link]"
- Email 3 (Day 7): "skincare tips 101 - check email ✌️"

**Zalo OA (if exists):**
- Email 2 (3 days): Broadcast special offer

## WORKFLOW 2: ABANDONED CART RECOVERY (3-5 touches)

### Goal:
Recover sales from customers who added to cart but didn't buy

### Trigger:
Customer adds product to cart but leaves without purchasing (usually 30min - 24h)

### Flow:

**Email 1 (1 hour after abandon)**
```
Subject: "Quên 250k trong giỏ hàng? 🛒"

Body:
- Show product they left
- Quick reassurance (freeship, return policy)
- No offer yet (soft reminder)

CTA: [Complete your order]

Timing: 1 hour (while they still remember)
```

**SMS 1 (3 hours after abandon)**
```
"Áo Nike 250k quên trong giỏ.
Order hôm nay freeship!
[Link]"

(Keep SMS short, urgent)
```

**Email 2 (24 hours after)**
```
Subject: "Giảm thêm 100k cho đơn hàng của bạn 💝"

Body:
- Same product info
- STRONG offer (urgency + discount)
- Explain scarcity (sale ending soon)

CTA: [Claim 100k discount now]

Timing: 24h later (one more chance)
```

**Email 3 (3 days after, if not purchased)**
```
Subject: "Hàng sắp hết 😱"

Body:
- URGENCY (limited stock)
- Social proof (X people bought this)
- Final offer

CTA: [Secure yours before sold out]

Timing: 3 days (last attempt)
```

**Retargeting ad (3-7 days after)**
```
Platform: Facebook, Google, TikTok
Creative: Product image + urgency message
Copy: "Forget something? [Product] - Use code COMEBACK for -100k"
Duration: Run for 7 days, then stop

(Don't keep showing ads after 7 days, likely a no)
```

### Key principles:

- **Email 1:** Soft reminder (they might come back themselves)
- **Email 2:** Real offer (now they need incentive)
- **Email 3:** Urgency (last chance feeling)
- **Remove after purchase:** Don't keep sending to someone who bought

## WORKFLOW 3: POST-PURCHASE NURTURE (3-5 emails)

### Goal:
Ensure they're happy, encourage review, prime next purchase, build loyalty

### Trigger:
Order confirmation (email sent immediately upon purchase)

### Flow:

**Email 1 (Immediate: Order confirmation)**
```
Subject: "Your order is confirmed! #[OrderID]"

Body:
- Order details (product, price, total)
- Tracking info (when available)
- Delivery estimate
- Thank you message

CTA: [Track your order]
```

**Email 2 (3 days after: Product arrives)**
```
Subject: "[Name], your package just arrived! 📦"

Body:
- Confirm delivery
- Quick start guide (how to use product)
- Ask for feedback

CTA: [How to use guide] / [Give feedback]

Timing: After delivery
```

**Email 3 (7 days after: Ask for review)**
```
Subject: "[Name], how do you like it?"

Body:
- Ask for review/rating
- Make it easy (link to review form)
- Small incentive (raffle entry, loyalty points)
- Thank them

CTA: [Leave a review] [Get bonus points]

Timing: 7 days (enough time to use product)
```

**Email 4 (14 days after: Cross-sell)**
```
Subject: "People who bought [X] also loved [Y]"

Body:
- Recommend complementary product
- Bundle discount
- Social proof (bought together)

CTA: [See bundle deal]

Timing: 2 weeks (past buyer mindset)
```

**Email 5 (30 days after: Repurchase)**
```
Subject: "Ready to replenish? [Name]"

Body:
- For consumable products only
- Convenient reorder
- Subscription option (if available)
- Loyalty discount

CTA: [Reorder now] [Subscribe & save]

Timing: 30 days (assuming product consumed by then)
```

## WORKFLOW 4: WIN-BACK CAMPAIGN (2-3 emails)

### Goal:
Re-engage inactive customers, bring them back

### Trigger:
Customer hasn't purchased in 60-90 days (define for your business)

### Flow:

**Email 1 (Soft re-engagement)**
```
Subject: "[Name], we miss you 💔"

Body:
- Acknowledge they bought before
- Share what's new (new products, improvements)
- Soft ask to come back
- No strong offer yet

CTA: [See what's new]
```

**Email 2 (3 days later, with incentive)**
```
Subject: "[Name], special offer just for you"

Body:
- Share improvement/new collection
- Special offer (limited time)
- Scarcity (limited stock, expires tomorrow)

CTA: [Claim offer]
```

**Email 3 (7 days later, if no response)**
```
Subject: "[Name], last chance - 50% off"

Body:
- Last ditch effort
- Strongest offer
- Survey (why didn't they repurchase?)
- Option to unsubscribe gracefully

CTA: [Get 50% off] [Tell us feedback]
```

**After 3 emails:**
- If no response → Remove from list or downgrade frequency
- Don't keep pestering

## WORKFLOW 5: BIRTHDAY / ANNIVERSARY (1-2 emails)

### Goal:
Personal touch, drive purchase around special dates

### Trigger:
Birthday, anniversary of first purchase, subscription date, etc.

### Flow:

**Email 1 (On birthday)**
```
Subject: "Happy birthday [Name]! 🎂 Here's a gift"

Body:
- Personal birthday message
- Special gift code (15-30% off)
- Celebratory tone
- Can use for anything

CTA: [Claim your birthday gift]

Note: Send at 9 AM on their birthday
(If their timezone unknown, send at 9 AM your time)
```

**SMS (Optional, if they opted in)**
```
"Happy birthday [Name]! 🎂
Get 25% off as our gift → [link]"

(Send on birthday)
```

**Email 2 (Next day, if no action)**
```
Subject: "[Name], birthday discount expires tonight!"

Body:
- Urgency (expiring soon)
- Same offer, last chance

CTA: [Use birthday code]
```

### Implementation tips:

- Collect birthday at signup (but make optional)
- Segment into birthday email list
- Schedule 1-2 weeks before (give them time to decide)
- Personalize message (use their name, reference purchase history)

## WORKFLOW 6: PRICE DROP ALERT (1 email)

### Goal:
Notify customers who wishlisted product about price drops

### Trigger:
Product price decreases + customer has wishlisted it

### Flow:

**Email (When price drops)**
```
Subject: "[Product name] price just dropped! 📉"

Body:
- Announce price drop (before/after)
- Show savings
- Limited stock message
- Urgency (don't miss out)

CTA: [Shop at new price]
```

### Technical setup:

```
1. Track wishlist items
2. When price drops:
   - Check if anyone has it wishlisted
   - Send email immediately
   - Include comparison (old price → new price)
```

## WORKFLOW 7: BACK-IN-STOCK ALERT (1 email)

### Goal:
Notify waitlist customers when sold-out product is back

### Trigger:
Out of stock item → back in stock + customers waiting for notification

### Flow:

**Email (When back in stock)**
```
Subject: "[Name], [product] is back in stock! 🎉"

Body:
- Announce: Product is back
- Limited stock warning
- Why they should act now
- Quick overview of product

CTA: [Order now before sold out]
```

### Technical setup:

```
1. On product page, offer "Notify me when back in stock"
2. Collect emails of interested customers
3. When stock = available:
   - Send email to waitlist
   - Offer early access or discount (optional)
   - Include link to product
```

When friend uses referral link:

```
When referred friend purchases:

### Goal:
Remind customers to reorder consumable products on schedule

### Trigger:
Based on product type, average usage duration (e.g., skincare used daily = 30-60 days reorder)

### Flow:

**Email 1 (Soft reminder)**
```
Subject: "[Name], time to replenish?"

Body:
- Reminder (based on purchase date + product)
- How long product lasts (e.g., "lasts 30 days")
- Easy reorder link

CTA: [Reorder [product]]
```

**Email 2 (3 days later, if no reorder)**
```
Subject: "[Name], stock running low soon?"

Body:
- Same reminder, softer
- Subscription option (auto-delivery every 30 days)

CTA: [Reorder] / [Subscribe & save 10%]
```

```
Consumable products:
- Skincare: 30-45 days
- Supplements: 45-60 days
- Shampoo: 60-90 days

Set automation to send reminder at day 20-25 (before likely empty)

## WORKFLOW 12: LEAD SCORING (Ongoing)

### Goal:
Identify high-intent leads, trigger sales team outreach

### How it works:

```
Assign points based on actions:

Email opens: +1 point
Email clicks: +5 points
Product page visit: +3 points
Add to cart: +10 points
Spend time on pricing page: +8 points
Download resource: +2 points
View case study: +5 points
Attend webinar: +15 points

Total score:
- 0-10: Cold lead (nurture)
- 11-30: Warm lead (email marketing)
- 31-50: Hot lead (sales outreach)
- 50+: Very hot (urgent outreach)
```

### Action triggered:

```
When score ≥ 30:
- Move to "Sales-ready" list
- Alert sales team
- Escalate to human contact

When score ≥ 50:
- Sales team calls within 24h
- Offer special deal / consultation
```

## WORKFLOW 15: CHURN PREVENTION (Ongoing)

### Goal:
Identify at-risk customers, prevent them from leaving

### Metrics to track:

```
Define "at-risk":
- Hasn't purchased in 90+ days (was regular buyer)
- Unsubscribed from emails
- Clicked "complaint" or "spam"
- No email opens in 3 months
- Clicking unsubscribe link
```

### Flow:

**Detection triggers:**

```
IF (last purchase > 90 days) AND (was active before):
→ TRIGGER: "At-risk customer"
```

**Action 1: Soft re-engagement**
```
Email subject: "[Name], we miss you 💔"
Body: Acknowledge previous relationship, show what's new
CTA: Soft (explore)
```

**Action 2: Survey**
```
Email subject: "[Name], quick question?"
Body: Ask why they're inactive (feedback loop)
Options: Too expensive / Found alternative / Not needed / Just busy / Other
CTA: [Take 1-min survey]
```

**Action 3: Win-back offer**
```
Email subject: "[Name], come back with -50%"
Body: Special offer to return
CTA: [Get 50% off]
```

**Action 4: Exit survey**
```
If still no action:
Email subject: "Can we do better? 😔"
Body: Final chance to give feedback
CTA: [Unsubscribe] / [Give feedback] / [Stay]
```

**Manual intervention:**
```
If high-value customer:
→ Have team member call
→ Understand churn reason
→ Offer custom solution
```

## MULTI-CHANNEL ORCHESTRATION

### Example: Customer journey across channels

```
Day 0: Subscribe to email list
→ SMS: "Welcome! Get 30% off → [link]"
→ Email: Welcome series begins

Day 3:
→ Email: Best sellers + offer
→ Zalo OA: Broadcast special deal

Day 7:
→ Email: Educational content
→ TikTok/Facebook: Remarketing ad

Day 14:
→ Email: Loyalty program invite
→ SMS: "Join our VIP club, get 50% off first order"

Day 30:
→ If purchased: Post-purchase sequence
→ If not purchased: Win-back sequence

Day 60:
→ Email: Birthday offer (if birthday known)
→ SMS: Flash sale announcement

Day 90:
→ If inactive: Churn prevention sequence
→ If active: Personalized recommendations
```

### Key principle:

Don't send all channels at once (overwhelming)
- Space out touches
- Use different channels for different messages
- Email = detailed, SMS = urgent, Social = brand building

## TIMING RULES

### Email send times (optimize by segment):

```
Consumer (B2C):
- Best: 9-10 AM, 7-8 PM
- Worst: 4-6 PM (work hours)

Professional (B2B):
- Best: 8-9 AM, 4-5 PM
- Worst: 12-2 PM (lunch), 6+ PM (off-hours)

Gen Z:
- Best: 7-9 PM (evening scrolling)

Age 40+:
- Best: 9 AM, 2-3 PM
```

### SMS send times:

```
Only send 9 AM - 8 PM (never before/after)
Best: 7-8 PM (highest engagement)
Avoid: Weekends for B2B, fine for B2C
```

### Push notification:

```
Best: 10 AM, 6-7 PM
Avoid: Before 8 AM, after 9 PM
```

## SEGMENT BY LIFECYCLE STAGE

### STAGE 1: SUBSCRIBER (New, no purchase)

Frequency: 2-3 emails/week (aggressive nurture)
Goal: Drive first purchase
Offers: 30-50% off incentive

### STAGE 2: FIRST-TIME BUYER

Frequency: 1-2 emails/week
Goal: Satisfaction, repeat purchase
Offers: 10-15% off next purchase

### STAGE 3: REPEAT CUSTOMER (2-5 purchases)

Frequency: 1 email/week
Goal: Loyalty, VIP status
Offers: Exclusive access, loyalty rewards

### STAGE 4: VIP / LOYAL CUSTOMER (5+ purchases)

Frequency: 2-3 emails/month (less is more)
Goal: Lifetime value, referrals
Offers: VIP treatment, early access, concierge service

### STAGE 5: INACTIVE / CHURNED

Frequency: 1-2 emails/month (minimal)
Goal: Win-back or graceful exit
Offers: Special comeback offer

## ROI CALCULATION: MARKETING AUTOMATION

```
Baseline (no automation):
- Email list: 10,000
- Manual send: 2 campaigns/month
- Open rate: 20%
- Click rate: 3%
- Conversion rate: 2%
- Monthly revenue from email: 500M

With automation:
- Same list size: 10,000
- Automated sends: 8 campaigns/month (4 nurture, 4 broadcast)
- Open rate: 25% (better timing)
- Click rate: 4% (better content)
- Conversion rate: 2.5% (better targeting)
- Monthly revenue: 2,000M

Improvement: 4x revenue
Tool cost: 50M/month
ROI: (2000M - 500M - 50M) / 50M = 29x

= Very positive ROI
```
