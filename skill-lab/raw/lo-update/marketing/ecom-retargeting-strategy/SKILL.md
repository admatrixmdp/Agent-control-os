# RETARGETING STRATEGY - ĐA KÊNH TOÀN DIỆN

## TỔNG QUAN

### Tại sao retargeting?
- 96% khách lần đầu chưa mua → Cần follow-up
- ROAS retargeting: 3-5x cao hơn prospecting (1-1.5x)
- Phù hợp cho TMĐT: Khách thường follow nhiều bước trước khi mua

### Segment khách theo hành vi:
1. **Viewed product** (60% audience) → Lowest priority
2. **Added to cart** (20% audience) → Medium priority
3. **Initiated checkout** (5% audience) → Highest priority
4. **Past purchasers** (15% audience) → Upsell/Cross-sell

---

## AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION & CREATIVE STRATEGY

### SEGMENT 1: VIEWED PRODUCT (Chưa add to cart)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Clicked link từ ads/organic/referral
- Viewed 1+ product pages
- Rời đi mà không add cart
- ~60% của pixel audience

**Hành động:**
- Quan tâm sản phẩm
- Nhưng chưa sẵn sàng mua ngay (browsing)
- Có thể quên (2-3 tuần sau không nhớ)

**Retargeting creative strategy:**
```
MESSAGE: Nhắc nhở sản phẩm + Social proof
TONE: Friendly, educational, not aggressive

Creative types:
1. Product reminder ads
   - Hình sản phẩm họ xem
   - Headline: "Quay lại xem [sản phẩm] chứ?"
   - Copy: "Giảm 10%, chỉ còn 2 tuần"

2. Social proof ads
   - "100+ người mua [sản phẩm]"
   - Rating 4.8⭐
   - "Đánh giá cao nhất tuần này"

3. Educational content
   - Video: "Cách chọn [loại sản phẩm]"
   - Blog: "5 lợi ích của [sản phẩm]"
   - Hướng dẫn sử dụng

4. Benefit-focused ads
   - "Tìm áo khoác? Cái này bền 2x lâu hơn"
   - "Free shipping, 30-day returns"
```

**Frequency:** 2-3 times/week (7-10 days)
**Timeline:** 7-21 days (nếu không convert → loại khỏi audience)
**Budget allocation:** 20-30% của retargeting budget

**Platform-specific:**
- Facebook: Product carousel ads
- Google: Dynamic product ads
- TikTok: Educational video content
- Email: Product discovery email

---

### SEGMENT 2: ADDED TO CART (Chưa checkout)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Added product to cart
- Nhưng chưa checkout
- Hành vi này = 70% chance mua nếu được nhắc đúng
- ~20% của pixel audience

**Hành động:**
- Có ý định mua
- Nhưng bị từ chối bởi: Giá, shipping, hoặc phân vân lựa chọn

**Retargeting creative strategy:**
```
MESSAGE: Remove barriers + Create urgency
TONE: Helpful, problem-solving, moderate urgency

Creative types:
1. Urgency + Scarcity
   - "Chỉ còn 3 cái! Giá hết nhanh"
   - "[X] người khác đang xem"
   - "Sale chỉ hôm nay, giá quay lại 500k ngày mai"

2. Incentive-based
   - "Mua hôm nay -50k, freeship"
   - "First-time buyers: -30%"
   - "Spent 300k: Freeship automatically"

3. Payment option highlight
   - "Trả góp 0%, không lãi suất"
   - "COD available, no prepay needed"
   - "Apple Pay, Google Pay in 1 click"

4. Trust/guarantee
   - "Money-back guarantee, 30 days"
   - "Free returns, free shipping both ways"
   - "1-year warranty"

5. Comparison
   - "Why choose us vs [competitor]"
   - "Same product, 50k cheaper + freeship"
```

**Frequency:** 3-5 times/week (aggressive, nhưng not spammy)
**Timeline:** 3-7 days (sau đó → remove nếu không convert)
**Budget allocation:** 40-50% của retargeting budget (highest priority)

**Platform-specific:**
- Facebook: Carousel with benefit-focused messaging
- Google: Text ads with urgency + offer
- TikTok: "Last chance to get [product]" video
- Email: "Don't forget [product]" - 1h, 24h, 48h sequence
- SMS: "Giỏ hàng còn 1 giờ nữa!" (short, punchy)

### SEGMENT 3: INITIATED CHECKOUT (Chưa purchase)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Went to checkout page
- Entered shipping info, but didn't complete payment
- Hoàn toàn sẵn sàng mua → Chỉ cần push
- ~5% của pixel audience
- Highest-intent segment

**Hành động:**
- Khách đã quyết định
- Encounter tại bước cuối (payment issue, shipping shock, etc)
- Nghị lực mua cao, chỉ cần remove barrier

**Retargeting creative strategy:**
```
MESSAGE: Direct offer + Remove doubts
TONE: Direct, empathetic, problem-solving (not pushy)

Creative types:
1. Direct discount
   - "Mua nhanh, -100k hôm nay"
   - "Hoàn tiền -200k nếu ship quá 3 ngày"
   - "[Special offer] dành riêng cho bạn"

2. Empathetic messaging
   - "Có vấn đề gì không? Chúng tôi giúp!"
   - "Sợ lỗi? 100% money-back guarantee"
   - "Không yên tâm payment? Try COD"

3. Alternative payment
   - "Prefer COD? Available now!"
   - "Stripe payment issue? Try Momo"
   - "Apple Pay, Google Pay available"

4. Reassurance
   - "1000+ people just ordered"
   - "Delivery in 24h, guaranteed"
   - "Free returns, no questions asked"

5. Help CTA
   - "Chat with us 24/7"
   - "Hotline: [number]"
   - "Questions? Click here"
```

**Frequency:** 4-5 times/week (highest frequency justified)
**Timeline:** 1-3 days (quick conversion window)
**Budget allocation:** 50-60% của retargeting budget (ROI cao nhất)

**Platform-specific:**
- Facebook: Single image + Strong offer
- Google: Text ads (high CTR)
- TikTok: Video showing checkout process (remove fear)
- Email: URGENT - 30min, 2h, 6h, 24h sequence
- SMS: Immediate response → "Quay lại order? -100k!"

---

### SEGMENT 4: PAST PURCHASERS (Đã mua trước)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Already purchased (1+)
- Trust level cao
- Lifetime value: 3-5x first-time buyers
- ~15% của pixel audience

**Hành động:**
- Goal: Upsell, Cross-sell, Repeat purchase
- Không phải convince → Enhance purchase

**Retargeting creative strategy:**
```
MESSAGE: Complementary products + VIP treatment
TONE: Familiar, appreciative, exclusive

Creative types:
1. Cross-sell (related products)
   - Mua áo → Suggest quần, giày
   - Mua vitamin → Suggest protein powder
   - "Customers who bought [X] also love [Y]"

2. Upsell (premium version)
   - Mua version basic → Suggest pro
   - "Upgrade for 50k more, 2x better"

3. New product launch
   - "New arrival for our VIP customers"
   - "Early access to [new product]"

4. Loyalty/rewards
   - "You have 500 loyalty points"
   - "Redeem for [discount]"
   - "Next purchase: Extra 10% off"

5. Bundle deals
   - "Bought [X]? Bundle with [Y] for -30%"
   - "Complete the set: [bundle]"

6. Personalized recommendation
   - Based on purchase history
   - "Customers with your taste love [product]"
```

**Frequency:** 1-2 times/week (low, respect relationship)
**Timeline:** 30-90 days (long-term retention)
**Budget allocation:** 10-20% của retargeting budget

**Platform-specific:**
- Email: Personalized product recommendations
- Facebook: Carousel with past purchase context
- TikTok: Content-style ads (not salesy)
- SMS: Milestone-based ("You've been a customer 1 year!")

**Sub-segments:**
- **Recent purchasers (0-30 days):** Suppress retargeting (too soon)
- **Repeat customers (2+ purchases):** VIP treatment
- **High-value customers (>5M spent):** Premium offers

### SEGMENT 5: ENGAGED (Like/Comment/Share/Video view)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Not viewed product page yet
- But engaged with brand content (like, comment, share, video view)
- Showing interest but not yet strong intent
- ~25% của pixel audience

**Hành động:**
- Interested in brand, not specific product
- Need: Introduce products related to content

**Retargeting creative strategy:**
```
MESSAGE: Build brand affinity + Soft product intro
TONE: Conversational, educational, brand-focused

Creative types:
1. Brand story
   - "Why we started [brand]"
   - "Behind-the-scenes"
   - Build emotional connection

2. Product intro (soft sell)
   - "You might like this based on your interest"
   - Video review/demo
   - Not aggressive sell, more educational

3. Educational content
   - "How to choose [product category]"
   - "5 tips for [related topic]"
   - Establish authority

4. User-generated content
   - Customer testimonials
   - User reviews
   - Social proof without selling
```

**Frequency:** 1-2 times/week
**Timeline:** 14-30 days
**Budget allocation:** 5-10% của retargeting budget

**Platform-specific:**
- TikTok: Organic-style content (educational)
- Facebook: Video content, not direct product ads
- Email: Newsletter-style, product discovery

---

### SEGMENT 6: VIDEO VIEWERS (25%/50%/75%/95% watched)

**Đặc điểm:**
- Watched brand video (ad video, YouTube, TikTok)
- Watched % indicates level of interest
- High watch % = stronger intent

**Audience breakdown:**
```
- 25% watched: Curious, testing
- 50% watched: Interested, exploring
- 75% watched: Very interested, almost convinced
- 95% watched: Extremely interested, needs small push
```

**Retargeting creative strategy by watch %:**

**25% viewers:**
- Educational content (continuation)
- Different angle of same product
- Not ready for aggressive sell

**50% viewers:**
- Product benefits
- Social proof
- Mild urgency

**75% viewers:**
- Strong offer
- Limited time messaging
- CTA-focused

**95% viewers:**
- Direct discount offer
- One-step purchase (link)
- Highest priority

**Frequency:** Adjust by watch % (95% → 5x/week, 25% → 1x/week)
**Timeline:** 3-30 days
**Budget allocation:** 10-15% của retargeting budget

## RETARGETING TRÊN TỪNG KÊNH

### FACEBOOK/INSTAGRAM RETARGETING

**Pixel setup:**
```
1. Install Facebook Pixel on website
2. Track events:
   - ViewContent (product page view)
   - AddToCart (cart addition)
   - InitiateCheckout (checkout start)
   - Purchase (order complete)

3. Create Custom Audiences:
   - Website visitors (all, 1-30 days)
   - View product (7, 14, 30 days)
   - Add to cart (1, 3, 7, 14 days)
   - Checkout (1, 3 days)
   - Purchased (exclude from retargeting)

4. Lookalike Audiences:
   - Based on purchasers
   - Based on high-value customers
   - Based on add-to-cart audience
```

**Campaign structure:**
```
Campaign: Retargeting
├─ Ad Set 1: Add to cart (1-3 days) - High budget
├─ Ad Set 2: Checkout (1 day) - Very high budget
├─ Ad Set 3: Viewed product (7-21 days) - Medium budget
├─ Ad Set 4: Engaged (30+ days) - Low budget
└─ Ad Set 5: Lookalike - Test budget
```

**Creative optimization:**
- Test 3-5 variations per audience
- Rotate: Image vs Video vs Carousel
- Strong headline + short copy
- CTA: "Buy Now" (direct) vs "Learn More" (awareness)

**Bidding strategy:**
- Add to cart (1-3 days): Bid high (maybe CPC 5-10k)
- Viewed product: Bid medium (CPC 3-5k)
- Engaged: Bid low (CPC 1-3k)

**ROAS targets:**
- Cart (1-3d): 4-6x ROAS
- Product view (7-21d): 2-3x ROAS
- Engaged: 1.5-2x ROAS

### GOOGLE REMARKETING

**Setup:**
```
1. Google Tag Manager / GA4 tracking:
   - event_name: "view_item"
   - event_name: "add_to_cart"
   - event_name: "begin_checkout"
   - event_name: "purchase"

2. Create remarketing lists:
   - All visitors (1-540 days)
   - Product viewers (7, 14, 30 days)
   - Cart abandoners (1, 3, 7, 14 days)
   - Purchasers (exclude from cart campaigns)

3. Create custom intent audiences:
   - "Ecommerce shoppers"
   - "Online shoppers"
   - "Shopping researchers"
```

**Campaign types:**

**A. Search Remarketing (RLSA)**
```
Campaign: Search Remarketing
Audience: Add-to-cart users
Bid adjustment: +40% (higher CPC)
Keyword strategy: Mix of product names + generic keywords
Ad: "Complete your purchase: [product] now - Free shipping"
```

**B. Display Remarketing**
```
Campaign: Display Retargeting
Placements: Automatic (Google network)
Format: Image ads (300x250, 728x90, etc)
Creative: Dynamic ads showing products in cart
Frequency cap: 5/day per user
```

**C. Dynamic Remarketing**
```
1. Feed product inventory to Google
2. Setup dynamic ads that show:
   - Exact products user viewed
   - Price, discount, rating
3. Auto-optimizes creative based on products

IMPORTANT: Product image + price + rating = high CTR
```

**Bidding:**
- Cart abandoners: Bid high (+40-60%)
- Product viewers: Bid normal
- Purchase exclusion: Bid very low or exclude

**ROAS targets:**
- Cart: 5-8x ROAS
- Product view: 2-4x ROAS

### TIKTOK RETARGETING

**Pixel setup:**
```
1. Install TikTok Pixel
2. Track events:
   - ViewContent
   - AddToCart
   - Checkout
   - Purchase

3. Create custom audiences:
   - Website visitors (7, 14, 30 days)
   - Video viewers (25%, 50%, 75%, 95%)
   - App users (if have app)
```

**Campaign structure (TikTok Ads Manager):**
```
Campaign: TikTok Retargeting (CBO)
├─ Ad Set 1: Cart abandoners - Native ads (video)
├─ Ad Set 2: Product viewers - Educational content
├─ Ad Set 3: Video viewers (75%+) - Testimonial videos
└─ Ad Set 4: Lookalike - High budget (prospecting)
```

**Creative strategy (TikTok-specific):**
- Hook first 2 seconds (TikTok users swipe fast)
- Native TikTok format (not polished, authentic)
- User-generated content performs well
- Trend-based content (trending sounds, effects)

**Audience note:**
- TikTok VN primarily Gen Z (13-28)
- Tone: Casual, funny, relatable
- Avoid overly salesy messaging

**ROAS targets:**
- Cart: 3-5x ROAS
- Product view: 1.5-2.5x ROAS

- Same product info
- Strong offer + urgency

Subject: "Chỉ còn 1 cái cuối cùng"
Body:
- URGENCY messaging
- Final offer

```
Email 1 (3 days later):
Subject: "You viewed [product] - See more like this"
Body:
- Product info
- 3 related products
- Soft CTA: [See details]

Email 2 (7 days later):
Subject: "Sale on [product category]"
Body:
- Discount on viewed category
- Multiple products
- CTA: [Shop sale]
```

**Sequence 3: Past purchasers (ongoing)**
```
Email 1 (3 days after purchase):
Subject: "How do you like your [product]?"
Body:
- Request review
- Link to review form

Email 2 (14 days after):
Subject: "Complete the set with [complementary product]"

- Cross-sell product

Email 3 (30 days after):
Subject: "You saved 50k this month! 💰"
Body:
- Loyalty messaging

```

**Email metrics to track:**

### SMS RETARGETING

**Use cases:**

**Urgent: Cart abandonment (within 1h)**
```
"Quay lại order! 250k trong giỏ.
Giảm thêm 100k nếu order trong 2h.
[Link]"

- Timing: 1h after abandon
- Frequency: 1-2 SMS per cart
- Response rate: 15-25%
```

**Medium: Product reminder (24h later)**
```
"Yêu thích áo Nike? Freeship hôm nay!
[Link]"
```

**Low: Promotional (3-7 days)**
```
"Sale items up to 50% off!
[Link]"
```

**Important SMS best practices:**
- Max 160 characters
- Personalized (use name if possible)
- Offer clear value
- Short link (use SMS shortener)
- Timing: 9-10am or 7-8pm (not 8pm+)
- Frequency: Max 2-3 SMS per week per user
- Easy unsubscribe option

**SMS provider:** Topship, Bamboo SMS (VN-friendly)

## CROSS-CHANNEL RETARGETING FLOW

### Example: Customer journey across channels

```
Day 1: Sees Facebook ad → Clicks → Views product page
[Pixel fires: ViewContent]

Day 2: Receives email "You viewed [product]"
[Email: Soft reminder]

Day 3: Sees Google Search ad for product
[Google Remarketing: "Product you viewed"]

Day 4: Sees TikTok video (educational content)
[TikTok Audience: 25% video viewers]

Day 5: Receives SMS "Flash sale on [product]!"
[SMS: Time-limited offer]

Day 6: Sees Facebook carousel with strong offer
[Facebook Audience: Cart abandoners if added, else product viewers]

Day 7: Receives email "Last chance!"
[Email: Final sequence]

Day 8: Customer converts
[Pixel fires: Purchase]
[Remove from all retargeting audiences]

Day 30: Email "You loved [product], try this too"
[Email: Upsell to past purchaser]
```

### Key principle:
- Don't show same message on all channels
- Each channel = different message/angle
- Frequency cap across all channels (3-5 total per week)
- Exclude purchased users from urgency campaigns

## FREQUENCY CAPPING & BURNOUT PREVENTION

### Problem:
- Show same ad 20x → Brand look spammy → Negative brand perception
- Frequency > 5/week → Potential negative ROAS

### Solution: Frequency capping

**Facebook/Instagram:**
```
Facebook Ads Manager:
[Campaign] → [Ad set] → Frequency cap
Options:
- 3 impressions per user per day
- 10 impressions per user per week
- 15 impressions per user per month

For cart: 3-5 per week
For product view: 2-3 per week
```

**Google Ads:**
```
Campaign settings → Frequency capping
Options:
- 3 impressions per user per day
- 15 impressions per user per week
```

**TikTok:**
```
Ad Set level → Frequency capping
Recommend: 1-2 impressions per day
```

### Creative rotation:
- Create 3-5 variations per audience
- Rotate every 3-5 days
- Pause if CTR < 0.5% (burned out)

### Audience churn:
```
If cart audience = 5,000 today:
- 30% convert (1,500 purchase, exclude)
- 20% never engage (1,000 inactive, pause)
- 50% still interested (2,500 remain)

Result: Audience shrinks, frequency increases automatically
Solution: Keep adding new pixel traffic
```

## ATTRIBUTION & PIXEL TRACKING

### Pixel placement (critical):
```
Homepage: Base pixel (all visitors)
Product page: ViewContent (+ product ID, price)
Add to cart: AddToCart (+ product ID, value)
Checkout: InitiateCheckout (+ value, currency)
Confirmation: Purchase (+ value, transaction ID)
```

### Facebook Pixel code example:
```
// ViewContent event on product page
fbq('track', 'ViewContent', {
  content_ids: ['product_id_123'],
  content_name: 'Nike Jacket',
  content_type: 'product',
  value: 250000,
  currency: 'VND'
});

// AddToCart
fbq('track', 'AddToCart', {
  content_ids: ['product_id_123'],
  value: 250000,
  currency: 'VND'
});

// Purchase
fbq('track', 'Purchase', {
  content_ids: ['product_id_123'],
  value: 250000,
  currency: 'VND',
  content_type: 'product'
});
```

### UTM parameters:
```
Use for email/SMS tracking:
utm_source=email
utm_medium=retargeting
utm_campaign=cart_abandon_1

Example URL:
https://shop.com/product?utm_source=email&utm_medium=retargeting&utm_campaign=cart_day1
```

### Multi-touch attribution:
- First-click attribution: Credit first touchpoint
- Last-click attribution: Credit last touchpoint (default)
- Data-driven attribution: Credit all touches

**Recommend:** Use data-driven or multi-touch (shows true value of retargeting)

## BUDGET ALLOCATION

### Total retargeting budget split:

```
100% retargeting budget:
├─ 50% Add-to-cart (1-7 days) - Highest ROAS
├─ 20% Checkout (1-3 days) - Second highest ROAS
├─ 15% Product viewers (7-21 days) - Medium ROAS
├─ 10% Engaged/Others - Lower ROAS
└─ 5% Testing/experimentation
```

### Channel allocation (total retargeting budget):
```
Assuming 10M VND/month retargeting budget:

├─ Facebook/Instagram: 40% (4M)
│  └─ Highest reach in VN
├─ Google: 30% (3M)
│  └─ Search + display combined
├─ Email: 15% (1.5M)
│  └─ Tool cost + list maintenance
├─ TikTok: 10% (1M)
│  └─ Growing platform, test carefully
├─ SMS: 3% (0.3M)
│  └─ High-intent moments
└─ Other (Zalo, etc): 2% (0.2M)
```

### Budget by time:
- Sales/Flash sale day: +300% retargeting budget (urgency)
- Off-peak: -20% retargeting budget (lower traffic anyway)
- Holiday season: +200% retargeting budget

## RETARGETING TIMELINE

### Recommended windows:

```
Day 0: User takes action (view, add cart, checkout)

Day 1: URGENT
- Email (1h after, 24h after)
- SMS (3-4h after)
- Facebook/Instagram ads (start)

Days 2-3: IMPORTANT
- Google search ads
- Email (48h after)
- Facebook/Instagram carousel
- TikTok native ads

Days 4-7: MEDIUM
- Google display ads
- Email (product recommendation)
- Facebook image ads
- Continue SMS if not converted

Days 8-30: LOW
- Lookalike audiences
- Newsletter/engagement content
- Past purchaser nurture

Day 30+: STOP
- Archive from retargeting lists
- Move to email newsletter or win-back campaigns
```

## EXCLUSION LISTS

### Important: Remove these users

**1. Purchasers**
```
Exclude from:
- Cart abandonment ads
- "Limited time" offers
- Urgency-based messaging

Allow in:
- Cross-sell / Upsell campaigns
- Newsletter
```

**2. High-frequency exposed users**
```
If user saw ad 10+ times in past week:
- Pause ads for 7 days
- Show different creative when restarted
```

**3. Non-converters (30+ days)**
```
If user in audience 30+ days, never converted:
- Remove from urgent campaigns
- Move to low-frequency nurture
```

**4. Traffic sources to exclude**
```
- Bots / Invalid traffic (if detected)
- Low-quality traffic sources
- VPN / Proxy (depending on strategy)
```

## KPI & MEASUREMENT

### Key metrics:

**1. Retargeting ROAS (Overall)**
```
ROAS = Revenue from retargeting ads / Spend on retargeting ads
Target: 3-5x (depends on margin)
```

**2. Conversion rate by audience**
```
Add-to-cart: 3-8% (highest)
Viewed product: 0.5-2%
Engaged: 0.2-0.5%

Track each audience separately for optimization
```

**3. Cost per acquisition (CPA)**
```
CPA = Retargeting spend / Conversions
Target: < 20% of product margin

Ví dụ: 300k product (100k margin)
Target CPA: < 20k
```

**4. Return on ad spend by channel**
```
Facebook: 4-6x
Google: 3-5x
Email: 8-12x (only tool cost)
SMS: 10-15x
TikTok: 2-4x (newer, lower ROAS)
```

**5. Frequency**
```
Track: Average impressions per user per week
Should be: 3-5 (not 1-2, not 10+)
```

**6. Audience size trends**
```
Week 1: 50,000 users
Week 2: 65,000 users (new pixel traffic)

Healthy: Audience stable or growing
Unhealthy: Shrinking (bad pixel or no new traffic)
```
