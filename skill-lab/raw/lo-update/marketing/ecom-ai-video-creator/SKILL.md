# SKILL: Tạo Video Marketing Hoàn Chỉnh Bằng AI Cho TMĐT

## 1. 7 LOẠI VIDEO TMĐT CẦN TẠO

### 1.1. Product Showcase (15-30 giây) — Quay 360°, Zoom Chi Tiết, Lifestyle

**Mục đích:** Hiển thị sản phẩm dưới các góc khác nhau, chi tiết, chất liệu

**Cấu trúc:**
```
Giây 0-3: Máy quay từ xa, sản phẩm trên background đơn giản
Giây 3-8: Xoay 360 độ chậm rãi
Giây 8-15: Zoom vào chi tiết (zipper, texture, logo, seams)
Giây 15-25: Đặt tay cảm sản phẩm (nếu là fashion/texture product)
Giây 25-30: Pull back, show final composition, gradient overlay với product name
```

**AI Tools tốt nhất:** Google Veo, Kling AI, Seedance
**Thời lượng:** 15-30 giây
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical) cho social, 16:9 cho website
**BPM nhạc:** 120-140 (energetic but not frantic)

**Prompt Veo:**
```
A premium [PRODUCT NAME] centered on white minimal background. Camera
slowly orbits around the product 360 degrees from multiple angles,
showing all sides of the product. Then camera zooms in on key details
like stitching, material texture, logo, finishing touches. Smooth,
slow camera movement. Product looks expensive and high-quality.
Studio lighting with soft shadows. Duration 25 seconds. Professional
product video quality, 1080p, cinematic
```

**Ví dụ cụ thể - Túi xách:**
```
A beautiful caramel brown leather crossbody bag centered on white
background. Camera slowly orbits around 360 degrees showing front,
sides, back, straps, and hardware. Then zooms in to show leather
grain, stitching detail, gold buckle engraving, interior pockets.
Lighting emphasizes leather quality. Duration 25 seconds.
Cinematic, professional product video, 1080p
```

**Post-production in CapCut:**
- Add product name text overlay (appear at 15s, rotate away at 25s)
- Background color fade to brand color
- Add music (energetic but not distracting)
- Add price text + "Shop Now" CTA at end
- Add product dimensions/specifications as subtitle crawl

### 1.2. TikTok/Reels Content (15-60 giây) — Hook + Demo + CTA

**Mục đích:** Hook người xem trong 3 giây đầu, giữ chú ý, tạo action

**Cấu trúc (15s version):**
```
Giây 0-2: HOOK - Câu hỏi, POV, shock, hoặc direct benefit
Giây 2-10: DEMO - Sản phẩm hoạt động hoặc lợi ích trực tiếp
Giây 10-15: CTA + End frame - "Link bio", "Shop now", social proof
```

**Cấu trúc (30s version):**
```
Giây 0-3: Hook mạnh (POV: "Bạn tìm được...", "Cách đơn giản nhất...")
Giây 3-10: Build - Nêu vấn đề hoặc tạo curiosity
Giây 10-20: Demo - Sản phẩm giải quyết vấn đề, hiệu quả
Giây 20-28: Social proof - Review highlight, testimonial snippet, số lượng bán
Giây 28-30: CTA + Link bio
```

**Hook formulas proven effective:**
1. "POV: You found the..." / "POV: Bạn tìm được..."
2. "Tôi không tin cho đến khi..." / "I didn't believe until..."
3. "Ai chưa thử..." / "Who hasn't tried..."
4. "3 secrets..." / "3 bí mật..."
5. "Trước vs sau" / "Before vs After"

**AI Tools:** Veo, Kling, Seedance (cho motion), DALL-E/Midjourney (cho still frames → motion)

**Prompt Veo (TikTok hook video):**
```
HOOK SCENE (0-3s): Woman's hand revealing a [PRODUCT] dramatically,
shocked expression, close-up, dynamic camera movement, bright lighting,
energetic. Duration 3 seconds.

DEMO SCENE (3-15s): Product in use, showing immediate benefit/result,
hands-on demonstration, smooth camera, slow motion on key moment,
satisfying. Duration 12 seconds.

CTA SCENE (15-15s): Product on table, price text overlay appears,
link appears at bottom. Duration 2 seconds.

Total: 15 seconds. Color grading: Bright, saturated, trendy.
Music sync: Energetic beat, drop at hook moment. TikTok aesthetic,
vertical 9:16, 1080p
```

**Ví dụ cụ thể - Mascara:**
```
HOOK (0-3s): Close-up of mascara wand touching eye, eye opens dramatically,
lashes looking long and dramatic. "OMG" text overlay. Dynamic camera,
bright white background.

DEMO (3-15s): Apply mascara frame-by-frame, show before/after eye comparison,
woman smiling confidently, swatch on arm showing dark black color.
Slow motion on opening eye moment.

CTA (15-15s): Product shot on white background, "LASH PERFECTION MASCARA",
price "99K", "Shop link in bio" button overlay.

Total 15 seconds. Bright, energetic color grading. Music: TikTok trending
sound with drops matching the hook moment.
```

**TikTok-specific tips:**
- Hook trong 1-3 giây là CRITICAL
- Dùng trending audio + adapt lyrics/meaning cho product
- Mobile-first: Vertical 9:16, text readable on 4-5 inch screen
- Text overlay không quá (max 3-4 lần)
- Subtitle/text lớn, high contrast (white on dark, black on light)
- Color grading: Trend hiện tại (warm gold, cool teal, vintage, vibrant)
- Pattern interrupt: 1 scene mỗi 2-3 giây (không stagnant)

### 1.3. Ads Video (15-30 giây) — Facebook/Google/TikTok Ads

**Mục đích:** Direct response, tối ưu conversion, khác TikTok (ít trendy, ít animation)

**Cấu trúc (15s Facebook Ads):**
```
Giây 0-2: Hook khác - Problem statement hoặc relatable scenario
Giây 2-8: Solution - Sản phẩm giải quyết từng bước
Giây 8-12: Proof - Results, testimonial, số lượng bán, rating
Giây 12-15: CTA - "Shop Now", "Add to Cart", "Learn More", price
```

**Cấu trúc (30s Google Ads):**
```
Giây 0-3: Problem - "Tìm không được [benefit]? Chúng tôi có giải pháp"
Giây 3-12: Product details - Features, benefits, material, specifications
Giây 12-20: Social proof - Customer reviews, before/after, star rating
Giây 20-28: Offer - Price, discount, limited time, special bonus
Giây 28-30: Call to action - "Click here", "Shop now", website link
```

**Ad angles khác nhau:**

**Angle 1 - Problem/Solution:**
```
Problem: "Khó tìm được [benefit]"
Solution: "Sản phẩm này giải quyết bằng..."
Demo: Show solution in action
Proof: Testimonial, results
CTA: Action
```

**Angle 2 - Urgency/Scarcity:**
```
Attention: "Chỉ còn X cái!"
Desire: Show why limited/popular
Action: Buy now before too late
```

**Angle 3 - Comparison/Alternative:**
```
Show problem with alternatives
Introduce your solution
Demo advantages
Testimonial vs competitors
CTA: Better choice

Ví dụ:
"Skincare mắc mà không hiệu quả? Thử [OUR BRAND] - kết quả trong 30 ngày"
```

**Angle 4 - Social Proof First:**
```
Start with results/testimonials (counter-intuitive works well)
Explain what it is
Show how it works
Urgency element
CTA
```

**Video specs by platform:**

| Platform | Aspect Ratio | Duration | Format | Audio |
|----------|--------------|----------|--------|-------|
| Facebook Ads | 1:1 hoặc 4:5 | 15-30s | MP4 H.264 | Important, include captions |
| Google Ads | 16:9 | 15-30s | MP4 | Background, not critical |
| TikTok Ads | 9:16 | 15-60s | MP4 | Critical, trending audio |
| Instagram Ads | 1:1 hoặc 4:5 | 15-30s | MP4 | Less important |
| YouTube Ads | 16:9 | 15-30-60s | MP4 | Important |

**Prompt Veo (Facebook Ads 30s):**
```
SCENE 1 (0-5s): Woman looking frustrated trying skincare, products
scattered, confused expression. Lighting: Soft window light, slightly
muted colors to show problem state.

SCENE 2 (5-15s): Introduce [PRODUCT NAME] in hand, show applying,
smooth transition to happy expression. Lighting becomes brighter.
Before/after skin side by side.

SCENE 3 (15-25s): Multiple women using product, glowing skin, confidence,
activities (work, date, shopping). Quick cuts. Color: Warm and bright.

SCENE 4 (25-30s): Product shot on clean background, text overlay
showing "Results in 30 days", price, discount offer, "Shop now" button.

Total 30 seconds. Color grading: Problem scenes muted, solution scenes
warm and bright. Music: Emotional piano building to upbeat ending.
Captions: Show main benefits as text (for muted viewing). Format 4:5
vertical for Facebook feed.
```

**Ví dụ cụ thể - Skincare ads:**
```
SCENE 1 (0-5s): Woman touching rough forehead, zoomed in on texture,
unhappy. Music: Sad piano. Text: "Dark spots? Rough texture?"

SCENE 2 (5-15s): Introduce serum bottle, show applying 2-3 drops,
massaging into skin. Close-up of serene face. Music: Building. Text:
"Clinically proven ingredients"

SCENE 3 (15-25s): Before/after split screen, after skin glowing, smooth,
bright. Woman smiling. Music: Positive. Text: "Results in 30 days!"

SCENE 4 (25-30s): Product on white background, price "299K", discount
"SAVE 30%", button "Shop Now". Text: "Limited time offer"

Format: 4:5 vertical, 30 seconds, MP4 with captions embedded
```

### 1.4. Unboxing/Review Style (30-90 giây) — UGC Look

**Mục đích:** ASMR-like, authentic, build anticipation, detail focus, emotional connection

**Cấu trúc (60s Unboxing):**
```
Giây 0-5: Hands receiving package, showing logo, excitement
Giây 5-15: Slowly open box, reveal tissue paper, anticipation music
Giây 15-30: Take out product, show from angles, touch/feel, texture close-up
Giây 30-45: Read packaging, show details, explain features, unpack accessories
Giây 45-55: Final product shot, trying on or using, reaction
Giây 55-60: Overall impression, recommendation, link
```

**UGC (User-Generated Content) characteristics:**
- Natural, không quá polished
- Vertical 9:16 video
- Natural lighting (không studio)
- Phone-quality (not 8K cinematic)
- Authentic reactions, giả vờ excited
- ASMR sounds: Crinkle packaging, soft touching, whispered voice
- "Haul" feeling: Not just 1 product, multiple items

**AI Tools:** Veo (for realistic hands/movement), Kling (for texture detail), plus authentic voice-over

**Prompt Veo (Unboxing 60s):**
```
POV hands unboxing a luxury skincare set. Hands in frame (manicured
nails, warm skin tone), slowly pulling apart a branded box with tissue
paper. Close-ups of each product being lifted out, turning to show
label, texture close-ups. Soft ASMR sound of tissue rustling, box
opening. Warm window lighting, on a marble or wooden table. First-person
POV mostly, some angles from side. Natural, authentic pace (slow,
deliberate). Duration 60 seconds. ASMR quality, 9:16 vertical mobile
format. 1080p.
```

**Audio/ASMR elements:**
- Box opening sound (satisfy)
- Tissue rustling
- Product touching
- Soft exhale of satisfaction
- Whispered voice-over (excited but not loud)
- Minimal music (just background ambient)

**Editing tips in CapCut:**
- Each product 10-15 seconds
- Slow motion on key moments (opening, first touch, using)
- Close-up audio emphasizing sounds (layer good microphone audio)
- Zoom in/out smoothly to follow hand movements
- B-roll of lifestyle with product
- Add review text overlays: "Love this!", "Smells amazing", "Perfect for..."

**Real example - Beauty unboxing:**
```
Channel POV: Hands unboxing beauty box
0-5s: Close-up of branded box exterior, logo, person's hands
5-15s: Slowly open box, show tissue, pull up first item (face wash)
15-25s: Examine bottle, read label, smell it (satisfied reaction)
25-35s: Take out second item (serum), show luxury packaging
35-45s: Take out third item (cream), swatch on arm, touch texture
45-55s: All 3 products arranged, person pours drinks to celebrate
55-60s: Final product shot, "Complete skincare routine from [BRAND]!",
link in description text

Audio: ASMR sounds + soft voice-over describing each product benefit
Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080p, no captions (voice IS the content)
```

### 1.5. Tutorial/How-to (1-3 phút) — Hướng dẫn sử dụng

**Mục đích:** Educate, show proper usage, reduce returns, build trust, increase time on page

**Cấu trúc (3 phút tutorial):**
```
Giây 0-15: Intro - "Today I'll show you how to..."
Giây 15-45: Setup - Gather materials, explain what you need
Giây 45-120: Step-by-step - 4-5 main steps, each 15-20 seconds
Giây 120-150: Results - Show final outcome, before/after comparison
Giây 150-180: Tips - Pro tips, common mistakes, variations
Giây 180: Outro - CTA, subscribe, link in description
```

**Tutorial types:**

**Type 1 - Product Usage Tutorial:**
```
Ví dụ: "How to apply skincare layering"
Step 1: Cleanse face
Step 2: Apply toner with cotton pad
Step 3: Apply essence by patting
Step 4: Apply serum with dropper
Step 5: Apply cream with massage
Result: Glowing, hydrated skin
```

**Type 2 - DIY/Recipe Tutorial:**
```
Ví dụ: "How to make face mask at home"
Ingredients shown
Step-by-step mixing
Apply and wait
Remove
Result/before-after
```

**Type 3 - Troubleshooting Tutorial:**
```
Ví dụ: "Why your makeup isn't lasting - SOLVED"
Problem explanation
Common mistakes highlighted
Correct technique shown
Product solutions introduced
Result: Long-lasting makeup
```

**Tutorial video specs:**
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape for web/YouTube), 9:16 (vertical for social)
- Quality: Full HD (1080p) minimum, 4K if product detail matters
- Close-ups critical: Show exact hand placement, amount of product, technique
- Lighting: Bright, even, no shadows (tutorial should be clear)
- Camera: Overhead (top-down) for most beauty tutorials, POV hands-on for lifestyle
- Text overlays: Step numbers, duration, product names/links
- Music: Calm, focused (não distract from instruction), 80-100 BPM
- Pacing: Slower than entertainment videos, allow time to follow along

**Prompt Veo (3-minute beauty tutorial):**
```
INTRODUCTION (0-15s): Woman introduces skincare routine, smiles at camera,
bright warm lighting, close-up of face then pullback to show all products.
Music: Calm educational, soft piano.

STEP 1: CLEANSE (15-45s): Hands applying water to face, gentle circular
motions, close-up of face while cleansing, water running off, patting dry.
Overhead camera angle showing technique.

STEP 2: TONER (45-75s): Pour toner onto cotton pad, close-up of texture,
apply to face with gentle swiping motions, pad shows dirt removal.
Voice-over explains: "Toner removes remaining impurities".

STEP 3: ESSENCE (75-105s): Apply essence by dropping into palm, patting
into face. Multiple angles showing patting technique. Voice-over: "Pat,
not rub - gentler on skin".

STEP 4: SERUM (105-135s): Apply serum drops, gentle massage upward.
Close-up showing how liquid absorbs. Voice-over: "Serum concentrates
active ingredients".

STEP 5: CREAM (135-165s): Apply cream with upward strokes, focus on
delicate eye area. Massage completed. Full face shot showing glow.
Voice-over: "Cream locks in hydration".

RESULT (165-180s): Before/after comparison side by side, satisfied face
shot, "10-minute routine for glowing skin!", product links shown.

Duration: 3 minutes exactly. Aspect ratio: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok.
1080p. Educational, clear, step-by-step pacing.
```

### 1.6. Brand Story (30-60 giây) — Emotional Storytelling

**Mục đích:** Connect emotionally, build brand value, justify premium pricing, create loyalty

**Cấu trúc (60s Brand Story):**
```
Giây 0-5: Hook - Opening image/statement that's emotional or intriguing
Giây 5-20: Problem/Inspiration - Why brand was created, pain point
Giây 20-40: Journey - How brand solves it, core values, craftsmanship
Giây 40-55: Impact - Customer testimonials, results, community
Giây 55-60: Call to action - Join the movement, be part of story
```

**Story angles:**

**Angle 1 - Founder's Personal Story:**
```
"Tôi từng gặp [problem], tìm giải pháp mãi không ra, nên tôi tự tạo [product].
Bây giờ mình muốn chia sẻ với người khác."
```

**Angle 2 - Mission-Driven:**
```
"Chúng tôi tin vào [value]. Mỗi sản phẩm được tạo với [principle].
Khi bạn mua, bạn đang hỗ trợ [cause]."
```

**Angle 3 - Community:**
```
"Bắt đầu từ X người yêu thích sản phẩm. Bây giờ có X,000 người.
Đây là một cộng đồng của những người [describe]."
```

**Angle 4 - Heritage/Craft:**
```
"Kỹ thuật truyền thống từ [place], kết hợp công nghệ hiện đại.
Mỗi sản phẩm là tác phẩm."
```

**Prompt Veo (60s Brand Story):**
```
OPENING (0-10s): Inspirational imagery. Woman's face showing emotion
(frustration then hope), or artisan's hands working. Warm, cinematic lighting.
Voiceover: "This story started with one woman's dream..."

JOURNEY (10-35s): Montage of creation process. Natural/sustainable
ingredients being sourced, hands crafting product, care and attention
shown. Montage paced with music, maybe 3-4 cuts per 10 seconds.
Voiceover: "We believe beauty should be [value]. We source from [place].
Each product takes [time/care]."

IMPACT (35-50s): Customers using product, smiling, testimonials appearing
as text or voice. Real people (UGC-style) or candid moments. Community
feeling. Voiceover: "Over 10,000 women trust us. Their transformation
is our mission."

CLOSING (50-60s): Brand logo animation, mission statement, beautiful
product shot. Inspirational music crescendo. Text: "Join the movement.
Shop [brand name]."

Duration: 60 seconds. Aspect ratio: 16:9 for YouTube/website, 9:16 for
social. Color grading: Warm, cinematic, premium aesthetic. Music: Emotional,
building, instrumental. Voiceover: Calm, authentic, female voice if female-
led brand. Quality: 4K cinematic production value.
```

**Brand storytelling tips:**
- Authentic > polished (real people > models)
- Emotional arc: Problem → Solution → Hope/Inspiration
- Show the "why", not just "what"
- Include diverse representation
- Highlight values (sustainability, fairness, quality, innovation)
- Music is 50% of emotional impact
- Pacing: Slower than entertainment videos, allow emotional moments to breathe

### 1.7. Livestream Highlight (15-30 giây) — Tổng Hợp Moments Hay

**Mục đích:** Clip từ livestream bán hàng để feed/Reels, FOMO creation, drive to livestream

**Cấu trúc (30s Highlight):**
```
Giây 0-2: Hook - "Watch this [moment] LIVE!"
Giây 2-10: Best moment #1 - Big sale, funny interaction, deal
Giây 10-18: Best moment #2 - Customer excitement, game giveaway
Giây 18-26: Best moment #3 - Flash sale, scarcity moment
Giây 26-30: CTA - "Join next livestream [DATE/TIME], tap to join LIVE"
```

**Moments to highlight:**
- Flash sale countdown and "SOLD OUT" moment
- Host's funny reaction/joke that went viral
- Customer comment that was heartfelt/funny
- Game/giveaway moment with winner celebration
- Big order excitement (phone vibration sound, ding)
- Host and team playful interaction
- Product demo that's impressive
- Deal reveal that's shocking/good

**Prompt Veo (30s livestream highlight compilation):**
```
HOOK (0-2s): Text "WATCH THIS LIVE MOMENT" with excitement emoji,
transition to...

MOMENT 1 (2-8s): Livestream scene - Host showing product excitedly,
customer comments scrolling fast, energy high, quick cuts showing
engagement. Host's smile. "Flash sale in 3, 2, 1!" Drama building.

MOMENT 2 (8-16s): Items getting marked "SOLD OUT" quickly, phone
dinging with orders, host's surprised/happy reaction, fist pump.
Energy peak. Text overlay: "Sold out in 2 minutes!"

MOMENT 3 (16-26s): Host doing game/giveaway, customer winning,
confetti animation, host and customer happy. Feel-good moment.
Music peak.

CLOSING (26-30s): "Join us LIVE every [DAY] at [TIME]! Link in bio!
Tap to join!" with date/time graphic. Transition to brand logo.

Duration: 30 seconds exactly. Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical for
social feed. 1080p. Fast-paced editing, cuts every 1-2 seconds
to maintain energy. Audio: Mix of livestream audio + music underneath.
Color grading: Bright, energetic, saturated.
```

**Editing livestream highlights in CapCut:**
- Take best moments from 1-4 hour livestream (can be 30-60 minutes of footage)
- Cut out slow parts, dead air, technical issues
- Speed up less interesting parts (1.5-2x speed)
- Add sound effects (ding for orders, drum hits for big moments, applause)
- Text overlays for context (product name, price, discount)
- Emojis and stickers for energy
- Music that matches livestream vibe (same song or similar energy)
- Clear CTA for next livestream date/time

## 2. AI VIDEO WORKFLOW - BỐN BƯỚC CHO MỖI LOẠI VIDEO

### 2.1. General 4-Step AI Video Workflow

**STEP 1: Script/Storyboard (Scripting Phase)**

Tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

Purpose: Define exactly what video will show

What to output:
```
Scene breakdown:
- Scene 1: [Description] Duration: [X seconds]
- Scene 2: [Description] Duration: [X seconds]
- Scene 3: [Description] Duration: [X seconds]
...

Details:
- Angle/POV: [Overhead/side/close-up/etc]
- Lighting: [Natural/studio/dramatic/etc]
- Colors: [Warm/cool/vibrant/etc]
- Transitions: [Cut/fade/zoom/etc]
- Music mood: [Energetic/calm/emotional/etc]
- Voiceover: [Yes/no, if yes: what tone, what words]
```

Example - Script for 15s TikTok ad:
```
SCENE 1 - HOOK (0-3s):
Close-up of mascara wand approaching eye from right side. Woman's eye opens
as wand applies. Fast, dramatic moment. Close POV. Bright white background
lighting. "OMG!" text overlay appears. No music yet.

SCENE 2 - DEMO (3-12s):
Show before eye and after eye side by side. Before: natural lashes, no makeup.
After: long, dramatic, dark lashes. Transition with zoom. Woman smiles
confidently. Music starts - energetic pop beat drops. Close-up to medium shot.
Bright warm lighting. Text: "12-hour wear"

SCENE 3 - CTA (12-15s):
Product bottle on white background. Price "99K" appears, discount "SAVE 30%"
fades in. "SHOP NOW" button glows. Logo of brand top-right. Music climax.
Black text on white, high contrast.

OVERALL:
Duration: 15 seconds exactly
Aspect: 9:16 vertical
Pacing: Fast, energetic (1 scene per 3-5 seconds)
Music: Trending TikTok sound OR original upbeat beat drops at 3s
Color: Bright, high saturation
Text: Max 4 text elements, large font, white/black contrast
```

---

**STEP 2: Generate Visuals (Visual Generation Phase)**

Tools: Google Veo, Kling AI, Seedance, DALL-E, Midjourney

Purpose: Create or source video footage using AI

Process:
```
Option A - Generate entire video from scratch:
Use Google Veo or Kling with detailed prompt based on script

Option B - Generate scenes separately:
Use Veo/Kling for each scene separately, then edit together in post

Option C - Use AI images as base:
Generate key frame image with DALL-E/Midjourney, then animate with Veo
(upload image, prompt Veo to "Create video from this image, add [movement]")

Option D - Hybrid:
Mix generated video + UGC footage + product shots
```

**Quick conversion: Script → Veo Prompt**

Script element → Veo Prompt element
```
Scene 1: Close-up mascara + eye opening
→ "Close-up of mascara wand approaching eye, eye opening dramatically,
fast movement, white background, bright studio lighting, 3 seconds"

Scene 2: Before/after + smile + music sync
→ "Split screen before eye (natural) and after eye (long lashes), zoom
transition, woman smiling confidently, 9 seconds, music sync on smile moment"

Scene 3: Product shot + text appearance
→ "Product bottle on white background, camera slow zoom-in, high
contrast lighting, 3 seconds, ready for text overlay to be added in post"
```

**Video generation tips:**

| Tip | Why | How |
|-----|-----|-----|
| Generate in 1080p minimum | Social platforms need clarity | Specify "1080p" or "4K" in prompt |
| Keep each Veo video 15-25s | Avoid generation quality drop after 30s | Split long videos into multiple Veo generations |
| Use reference images | AI video quality improves with visual reference | Upload image, use as style reference in prompt |
| Specify aspect ratio | Wrong ratio = can't use | Say "--ar 9:16" or "vertical 9:16" |
| Describe camera movement | Makes video less static | "Camera slowly zooms in", "pans right to left" |
| Name specific lighting | Lighting is everything | "Soft window light from left", "studio harsh light" |

**Common issues and fixes:**

| Issue | Reason | Fix |
|-------|--------|-----|
| Video looks fake | AI is trying too hard | Add "realistic", "authentic", remove "cinematic" |
| Product looks bad | Prompt not descriptive enough | Add "high-end product photography", specific brand reference |
| Movement is jerky | Duration too short for smooth motion | Increase duration, specify "smooth motion", "slow movement" |
| Color is wrong | No color specification | Add specific color words: "warm tones", "cool blue", "muted earth tones" |
| Aspect ratio stretched | Wrong ratio in prompt | Verify "9:16" or "16:9" explicitly in prompt |

## 8. A/B TESTING VIDEO VARIATIONS

```
Variation A: "POV: You just found..."

Variation C: "Don't make this mistake"
```

### Variable 2: Music
```
Same video with 3 different music tracks:
Track A: Trending TikTok sound
Track B: Upbeat energetic original music
Track C: Calm, sophisticated instrumental
→ Measure engagement metrics per version
```

### Variable 3: Duration
```
Same content, different lengths:
Version A: 15 seconds (hook + quick demo + CTA)
Version B: 30 seconds (more detail on demo)
Version C: 60 seconds (deep dive tutorial style)
→ Test which length has best completion rate on each platform
```

### Variable 4: CTA Type
```
Same video, different CTAs:
Version A: "Shop now" button
Version B: "Comment below to enter giveaway"
Version C: "Link in bio"
Version D: "Follow for more"
→ Track which CTA drives most conversions
```

### Variable 5: Color Grading
```
Same video, different color treatments:
Version A: Warm golden tones (cozy feel)
Version B: Cool blue tones (modern feel)
Version C: Vibrant saturated (trendy feel)
Version D: Muted natural (authentic feel)
→ Test which aesthetic resonates with audience
```

**A/B test framework:**

```
Step 1: Choose ONE variable to test

Step 5: Measure: CTR, conversion rate, cost per acquisition
Step 6: Scale the winner, test next variable
Step 7: Document learnings
```

- Example: Cafe morning routine video with skincare product

**Format 2: Before/After Transformation**
- Problem + immediate solution
- Split screen or quick cut
- Satisfying transformation (makeup, skincare, clothes)
- Fast-paced, satisfying music
- Example: Makeup transformation in 10 seconds

**Format 3: Duet/Reaction Format**
- Partner with micro-influencer
- Show reaction to product
- Authentic, unscripted feel
- High engagement (comments on reactions)
- Example: Friend reacting to new fashion item

**Format 4: Educational + Entertainment**
- Teach something useful related to product
- Funny/entertaining tone
- Build authority, not just selling
- Longer format (30-60s)
- Example: "5 makeup mistakes you're probably making"

**Format 5: Trending Audio + Product Hook**
- Use viral TikTok sound
- Adapt lyrics/rhythm to product
- Catchy hook mentioning product name
- High discoverability via sound
- Example: Famous Vietnamese rap, replace lyrics with brand name

**Format 6: Minigame/Participation**
- Ask audience to participate
- "Comment if you agree..."
- "Tag someone who needs this..."
- High engagement, viral potential
- Example: "Comment your favorite product, I'll send 3 gifts"

**Format 7: Behind-the-Scenes/Authenticity**
- Show real team, real production
- Making of product
- Founder's story
- Trust-building
- Example: Factory footage showing quality control

**Format 8: Meme/Humor Format**
- Funny scenario or skit
- Product solves funny problem
- Relatable humor for Gen Z
- Viral potential via shares
- Example: "When you run out of [product], be like..." (relatable struggle)

**Format 9: Comparison Format**
- "Expensive vs Cheap" product
- Side-by-side comparison
- Your product = best value
- High engagement from debate
- Example: Luxury brand vs [your brand] skincare quality

**Format 10: Testimonial/Social Proof**
- Real customer speaking (not actor)
- Authentic emotion
- Specific results (numbers if applicable)
- Short but powerful
- Example: "3 months using [product], this is my skin now" (before/after)
