# Chiến Lược Giá cho TMĐT Việt Nam

## 1. 10 Chiến Lược Giá TMĐT & Khi Nào Dùng

### 1.1 Cost-Plus Pricing (Giá vốn + Margin)

**Định nghĩa:**
Cộng chi phí sản xuất/mua hàng + Chi phí vận hành + Margin lợi nhuận

**Công thức:**
```
Giá bán = COGS + (COGS × Margin %)

Ví dụ:
├─ COGS áo: 80K (vốn hàng)
├─ Target margin: 100% (double up)
└─ Giá bán = 80K + (80K × 100%) = 160K

Công thức chi tiết:
Giá bán = [(COGS + Operating cost + Profit margin) / Markup ratio]

Áo ví dụ chi tiết:
├─ COGS (vốn hàng): 80K
├─ Platform fee (Shopee 5%): 8K (of giá)
├─ Shipping cost: 15K (shop pays)
├─ Packaging: 3K
├─ Marketing allocation: 10K (adspend chia đều)
├─ Customer support: 2K
├─ Warehouse & ops: 5K
│  = Total cost: 80K + 43K = 123K
│
├─ Margin target: 50K (để profit)
└─ **Giá bán = 123K + 50K = 173K** → Làm tròn 179K (charm pricing)
```

**Ưu điểm:**
- ✅ Simple, dễ tính
- ✅ Đảm bảo mỗi cái bán đều có lợi
- ✅ Dễ scale (Công thức lặp lại)

**Nhược điểm:**
- ❌ Không xem xét demand/competition
- ❌ Giá có thể cao/thấp so thị trường
- ❌ Không optimal cho revenue

**Khi dùng:**
- Sản phẩm mới (Chưa biết demand)
- B2B orders (Cần margin fixed)
- Wholesale/reseller (Standard margin)

**Việt Nam context:**
- Phổ biến nhất, 70% shop vừa & nhỏ dùng
- Margin target: 50-100% là chuẩn cho fashion
- Food/beverage: 30-50% margin

### 1.2 Competitive Pricing (Theo Đối Thủ)

**Định nghĩa:**
Đặt giá dựa trên giá của đối thủ (competitors)

**Quy trình:**
```
1. Xác định competitors chính (5-10 shops top)
   ├─ Trên Shopee
   ├─ Trên Lazada
   ├─ Trên các sàn khác
   └─ Direct competitors (Same product, same quality)

2. Monitor giá hàng tháng:
   ├─ Áo trắng M: Competitor A 289K, B 299K, C 279K, D 309K
   ├─ Giá trung bình: 294K
   ├─ Giá thấp nhất: 279K
   └─ Giá cao nhất: 309K

3. Chiến lược định giá:
   ├─ Premium positioning: Giá cao hơn 10% (Competitor A)
   │  └─ Giá = 294K × 1.1 = 323K (Phải có lý do: quality, service)
   │
   ├─ Market positioning: Giá ngang = 294K → 299K
   │  └─ Cạnh tranh fair, standard position
   │
   └─ Price leader: Giá thấp hơn 10% (Competitor C)
      └─ Giá = 279K - 5% = 265K (Chỉ nếu có lợi thế chi phí)
```

**Ví dụ thực tế:**
```
Monitor Shopee - Áo cotton trắng women, size M-L:
├─ Áo Fashion Hub: 299K (1,450 reviews, 4.9⭐) ← Top seller
├─ Áo Style Shop: 289K (850 reviews, 4.8⭐)
├─ Áo Classic Wear: 279K (620 reviews, 4.7⭐) ← Price leader
├─ Áo Premium Threads: 339K (420 reviews, 5⭐) ← Premium
├─ Your shop: ??? (TBD)

Analysis:
├─ Price range: 279K - 339K (60K spread)
├─ Most shops: 289-299K (Sweet spot)
├─ My recommendation: 289K (Ngang competitor B, undercut premium)

Why 289K not 279K?
├─ Giá 279K = Race to bottom, margin too thin
├─ Giá 289K = Still competitive, better margin
├─ Assume COGS 85K:
│  ├─ At 279K price: Profit only 194K (Margin 2.7K before fees)
│  └─ At 289K price: Profit 204K (Margin 12K after fees)
└─ Choose 289K!
```

**Ưu điểm:**
- ✅ Cập nhật thị trường
- ✅ Tránh undercut/overcharge
- ✅ Balanced positioning

**Nhược điểm:**
- ❌ Phải monitor thường xuyên (Chán)
- ❌ Race to bottom nếu không cẩn thận
- ❌ Competitors thay đổi, bạn phải thay theo

**Khi dùng:**
- Sản phẩm commoditized (Áo, quần, giày cơ bản)
- Thị trường mature (Có nhiều seller)
- Cạnh tranh cao

**Việt Nam context:**
- Phổ biến trên Shopee/Lazada
- Cần monitor hàng tuần
- Tools: Manual check + Sprout Social, Keepa, Jungle Scout

### 1.3 Value-Based Pricing (Giá theo Giá Trị Cảm Nhận)

**Định nghĩa:**
Giá dựa trên giá trị mà khách cảm nhận, không phải chi phí

**Ví dụ:**
```
Sản phẩm: Áo linen premium (Từ Italy, limited edition)
├─ COGS: 150K
├─ Competitor price: 400K (linen thường)
├─ Value factors:
│  ├─ Material: Imported from Italy (Cảm giác cao cấp)
│  ├─ Scarcity: Limited 100 pieces/month
│  ├─ Brand: Collaborates with famous designer
│  ├─ Story: "Sustainable, organic farming"
│  └─ Celebrity endorsement: [Actress name] wore it
│
├─ Perceived value: +300% (Khách sẵn sàng trả gấp 3-4x)
└─ **Giá bán = 700K** (Cao hơn competitor 75%!)

Tại sao người mua ngồi yên?
├─ "Premium quality" vs "basic quality"
├─ Tính "scarcity" (Nếu hết, khôn có nữa)
├─ Story/Brand value (Không chỉ áo, mà là lifestyle)
└─ Willingness to pay (Khách giàu sẵn sàng)
```

**Implement strategy:**
```
1. Identify value drivers:
   ├─ What makes customers willing to pay more?
   ├─ Quality? Brand? Story? Exclusivity?
   └─ Service? Warranty? Community?

2. Communicate value (CRITICAL):
   ├─ Tell the story (Where materials come from)
   ├─ Show the quality (Photos, videos, unboxing)
   ├─ Create exclusivity (Limited, numbered edition)
   ├─ Build brand (Logo, tagline, identity)
   └─ Celebrity/influencer endorsement (Social proof)

3. Price accordingly:
   ├─ Premium product → Premium price
   ├─ Average product + Good story → Medium-premium price
   └─ Basic product → Commodity price

Example Vietnamese brand "Aha Wear":
├─ Áo linen cơ bản: 350K (commodity)
├─ Same áo, "Ethically sourced, woman-owned": 450K (+28%)
├─ Same áo, "Collab with [Actress]": 550K (+57%)
└─ Same áo, "Limited 50 pieces, designed in Paris": 750K (+114%)

Result: Same product, 4 price points, different customer segments
├─ Segment A (Budget): 350K → Volume 500/month
├─ Segment B (Quality-conscious): 450K → Volume 200/month
├─ Segment C (Trend-followers): 550K → Volume 150/month
├─ Segment D (Premium/Exclusive): 750K → Volume 50/month
└─ Total revenue: (500×350 + 200×450 + 150×550 + 50×750) = 322.5M ✅
    (If priced at 350K for all: Only 152.5M)
```

**Ưu điểm:**
- ✅ Highest profit margin potential
- ✅ Less price competition
- ✅ Premium positioning

**Nhược điểm:**
- ❌ Cần brand/story/quality thực sự
- ❌ Khó scale (Limited volume)
- ❌ Market smaller (Premium tier)

**Khi dùng:**
- Premium/luxury products
- Unique/branded products
- Products with story/differentiation

**Việt Nam context:**
- Ngày càng phổ biến
- Brand local đang grow (Ngôn từ, story)
- Niche markets (Eco-conscious, sustainable, local)

### 1.5 Bundle Pricing (Combo Giảm Giá)

**Định nghĩa:**
Bán nhiều sản phẩm cùng lúc với discount

**Ví dụ:**
```
Individual pricing:
├─ Áo trắng: 299K
├─ Quần kem: 349K
├─ Belt: 99K
└─ Total if mua riêng: 747K

Bundle options:
├─ Bundle 2 (Áo + Quần): 599K (save 49K, -6.5%)
│  └─ Khách cảm giác: Deal, but not huge
│
├─ Bundle 3 (Áo + Quần + Belt): 799K (save 48K, -6.4%)
│  └─ Khách cảm giác: Combo outfit, convenient
│
├─ Bundle 3 Better (Áo + Quần + Belt): 749K (save 98K, -13%)
│  └─ Khách cảm giác: Real savings, TAKE IT!
│     └─ You still make profit if COGS low enough
│
└─ Flash bundle (Limited, 2 giờ): 599K for all 3 (save 248K, -33%)
   └─ Khách sẽ chốt ngay vì urgency + big discount
```

**Khi nào dùng:**
- ✅ Raise AOV (Average order value)
- ✅ Clear slow-moving inventory
- ✅ Cross-sell complementary products
- ✅ Increase customer lifetime value (Customer buys more)

**Ví dụ thực tế:**
```
Fashion bundle trên Lazada:
├─ "Spring Outfit Complete": Áo + Quần + Phụ kiện = 799K
│  └─ Individual total: 900K (save 11%)
│  └─ Margin: Still 35% (Do volume)
│
├─ "Date Night Bundle": Váy + Giày + Bag = 999K
│  └─ Individual total: 1.2M (save 17%)
│  └─ Margin: 32%
│
└─ "Wardrobe Starter" (3 áo + 2 quần): 1.39M
   └─ Individual: 1.7M (Save 18%)
   └─ Margin: 30%
```

**Formula:**
```
Bundle discount % = (Margin loss from discount) / (Additional AOV gain)

Example:
Normal AOV per order: 1M (3 items on average)
├─ Margin: 350K (35%)
│
Bundle offer: Buy 3+ items, save 10%
├─ Expected: AOV increases from 1M → 1.5M (50% boost)
├─ Discount cost: 1.5M × 10% = 150K
├─ Additional margin from 0.5M extra items: 0.5M × 35% = 175K
├─ Net gain: 175K - 150K = 25K ✅ (Worth it!)
│
Total margin now: 350K + 175K - 150K = 375K (Better!)
└─ Do: Implement bundles
```

**Ưu điểm:**
- ✅ Increase AOV
- ✅ Clear inventory
- ✅ Train customer to buy multiple items
- ✅ Higher total margin (Even with discount)

**Nhược điểm:**
- ❌ Perception: "Bundle" hạ giá → Can affect premium positioning
- ❌ May cannibalize full-price sales
- ❌ Margin might drop if discount too high

**Việt Nam context:**
- Siêu phổ biến trên Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop
- Khách Việt rất thích combo (Tâm lý: "May mắn", tiết kiệm)
- Flash bundle (limited time) perform tốt

### 1.6 Tiered Pricing (Mua Nhiều Giảm Nhiều)

**Định nghĩa:**
Giá giảm dần nếu khách mua số lượng lớn

**Bảng giá tiered:**
```
Sản phẩm: Áo cotton (Wholesale/B2B hoặc high volume)

Tier 1: 1-10 cái = 150K/cái
Tier 2: 11-20 cái = 140K/cái (6.7% discount)
Tier 3: 21-50 cái = 130K/cái (13.3% discount)
Tier 4: 51-100 cái = 120K/cái (20% discount)
Tier 5: 101+ cái = 110K/cái (26.7% discount)

Customer examples:
├─ Khách A: Mua 5 cái = 5 × 150K = 750K
├─ Khách B: Mua 15 cái = 15 × 140K = 2.1M (Saves 150K vs tier 1)
├─ Khách C: Mua 100 cái = 100 × 120K = 12M (Saves 3M vs tier 1)
└─ Large order D: Mua 200 cái = 200 × 110K = 22M (Saves 8M)
```

**Margin still works:**
```
Assuming COGS = 70K/áo:

Tier 1: 150K - 70K = 80K profit (53%)
Tier 2: 140K - 70K = 70K profit (50%)
Tier 3: 130K - 70K = 60K profit (46%)
Tier 4: 120K - 70K = 50K profit (42%)
Tier 5: 110K - 70K = 40K profit (36%)

All tiers are profitable! Margin decreases nhưng volume increases.
```

**Khi nào dùng:**
- ✅ B2B/Wholesale (Standard)
- ✅ Dropshipper/Reseller
- ✅ High-volume retailers
- ✅ Staff/employee discounts
- ❌ B2C (Not recommended, confusing)

**Việt Nam context:**
- Phổ biến cho B2B, ít dùng B2C
- Dropshipper rất thích (Để lợi nhuận của họ)
- "Giá sỉ" là standard tại các chợ truyền thống

### 1.7 Dynamic Pricing (Giá Thay Đổi)

**Định nghĩa:**
Giá tự động thay đổi dựa trên demand, supply, time, customer segment

**Ví dụ:**
```
Sản phẩm: Váy hoa (Seasonal, high demand)

Pricing calendar:
├─ Off-season (Dec-Mar): 299K (Stock high, demand low)
├─ Shoulder season (Apr, Oct): 349K (Normal demand)
├─ Peak season (May-Sep): 399K (High demand, hot trend)
├─ Holiday (Tet, 8/3, Christmas): 449K (Maximum demand, scarcity)
└─ End of season clearance (Oct): 179K (Clear old stock)

By inventory level:
├─ Stock > 100: 349K (Plenty)
├─ Stock 50-100: 349K (Normal)
├─ Stock 20-50: 379K (Warn, getting low)
├─ Stock < 20: 399K (Scarce, urgency)
└─ Stock < 5: 449K (LAST PIECES!)

By time of day (Seasonality):
├─ Morning (6-10am): 349K (Normal, commute shoppers)
├─ Lunch (11am-2pm): 329K (Discount for impulse)
├─ Afternoon (3-6pm): 349K (Normal)
├─ Evening (7-11pm): 369K (Premium, night owl shoppers, high intent)

By customer segment (Personalization):
├─ New customer: 319K (Acquisition discount)
├─ Repeat customer: 349K (Standard)
├─ VIP member (5+ purchases): 299K (Loyalty reward)
├─ About to churn (60+ days): 279K (Win-back)

By device:
├─ Desktop: 349K (Normal, serious buyers)
├─ Mobile: 329K (Lower friction, more impulse)
└─ App: 319K (App users tend to convert better)
```

**Implementation tools:**
```
Simple: Manual pricing updates (Weekly)
├─ Monitor demand
├─ Check competitor
├─ Update pricing manually
└─ Zeit: 30 min/week

Advanced: Automation rules
├─ Use Shopee/Lazada rules (If stock < X, then price = Y)
├─ Use chatbot trigger ("Stock low! Price goes up tomorrow")
├─ Use tools: Sellfy, BigCommerce, Shopify (Dynamic pricing apps)

Most advanced: AI/Machine learning
├─ Tools: Amazon pricing engine (For big companies)
├─ Or: Price optimization SaaS (Prisync, Competera)
└─ Not practical for small VN shop (Cost vs benefit)
```

**Ưu điểm:**
- ✅ Maximize revenue (Sell high when demand high)
- ✅ Clear inventory (Sell low when stock high)
- ✅ Responsive to market
- ✅ Data-driven

**Nhược điểm:**
- ❌ Complex to manage (Many variables)
- ❌ Customer backlash (If see price drop after buy)
- ❌ Needs monitoring (Manual labor or expensive tools)
- ❌ Antitrust risk (In some markets, illegal to manipulate price)

**Việt Nam context:**
- Limited use, some shops do manually
- Shopee/Lazada allow price changes anytime
- TikTok Shop allows dynamic pricing
- Tool: Pancake, ManyChat support price rules

### 1.8 Penetration Pricing (Giá Thấp để Chiếm Thị Phần)

**Định nghĩa:**
Giá cực thấp ban đầu để:
1. Nhanh chóng chiếm thị phần
2. Làm khó competitors
3. Xây dựng customer base lớn

**Ví dụ:**
```
Scenario: Bạn launch sản phẩm mới "Áo linen sang xịn"

Option A: Premium pricing
├─ Giá: 599K
├─ Margin: 60%
├─ Expected sales: 50 cái/tháng
└─ Revenue: 29.95M/tháng

Option B: Penetration pricing
├─ Giá: 299K (50% discount, loss leader)
├─ Margin: 10% (Kiếm ít, focus on volume)
├─ Expected sales: 500 cái/tháng (10x volume!)
├─ Revenue: 149.5M/tháng
├─ Loss on each unit: Acceptable because volume cover
└─ Lifetime value: High (Customers become repeat)

Result:
├─ After 6 months penetration, 3000 customers acquired (loyal base)
├─ Raise price to 449K (Still cheaper than original 599K)
├─ Same 500 units sell at higher price
├─ Revenue now: 224.5M/tháng (Better than both options!)
└─ Profit: Now profitable due to volume + existing customer base
```

**Khi dùng:**
- ✅ New market entry
- ✅ New product launch
- ✅ Category education (Teach market that product exists)
- ✅ Niche/Premium category (Willing to sacrifice margin for volume)

**Việt Nam context:**
- Tech companies use (Tiktok launched with free gifts)
- Fashion brands: Less common (Sợ mất value)
- Food delivery: Aggressive (Grab, Shopeepay discount everything)

### 1.9 Premium Pricing (Giá Cao = Chất Lượng Cao)

**Định nghĩa:**
Set giá cao để signal "premium quality"

**Ví dụ:**
```
Market context: Áo cotton simple

Tier 1 - Cheap (50K): Basic, no brand, maybe defect
Tier 2 - Medium (199K): Standard quality, basic brand
Tier 3 - Premium (449K): "Premium imported cotton, ethical production"
Tier 4 - Luxury (699K): "Designer collaboration, limited edition"

Giả sử all 3 áo quality tương tự.
Nhưng Tier 3 expensive hơn 125%!

Why customers buy Tier 3:
├─ Signal: "I can afford 449K áo" (Status)
├─ Quality assumption: "Must be better if expensive"
├─ Scarcity: "Limited, exclusive" (Psychological)
├─ Brand: "Premium brand, I trust them"

Margin difference:
├─ Tier 1: 50K price, 20K COGS = 30K profit (60%)
├─ Tier 2: 199K price, 60K COGS = 139K profit (70%)
├─ Tier 3: 449K price, 100K COGS = 349K profit (78%)
└─ Tier 4: 699K price, 150K COGS = 549K profit (79%)
```

**Psychology at work:**
```
Price = Quality perception (In mind)

Most people believe:
├─ "If giá cao, quality phải tốt"
├─ "If giá quá thấp, có gì đó sai sai"
└─ "Mid-range price = safe bet"

Danger zone:
├─ Price too low (Khách ngờ quality)
├─ Price too high (Khách không mua despite good quality)
└─ Price inconsistent (Why cheaper yesterday?)
```

**Khi dùng:**
- ✅ Premium/luxury brands (Established)
- ✅ High trust (Lots of reviews, brand authority)
- ✅ Differentiated product (Clearly better)
- ✅ High margin business model

**Việt Nam context:**
- Growing luxury market
- Status-conscious consumers
- Premium positioning works if backed by:
  - Good brand story
  - High review rating
  - Celebrity endorsement
  - Beautiful packaging/presentation

### 1.10 Freemium/Loss Leader (Bán Lỗ SP Chủ Lực)

**Định nghĩa:**
Bán 1 sản phẩm/service với giá loss (hoặc free) để:
1. Attract customers
2. Upsell products with high margin
3. Build customer base

**Ví dụ:**
```
Scenario: Fashion shop selling áo + accessories

Strategy:
├─ "Hero product" (Loss leader): Áo basic = 149K
│  ├─ COGS: 70K
│  ├─ Margin: -3% (Bán lỗ!)
│  ├─ Purpose: Get customers in the door
│  └─ Expected: 80% buyers buy áo
│
├─ "Upsell products" (High margin): Accessories
│  ├─ Belt: 99K (COGS 20K, Margin 80%)
│  ├─ Necklace: 149K (COGS 30K, Margin 80%)
│  ├─ Purse: 299K (COGS 80K, Margin 73%)
│  └─ Expected: 40% of áo buyers also buy accessories
│
└─ Unit economics:
   ├─ 100 customers buy áo: Cost 100×(-3K) = -300K
   ├─ 40 customers also buy accessories: Profit 40 × 100K avg = 4M
   └─ Net profit: 4M - 300K = 3.7M (8.2% margin overall) ✅
```

**Ví dụ khác - Fast food:**
```
KFC marketing:
├─ Loss leader: Combo rice cheap 59K (vs make separately 89K)
│  └─ Purpose: Get people in, buy drinks (high margin)
│
├─ Upsell: Drinks & dessert
│  ├─ Pepsi: 29K (COGS 8K, margin 72%)
│  ├─ Dessert: 35K (COGS 10K, margin 71%)
│  └─ 70% combo buyers add drink
│
└─ Result:
   ├─ Combo alone: Loss
   ├─ Combo + drink: Breakeven
   └─ Combo + drink + dessert: Profitable!
```

**Khi dùng:**
- ✅ High-margin complementary products exist
- ✅ Subscription model (Hook, then retain)
- ✅ Customer acquisition (Frontload loss, profit from LTV)
- ✅ Category education (Bring market in, then sell premium)

**Việt Nam context:**
- Retail chains use (Comfort đầu, tối ưu hóa later)
- E-commerce less common (Hard to control margin)
- Flash sale usage (Borrow concept)

## 2. Pricing Psychology Cho Người Việt

### 2.1 Charm Pricing (Giá Lẻ)

**Định nghĩa:**
Áp dụng giá lẻ (x99, x49, x9) thay vì số tròn

**Ví dụ:**
```
So sánh:
├─ 200K vs 199K → 199K sells 20% more (psychological)
├─ 300K vs 299K → 299K sells 15% more
├─ 500K vs 499K → 499K sells 10% more
├─ 1M vs 999K → 999K sells 8% more
```

**Tại sao?**
```
Brain psychology:
├─ 199K được đọc là "một trăm chín mươi..." (reads like 100K)
├─ 200K được đọc rõ "hai trăm" (sounds like 200K)
├─ Khách tâm lý: "199K cheap hơn" dù chỉ khác 1K

Evolutionary reason:
├─ Before calculator era, left digit (1 vs 2) more salient
├─ Brains evolved to process left-to-right
├─ So 199 "feels" like 100, not 200
└─ This bias still exists today!
```

**Implement:**
```
DON'T do:
├─ 200K (Tròn, nhàm chán)
├─ 250K (Phân nửa, cũng nhàm)
└─ 300K

DO this:
├─ 199K ← Default, most common
├─ 249K (Nếu want 250K ballpark)
├─ 299K

### 2.2 Anchoring (Hiển Thị Giá Gốc Gạch Ngang)

**Định nghĩa:**
Show original price (gạch ngang) cạnh sale price để create perception of value

**Ví dụ:**
```
Psychology at work:
├─ "299K" → "Just a price"
├─ "~~499K~~ 299K" → "SAVE 200K! Great deal!"
│
└─ Same price, but 2nd version sells 40% more!
```

**How anchoring works:**
```
Theory: Brain compares to anchor (First number seen)

├─ See 499K first (Anchor in mind)
├─ See 299K second (Discounted)
├─ Brain math: 499 - 299 = 200 saved, so "good deal"

If only showed 299K:
├─ No anchor
├─ Brain: "Is 299K expensive? Don't know..."
└─ Result: Hesitation to buy
```

**Implementation:**
```
BEST PRACTICE for VN:

Product: Áo cotton
├─ Regular price (no promotion): 349K (Show normally)
├─ During sale: ~~449K~~ 299K (Show with strike-through)
│  └─ Save: 150K (33% discount) ← Put this prominently!
│
├─ Flash sale: ~~499K~~ 199K (Show with strike-through)
│  └─ Save: 300K (60% discount!)
│
└─ Don't: ~~399K~~ 299K if regular price is 349K
   └─ Fake anchor = Lose trust when caught
```

**Anchor rules:**
```
✅ Legitimate anchors:
├─ Original MSRP (Manufacturer suggested price)
├─ Your previous price (If you raised, then sale)
├─ Competitor price (If document-able)
└─ List price before sale started

❌ Fake anchors (Don't do!):
├─ Showing price that was never real
├─ Showing price from fake competitor
├─ Exaggerated savings
└─ Bait and switch

Legal in VN?
├─ Somewhat gray area
├─ Better safe: Use real anchors
└─ Perception matters (Better for brand)
```

**Anchor strength:**
```
Weak anchor: Only 5% difference (~~350K~~ 299K)
├─ Save: 51K (14%)
├─ Khách: "Meh, not wow"

Medium anchor: 15-25% difference (~~399K~~ 299K)
├─ Save: 100K (25%)
├─ Khách: "Good deal"

Strong anchor: 30-50% difference (~~499K~~ 299K)
├─ Save: 200K (40%)
├─ Khách: "WOW, must buy!"

Too strong anchor: >50% (~~799K~~ 299K)
├─ Khách: "Something's wrong... (sus)"
└─ Perception: Fake discount, lose trust
```

### 2.3 Decoy Effect (3 Gói: Small/Medium/Large)

Offer 3 price tiers, middle one is "trap" to push customer to highest tier

├─ Price: 599K (2x price for 3x value, feels expensive)
├─ Value: Medium
├─ Margin: 120%
└─ Few people buy this (It's the trap!)

Option C (Real target): 12 months
├─ Price: 999K (1.67x price for 12x value, BEST DEAL)
├─ Value: Highest
├─ Margin: 150%
└─ Most people buy this (Decoy made it look good!)

Math:
├─ Without Option B: 30% buy A, 70% buy C
├─ With Option B (decoy): 5% buy A, 20% buy B, 75% buy C
│  └─ Option C % increased! (Decoy made it more attractive)
```

**How it works:**

Pricing tiers psychology:

Without middle option:

├─ Option 3: 999K (Premium, best value per unit) ← Customers gravitate here!
│ └─ Option 2 makes Option 3 look great by comparison
```

## 4. Margin Calculation & Break-Even Analysis

### 4.1 Product Margin Formula

**Simple version:**
```
Margin = Revenue - COGS - Direct costs

Example (Áo cotton):
├─ Selling price: 299K (Shopee)
├─ COGS: 80K (vốn hàng)
├─ Platform fee (5%): 15K
├─ Shipping (shop pays): 20K
├─ Packaging: 3K
│
├─ Total costs: 80 + 15 + 20 + 3 = 118K
├─ Margin per unit: 299 - 118 = 181K
├─ Margin %: 181/299 = 60.5%
```

**Advanced version (With operating costs):**
```
Revenue per unit: 299K
│
├─ MINUS: Variable costs
│  ├─ COGS: -80K
│  ├─ Platform fee: -15K
│  ├─ Shipping: -20K
│  ├─ Packaging: -3K
│  ├─ Payment processing: -2K (0.7%)
│  └─ = Subtotal variable: 120K
│
├─ Contribution margin: 299 - 120 = 179K
│
├─ MINUS: Allocated operating costs
│  ├─ Customer support (2%): -6K
│  ├─ Marketing (allocated): -10K (Divide total ad spend by # units)
│  ├─ Warehouse/ops (2%): -6K
│  └─ = Subtotal operating: 22K
│
└─ **Net margin: 179 - 22 = 157K (52.5%)**
```

### 4.2 Break-Even Analysis

**Definition:**
At what price/volume do you make 0 profit?

**Formula:**
```
Break-even price = Total fixed costs / Units sold + Variable cost per unit

Example:
├─ Monthly fixed costs: 50M (Rent, salaries, etc)
├─ Variable cost per unit: 120K
├─ Expected monthly units: 300
│
├─ Fixed cost per unit: 50M / 300 = 167K
├─ Break-even price: 167 + 120 = 287K
│
└─ At 287K, 300 units: Zero profit
   ├─ Above 287K: Profit
   └─ Below 287K: Loss
```

**Practical break-even table:**
```
Variable cost per unit: 120K
Fixed costs: 50M/month

Price | Units/month | Revenue | Total variable | Profit/(Loss) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
279K | 300 | 83.7M | 36M | -2.3M ❌
287K | 300 | 86.1M | 36M | 0M ⚠️
299K | 300 | 89.7M | 36M | +3.7M ✅
299K | 250 | 74.75M | 30M | -5.25M ❌
299K | 350 | 104.65M | 42M | +12.65M ✅

→ Price 299K, volume 300-350 units = healthy profit
```

### 4.3 Minimum Viable Price (MVP)

**Definition:**
Lowest price you can sell at to stay in business

**Formula:**
```
MVP = (Fixed costs / Expected annual units) + Variable cost per unit

Example:
├─ Annual fixed costs: 600M
├─ Expected annual units: 5,000 (300×12 or ~10/day)
├─ Variable cost: 120K
│
├─ MVP = (600M / 5000) + 120K = 120K + 120K = 240K
│
└─ At 240K, you make 0 profit
   (All revenue = Fixed + Variable costs)
```

**Safety margin:**
```
Don't sell at MVP!

Instead: Sell at (MVP × 1.5) to (MVP × 2)
├─ Reason: Unexpected costs, inflation, bad months
├─ Example: MVP 240K → Sell at 360-480K
│
└─ This gives you 50-100% margin of safety
```

### 4.4 Price Elasticity (When to Discount)

**Definition:**
How much sales volume increases when price drops?

**Formula:**
```
Elasticity = (% change in quantity) / (% change in price)

Example:
├─ Current: 299K, selling 100 units/month
├─ Test: Drop to 279K (-6.7% price), sell 120 units/month (+20% volume)
│
├─ Elasticity = 20% / 6.7% = 3.0
│  └─ Elastic demand (Volume sensitive to price)
│
├─ Rule:
│  ├─ Elasticity > 1: Price sensitive (Cut price helps revenue)
│  ├─ Elasticity < 1: Not price sensitive (Keep price high)
│  └─ Your case: 3.0 is highly elastic!
│
└─ Recommendation: 6.7% price cut brings 20% more volume = Higher total revenue!
   ├─ Revenue before: 100 × 299K = 29.9M
   └─ Revenue after: 120 × 279K = 33.48M (+12% revenue!) ✅
```

## 7. A/B Testing Pricing

### 7.1 Methodology

```
Goal: Find optimal price

Hypothesis: "299K price will outsell 349K"

Setup:
├─ Duration: 2 weeks minimum (To cover weekly variations)
├─ Sample size: 100+ units per variant (Statistical significance)
├─ Variables:
│  ├─ Product: Same
│  ├─ Listing: Same (Except price)
│  ├─ Audience: Same traffic source
│  └─ Promotion: Same (Unless testing promotion impact)
│
├─ Tracking:
│  ├─ Units sold per price
│  ├─ Revenue per price
│  ├─ Conversion rate per price
│  └─ Customer feedback
│
└─ Analysis:
   ├─ Winning price = Highest revenue (Not just highest volume)
   ├─ Calculate confidence level (90%+ before declare winner)
   └─ Implement winner, repeat next SKU
```

### 7.2 Pricing Test Example

```
Product: Áo cotton (SKU)
Current price: 299K
Test variant: 349K (17% increase)

Test period: April 1-14, 2025

Results:

Price | Units sold | Revenue | CVR | Customer feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
299K (control) | 150 | 44.85M | 1.5% | Happy, recommend |
349K (variant) | 110 | 38.39M | 1.1% | Hesitation on price |

Analysis:
├─ 299K: Higher volume, higher revenue ✓ WINNER
├─ 349K: Lower volume, lower revenue
├─ Verdict: 299K is optimal for this SKU
│
├─ Statistical significance:
│  ├─ 150 vs 110 (28% difference)
│  ├─ Sample size: 260 total (Good)
│  ├─ Confidence: 95%+ (Strong)
│  └─ Conclusion: Difference is real, not luck
│
└─ Action: Lock 299K for this SKU, test other variants
```
