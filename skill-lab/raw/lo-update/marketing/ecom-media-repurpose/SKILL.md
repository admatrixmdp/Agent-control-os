# Tái Sử Dụng Content: Từ 1 Thành 20+

## PHẦN 1: CONTENT REPURPOSING MATRIX

### 1.1 TỪ 1 VIDEO GỐC (3-5 PHÚT) TẠO RA 15-20+ CONTENT PIECES

#### VIDEO GỐC SPECIFICATIONS
```
Duration: 3-5 minutes (ideal)
- Not too short: Enough variety to extract clips
- Not too long: Won't overwhelm editing time
- Sweet spot: 4 minutes = ~240 seconds

Format: MP4, H.264 codec, 1080p minimum
- High resolution allows flexibility
- Can resize down for any platform
- Audio clear (critical for transcription)

Content: Educational, entertaining, or product-focused
- Clear narrative arc: Start → Middle → End
- Multiple camera angles (helps with clips)
- Natural cuts/transitions (easier to segment)

EXAMPLE VIDEO: "5 Tips for Skincare in Summer" (4 minutes)
- Intro (15s): Problem statement
- Tips 1-5: ~40 seconds each
- Outro (20s): CTA + brand

This structure: Perfect for repurposing
```

---

#### REPURPOSING OUTPUT: COMPLETE LIST

```
FROM 1 VIDEO (4 minutes) CREATE:

1. SHORT-FORM VIDEO CLIPS (for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts)
   • 3-5 individual clips: 15-60 seconds each
   • Each clip = 1 tip or key moment
   • Total: 5 clips × 30 seconds = 150 seconds
   • Platform: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Zalo Story

2. YOUTUBE SHORTS / VERTICAL VIDEO
   • 1 best-moment edit: 45-60 seconds
   • Highest-impact segment from original
   • Vertical format (1080x1920)

3. STATIC IMAGES WITH QUOTES
   • Extract 5-10 key phrases from video
   • Create quote cards: 1080x1080 (Instagram)
   • Design: Text overlay on image from video
   • Purpose: IG feed, Facebook, Pinterest

4. CAROUSEL SOCIAL MEDIA POST
   • Convert video into 5-8 slides
   • Slide 1: Headline / hook
   • Slides 2-6: Each tip (text + icon)
   • Slide 7: Before/after or result
   • Slide 8: CTA
   • Platform: Instagram carousel, Facebook carousel, LinkedIn

5. BLOG POST / ARTICLE
   • Transcribe video dialogue
   • Expand with additional research
   • Add photos from video scenes
   • Add video embed
   • Result: 800-1200 word blog post
   • SEO keywords: Target keywords from video

6. STORY POSTS (Instagram, Facebook)
   • 3-5 individual stories: 5-15 seconds each
   • Behind-the-scenes from filming
   • Quotes or tips (1 per story)
   • Build suspense: "Wait for the tip..."
   • Interactive: Poll, question sticker

7. EMAIL NEWSLETTER SEQUENCE
   • Email 1: Teaser + link to video (1st tip)
   • Email 2: Summary of all 5 tips
   • Email 3: Deep dive into most popular tip
   • Email 4: CTA (product/course related to topic)
   • Spacing: Send over 1-2 weeks

8. CAPTIONS / SOCIAL MEDIA POSTS (Multiple Angles)
   • Post 1: Focus on tip #1 + link
   • Post 2: Focus on tip #3 (different demographic)
   • Post 3: Emotional angle ("Why tip #2 changed my life")
   • Post 4: Question angle ("Which tip surprised you?")
   • Spacing: Spread across 1-2 weeks

9. INFOGRAPHIC / VISUAL SUMMARY
   • Compile all 5 tips into 1 visual
   • Icons + text + colors
   • 1080x1350 or 1200x1600 (Pinterest optimal)
   • Format: Vertical, scrollable on mobile

10. PODCAST / AUDIO CONTENT
   • Extract audio from video
   • Remove background music (if needed)
   • Create audio clip: 3-5 minutes
   • Upload to: Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube Music
   • Add podcast cover art (3000x3000 for Spotify)

11. 5+ THUMBNAIL VARIATIONS
   • Create unique thumbnails for:
     - YouTube thumbnail (custom)
     - TikTok thumbnail (auto or custom)
     - Email thumbnail
     - Blog post thumbnail
     - Pinterest pin thumbnail
   • Each optimized for platform specs

12. LONG-FORM WRITTEN PIECE / THREAD
   • Twitter/X thread: 20+ tweets (1 tip per tweet)
   • LinkedIn article: 1000+ word deep dive
   • Medium article: Essay format
   • Purpose: Reach different audience segments

═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TOTAL OUTPUT: 1 video → 25+ CONTENT PIECES
Time to create: 5-7 hours (including AI assist)
Content lifespan: 3-6 months (across all platforms)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

### 1.2 TỪ 1 BÀI BLOG TẠO RA 15+ CONTENT PIECES

#### BLOG POST SPECIFICATIONS
```
Word count: 1000-2000 words (ideal)
Structure:
  - Headline / Intro (150 words)
  - Section 1: Main topic (300 words)
  - Section 2: Main topic (300 words)
  - Section 3: Main topic (300 words)
  - Conclusion / Wrap-up (150 words)

Visual elements:
  - Feature image (1200x630)
  - 3-5 in-text images
  - Bullet points
  - Tables (if data-heavy)

SEO: Optimized for target keyword

EXAMPLE: "The Complete Guide to Hair Care for Curly Hair" (1500 words)
Sections:
  1. Understanding curly hair types
  2. Best products for curly hair
  3. Styling techniques
  4. Common mistakes to avoid
  5. Maintenance routine
```

---

#### REPURPOSING OUTPUT

```
FROM 1 BLOG POST (1500 words) CREATE:

1. SOCIAL MEDIA POSTS (5-10 individual posts)
   • Post 1: Main headline + link

3. CAROUSEL POST (Instagram)

4. VIDEO SCRIPT

   • Key points: Narrate main sections
   • Visuals: Pair script with relevant images
   • Shoot/edit into final video

5. EMAIL NEWSLETTER SEQUENCE
   • Email 1: Intro + "Here's what you'll learn..."
   • Email 2: Section 1 content
   • Email 3: Section 2 content

8. PODCAST EPISODE SCRIPT
   • Convert blog into podcast format: 30-40 minutes
   • Add interviews / expert quotes (optional)
   • Include tangents and personal stories

9. SLIDE PRESENTATION (Google Slides / Canva)
   • Convert blog into 15-20 slides
   • Title slide + content slides + conclusion
   • Use for: Webinar, workshop, email course

10. SHORT-FORM CONTENT (TikTok, Reels)
   • Create 5+ clips: 15-30 seconds each
   • Hook: Surprising fact from blog
   • Content: Key point from blog section
   • CTA: "Read full article in bio"

11. PINTEREST PIN COLLECTION
   • Create 5-10 different pin designs
   • Same content, different text/visual
   • Optimal size: 1000x1500
   • Each pin links back to blog

   • Use to build email list
```

### 1.3 TỪ 1 LIVESTREAM (2 GIỜ) TẠO RA 20-30+ CONTENT PIECES

#### LIVESTREAM SPECIFICATIONS
```
Duration: 1.5-2 hours (ideal)
Format: Facebook Live, Instagram Live, YouTube Live, or TikTok Live
Content: Interviews, Q&A, demo, product launch, training, AMA
Interactive: Comments, polls, donations, shout-outs
Audience: 50-500 people live

EXAMPLE: "Live Q&A with Beauty Expert" (90 minutes)
  - Intro: 5 minutes
  - Segment 1 (Beauty routine): 20 minutes
  - Segment 2 (Product recommendations): 20 minutes
  - Segment 3 (Q&A from audience): 30 minutes
  - Segment 4 (Giveaway announcement): 10 minutes
  - Outro: 5 minutes
```

## PHẦN 2: WORKFLOW REPURPOSE BẰNG AI

### 2.1 STEP-BY-STEP AI-ASSISTED WORKFLOW

#### BƯỚC 1: TRANSCRIBE VIDEO / AUDIO → TEXT

```
TOOL OPTIONS:

A. WHISPER (OpenAI - Free)
   - Upload video/audio
   - Auto-transcribe (accuracy: 95%+)
   - Output: TXT file
   - Vietnamese support: YES
   - Cost: Free (if self-hosted) or API pay-per-use
   - Time: 1 minute for 3-minute video

B. GEMINI / CLAUDE (AI models)
   - Upload video transcript (manual or via Whisper)
   - Ask: "Summarize this 3-minute video transcript"
   - Output: 200-500 word summary
   - Vietnamese support: Excellent
   - Cost: Free tier available

C. PROFESSIONAL TRANSCRIPTION SERVICE
   - Rev, Otter, Descript
   - Accuracy: 99%
   - Cost: $0.25-1 per minute
   - Time: 1-2 days
   - Best for: High-stakes content

WORKFLOW:
1. Upload video to Whisper / Otter
2. Get transcription (auto-corrected)
3. Manual proofread (5-10 minutes)
4. Save as TXT file
5. Feed to AI for next steps
```

```
3. Email 3 (Content): Share tip #3 + tip #4 (150 words)

"From the transcript, create 8 different social media posts.

```

## PHẦN 4: PLATFORM ADAPTATION RULES

### 4.1 TIKTOK - ADAPTATION GUIDE

```
TIKTOK BEST PRACTICES:

Format:
  - Vertical (9:16 or full screen)
  - 15-60 seconds optimal (can do up to 10 mins)
  - High energy, fast-paced

Text overlay:
  - Max 5-10 words per scene
  - Bold, readable font
  - Position: Top or bottom (not covering face/action)

Audio:
  - CRITICAL: Use trending sounds/music
  - Lip-sync to audio (if applicable)
  - Original audio OK if entertaining/educational

Hook (First 3 seconds):
  - Shocking statement
  - Question ("Did you know...")
  - Visual movement/change

Editing:
  - Fast cuts (not slow transitions)
  - Trending effects + transitions
  - Caption every scene (for silent viewing)

Call-to-action:
  - Implicit: "Follow for more tips"
  - Explicit: "Link in bio"
  - Engagement: "Comment your question"

Captions/Hashtags:
  - Use 5-10 relevant hashtags (#skincare #tips #summer)
  - Niche hashtags (10K-100K views)
  - Trending hashtags (1M+ views)

Algorithm boost:
  - Post consistent time (3-7 days per week)
  - Encourage shares > favorites
  - Comments = engagement boost
  - Watch time = key metric

ADAPTATION FROM BLOG → TIKTOK:
  Original (1500 words) → TikTok (30-45 seconds)

  Compress: Each tip = 1 TikTok (3-5 tips = 3-5 TikToks)
  Eliminate: Backstory, details, nuance
  Emphasize: Hook, curiosity gap, call-to-action
  Add: Trending sound, captions, energy
```

---

### 4.2 FACEBOOK - ADAPTATION GUIDE

```
FACEBOOK BEST PRACTICES:

Format:
  - Square (1:1) or horizontal (1.9:1)
  - Video or image
  - Text overlay limited (Facebook penalizes text-heavy)

Text overlay strategy:
  - IF you must use text: Keep <20% of image
  - Example: Small "20% OFF" badge on product
  - Better: No text overlay, write in caption instead

Caption:
  - LONGER text is OK in Facebook captions
  - 2-3 paragraphs (200-300 words acceptable)
  - Storytelling > hard sell
  - Ask questions to encourage comments

Engagement mechanics:
  - Facebook favors: Comments > Shares > Likes
  - Ask for engagement: "What's your biggest skincare struggle?"
  - Respond to comments (shows activity)

Video vs Image:
  - Video: Gets 2-3x more engagement
  - Optimal: 2-5 minutes (Facebook will auto-truncate longer)
  - Captions: Essential (60% watch without sound)

Ad format (if promoting):
  - Carousel ads: Show multiple tips
  - Single image: Simple, clean
  - Video: Highest conversion

Timing:
  - Post when audience active: 1-3 PM, 7-9 PM
  - Weekdays > Weekends (for B2B)
  - Consistent time (algorithm learns pattern)

ADAPTATION FROM TIKTOK → FACEBOOK:
  Original: Fast, short, trending audio
  Facebook version:
    - Keep main content
    - Slow down pacing (Facebook audience skips fast videos)
    - Add captions (remove trending audio)
    - Write longer caption with context + CTA
    - Replace music with softer background (if needed)
```

### 4.3 INSTAGRAM - ADAPTATION GUIDE

```
INSTAGRAM BEST PRACTICES:

Feed Post:
  - Square (1:1) optimal
  - Vertical (4:5) also good
  - AESTHETIC = critical (grid consistency)
  - Same color palette, filters, style across posts

Caption:
  - 1-3 paragraphs
  - Emoji: 3-5 strategic placements
  - Hashtags: 20-30 (in first comment to avoid clutter)
  - CTA: "Link in bio" or "Save this"

Story:
  - Vertical (9:16)
  - Max 15 seconds per story
  - Stickers: Poll, question, link sticker (10K+ followers)
  - Swipe-up: Use if available

Reels:
  - Vertical (9:16)
  - 15-90 seconds
  - Hook in first 3 seconds
  - Captions + trending audio CRITICAL
  - High engagement = algorithm boost

Carousel (Multiple slides):
  - 2-10 slides per carousel
  - Swipe-through engagement = metric
  - Each slide must stand alone
  - Last slide: CTA

Hashtag strategy:
  - Mix: Niche (10K-100K) + Popular (100K-1M) + Trending
  - Don't use all 30 at once (spread across captions + comments)
  - Research competitor hashtags

Consistency matters:
  - Post 3-5 times per week (no less than 2)
  - Same time daily (algorithm learns)
  - Engage with audience (comment on their posts)

ADAPTATION FROM BLOG → INSTAGRAM:

Carousel option:
  Slide 1: Eye-catching hook ("5 Summer Skincare Tips...")
  Slide 2-6: Each tip (icon + short text)
  Slide 7: Before-after or result
  Slide 8: CTA ("DM for consultation" or "Shop our collection")

Grid aesthetic:
  Color palette: 3-4 colors across all posts
  Filters: Same filter or similar tone
  Subject: Product + lifestyle + educational mix (70/20/10 ratio)
```

### 4.4 YOUTUBE - ADAPTATION GUIDE

```
YOUTUBE BEST PRACTICES:

Video structure:
  - Intro (0-10 secs): Hook viewers immediately
  - Main content (10-90% of video): Value delivery
  - Outro (final 10 secs): CTA + subscribe prompt

Thumbnail:
  - 1280x720px
  - High contrast colors
  - Large text (readable at 250px)
  - Face + expression (if applicable)
  - Consistency (same style across uploads)

Title:
  - Keyword-rich (for SEO)
  - 50-70 characters (gets truncated in search)
  - Hook: "5 Tips", "Complete Guide", "How to..."
  - Example: "5 Summer Skincare Tips for Oily Skin [2024 Guide]"

Description:
  - First 2-3 lines: Key info (SEO, summary)
  - Links: Clickable links to products/website
  - Timestamps: [0:00] Intro [0:30] Tip #1 [2:15] Tip #2...
  - Tags: Add 5-10 relevant tags

Tags & Categories:
  - Category: People & Blogs (or Beauty & Fashion)
  - Tags: #skincare #summer #tips #beauty #tutorial

YouTube Shorts:
  - Vertical (9:16)
  - Max 60 seconds
  - Trending audio (use YouTube's audio library)
  - High engagement = recommendation boost

Community post:
  - Text + image/poll
  - Build audience before video release
  - Can rank in search

Playlist:
  - Group related videos
  - Increases watch time
  - Algorithm boost for entire channel

End screens:
  - Add at 20 seconds before end
  - Suggest next video (increase watch time)
  - Subscribe button prompt

SEO optimization:
  - Keyword in: Title, description, tags, captions
  - Backlinks: Embed video on website/blog
  - Captions: Essential (improves indexing + accessibility)

Consistency:
  - Upload 1-2x per week minimum
  - Same upload day/time (algorithm learns)
  - Expect 2-3 months to gain traction

ADAPTATION FROM BLOG → YOUTUBE:

Convert blog structure to video script:
  Blog intro (150 words) → Video intro (30 seconds)
  Blog section 1 (300 words) → Video segment 1 (2-3 mins)
  Blog section 2 (300 words) → Video segment 2 (2-3 mins)
  ...
  Blog conclusion (150 words) → Video outro (30 seconds)

Visuals:
  Add B-roll, animations, on-screen text
  Show examples from blog (images, charts)
  Include captions for every statement (CRITICAL)

Production quality:
  iPhone video OK (4K is standard now)
  Audio quality > video quality (invest in mic)
  Consistent lighting (key for professional look)
```

### 4.5 EMAIL - ADAPTATION GUIDE

```
EMAIL BEST PRACTICES:

Subject line:
  - Max 50 characters (truncated on mobile)
  - Avoid spam words: "Free", "Act now", excessive symbols
  - Good: Curiosity, benefit, urgency
  - Examples:
    ✓ "4 Skincare Secrets Dermatologists Won't Tell You"
    ✓ "Save Your Summer Skin (before it's too late)"
    ✗ "FREE TIPS!!! Act NOW!!!"

Preheader:
  - Text after subject line (preview in inbox)
  - 40-50 characters
  - Complement subject (don't repeat)
  - Example: Subject: "Summer Skincare Tips"
            Preheader: "Protect your skin before July..."

Content structure:
  - Mobile-first (60% open emails on mobile)
  - One column layout (easier to read)
  - Large images (400px wide max)
  - Short paragraphs (3-4 lines max)

Text:
  - Personalization: Hi [First name],
  - Conversational tone (not salesy)
  - Bold key points
  - 80-100 word length (short emails)

Button/CTA:
  - Contrasting color (stand out)
  - Actionable text: "Read More", "Shop Now", not "Click Here"
  - Spacing: Surrounded by white space
  - Mobile-friendly: Min 44x44 pixels

Footer:
  - Unsubscribe link (required by law - CAN-SPAM Act)
  - Contact info / address
  - Social media links (optional)

Timing:
  - Send when audience checks email (usually 7-9 AM or 12-2 PM)
  - Avoid Friday PM, Sunday (low open rates)
  - Test different send times (A/B test)

Frequency:
  - 1-2 emails per week (not too many)
  - Consistent schedule (audience expects it)
  - Quality > Quantity (1 great email > 3 mediocre)

Open rate targets:
  - Average: 15-25%
  - Good: 25-35%
  - Great: 35%+

Click-through rate (CTR) targets:
  - Average: 2-5%
  - Good: 5-10%
  - Great: 10%+

ADAPTATION FROM BLOG → EMAIL:

Email sequence (5 emails):

  Email 1 (Day 1): Subject: Hook/curiosity
    Content: Brief intro + teaser
    CTA: "Read full article" (link to blog)
    Goal: Drive traffic to blog

  Email 2 (Day 3): Subject: First insight
    Content: Expand on tip #1 from blog
    CTA: "Learn more" (link to product related to tip #1)
    Goal: Introduce product

  Email 3 (Day 5): Subject: Second insight
    Content: Expand on tips #2-3
    CTA: "Shop tip #2 products"
    Goal: Encourage purchase

  Email 4 (Day 7): Subject: Urgency/scarcity
    Content: Summary of all tips + bonus info
    CTA: "Limited time offer" (coupon code)
    Goal: Drive conversion

  Email 5 (Day 14): Subject: Follow-up
    Content: Customer testimonial + success story
    CTA: "Start your routine"
    Goal: Re-engage non-purchasers
```

```
     → Create infographic (1 visual)
     → Create video script (film 3-5 min video)
     → Create email sequence (5 emails)
     → Create social captions (8 posts)
     → Create quote cards (5-10 images)

     → Create Pinterest pins (5 designs)

           → Clip highlights (10-20 clips)

TOTAL: 20-30 pieces from 1 livestream
```
