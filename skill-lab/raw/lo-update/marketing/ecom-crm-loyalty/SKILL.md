# CRM, CHĂM SÓC KHÁCH HÀNG & CHƯƠNG TRÌNH LOYALTY

## PHẦN 1: CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION VIA RFM MODEL

### 1.1. RFM Model là gì?

**RFM = Recency, Frequency, Monetary**
- **Recency (R):** Gần đây nhất khách mua cách hôm nay bao lâu?
- **Frequency (F):** Trong 6 tháng, khách mua mấy lần?
- **Monetary (M):** Tổng tiền khách đã chi?

**Công thức:**
```
RFM Score = (Recency Score × 1) + (Frequency Score × 1) + (Monetary Score × 1)

Scoring (mỗi metric 1-5 điểm):
- 5 points: Top 20% performers
- 4 points: Second 20%
- 3 points: Middle 20%
- 2 points: Fourth 20%
- 1 point: Bottom 20%

Example:
- Recency score: 5 (bought 3 days ago)
- Frequency score: 4 (bought 8 times in 6 months)
- Monetary score: 5 (spent 5M)
- Total RFM score: 5 + 4 + 5 = 14 (Max: 15)
→ This is a VIP customer!
```

### 1.2. 6 Segmentation từ RFM

**Segment 1: CHAMPIONS (RFM 13-15)**
- **Profile:** Mua gần đây, mua thường, chi tiền nhiều
- **Ví dụ:** Mua 10 ngày trước, 15 lần/năm, 10M spent
- **Value:** Tạo 30-40% revenue
- **Action:** VIP treatment, exclusive benefits, high-touch
- **Strategy:**
  - Tặng thẻ VIP, nhận ưu đãi exclusive (30% off special)
  - Gửi sản phẩm mới trước (early access)
  - Mời tham gia loyalty program cao nhất
  - Có dedicated account manager (call them personally)
  - Tặng birthday gift, anniversary reward
- **Retention:** 95% (they're loyal, just maintain)
- **Cost:** Invest heavily (10% of revenue from them)

**Segment 2: LOYAL CUSTOMERS (RFM 11-12)**
- **Profile:** Mua often, gần đây, chi tệp trung bình
- **Ví dụ:** Mua 20 ngày trước, 10 lần/6 months, 3-5M spent
- **Value:** Tạo 20-30% revenue
- **Action:** Keep & nurture to Champion status
- **Strategy:**
  - Enroll in loyalty program (points system)
  - Frequent promotions (20-25% off)
  - Early access to flash sale
  - Birthday wishes, thank you messages
  - Referral bonuses (give incentive to bring friends)
- **Retention:** 80% (at risk if not nurtured)
- **Cost:** Moderate investment

**Segment 3: POTENTIAL CUSTOMERS (RFM 9-10)**
- **Profile:** Mua gần đây, nhưng ít tần suất, chi tiền vừa
- **Ví dụ:** Mua 15 ngày trước, 2-3 lần/6 months, 1-2M spent
- **Value:** Tạo 10-15% revenue (potential to grow)
- **Action:** Convert to Loyal → Champion
- **Strategy:**
  - Upsell & cross-sell (recommend complimentary products)
  - Increase purchase frequency (limited-time offers: "Only 3 days!")
  - Personalized recommendations based on purchase history
  - "Complete your collection" message (if they bought serum, recommend toner)
  - Loyalty incentive: "3 more purchases = free gift"
- **Retention:** 60% (can easily churn)
- **Cost:** Medium (need to invest to unlock potential)

**Segment 4: AT-RISK CUSTOMERS (RFM 5-8)**
- **Profile:** Chưa mua lâu, hoặc mua ít, chi tiền ít
- **Ví dụ:** Mua 80 ngày trước, 1-2 lần/year, <1M spent
- **Value:** Low value now, but can recover
- **Action:** Win-back campaign
- **Strategy:**
  - Send "We miss you" email (offer 30% discount to come back)
  - Survey: "What can we do better?"
  - Reminder: "Your favorite product is back in stock"
  - Limited-time offer: "Come back, get 40% off this weekend only"
  - Product recommendation: "Based on your past purchase, we think you'll love this"
- **Retention:** 20-30% (high churn rate, but worth trying)
- **Cost:** Low (use automated email)

**Segment 5: NEW CUSTOMERS (Only 1-2 purchases total)**
- **Profile:** Mới mua lần 1 hoặc 2, chưa đủ data
- **Ví dụ:** Mua 5 ngày trước (hoặc hôm trước), 1 purchase, 500K spent
- **Value:** Unknown (could be champion or never return)
- **Action:** Welcome & onboard properly
- **Strategy:**
  - Send thank you email within 24 hours
  - Offer loyalty program signup (10% discount for next purchase)
  - Follow up: "How's your product? Any questions?"
  - Ask for review (incentivize: "Leave review, get 5% off next order")
  - Send beginner tips (how to use product properly)
  - Recommend related products (but not too aggressively)
  - Invite to exclusive new customer group (next 30 days only)
- **Retention:** 40-60% (critical period, decide if repeat or not)
- **Cost:** Medium (this is investment in future champions)

**Segment 6: LOST CUSTOMERS (RFM 1-4)**
- **Profile:** Chưa mua rất lâu (6+ months), hoặc mua 1 lần 1 năm trước rồi không quay lại
- **Ví dụ:** Mua 200+ ngày trước, 1 lần, <500K spent
- **Value:** ~0 (they've churned)
- **Action:** Re-activation or abandon
- **Strategy:**
  - Final win-back: "Last chance - 50% off everything"
  - Survey: "Why did you leave? Help us improve"
  - Highlight new products: "Look what's new!"
  - Massive discount: 50% off (this is your last shot)
  - Remove from normal email list (to avoid spam feelings)
  - Keep in low-frequency list (quarterly check-in)
- **Retention:** 5-10% (if they come back, likely due to personal need, not loyalty)
- **Cost:** Low (don't spend much on lost customers)

### 2.1. Points System

**Khái niệm:** Mỗi đơn hàng, khách nhận điểm → quy đổi thành tiền/giảm giá

**Setup points system:**

```
Basic formula:
1,000 đ chi tiêu = 1 điểm
100 điểm = 50,000 đ discount (redemption rate 1.8%)

Example:
- Customer buys serum 300,000 đ
- Earn: 300 points (300,000 / 1,000)
- To reach 100 points = 6 purchases
- After 6 purchases: Can use 100 points = 50,000 đ off next order
- Effective discount: 50,000 / 1,800,000 (6 × 300K) = 2.8%
```

**Better structure - Tiered point earning:**

```
Regular purchase: 1 điểm / 1,000 đ
Member Tier 1 (Silver, >1M spent/year): 1.2 điểm / 1,000 đ
Member Tier 2 (Gold, >3M spent/year): 1.5 điểm / 1,000 đ
Member Tier 3 (Platinum, >5M spent/year): 2 điểm / 1,000 đ

Redemption rates:
- 100 points = 50,000 đ (regular)
- 80 points = 50,000 đ (if you're Silver) - bonus!
- 60 points = 50,000 đ (if you're Gold)
- 40 points = 50,000 đ (if you're Platinum)

Incentive: Spend more → earn points faster
```

**Where to display points:**
- Shopee: In shop (can show in About section)
- Lazada: Similar
- TikTok: Show in livestream ("Earn points every purchase!")
- Own website/app: Prominent in header

**Point expiration:**
- Standard: Points expire 1 year from earn date
- Platinum: Points never expire
- Incentive: Keep members engaged year-round

### 2.3. Cashback Program

**Alternative to points: Direct cashback**

**Structure:**
```
5% cashback on all purchases
- Customer buys 500K serum
- Immediately earn 25K cashback
- Can use as credit for next purchase (or withdraw)

Tier 2 (Silver): 7% cashback
Tier 3 (Gold): 10% cashback
Tier 4 (Platinum): 15% cashback
```

**Pros vs Points:**
- Cashback: Simpler to understand, immediate value
- Points: Creates long-term engagement, harder to game

**Hybrid model (recommended):**
```
1% cashback (immediate, easy to redeem)
+ 0.8% points (long-term, bigger rewards)
= Total 1.8% value

Customer perceives:
- 1% I can use now
- 0.8% I'm saving for bigger discount later
```

### 2.5. Gamification (Spin wheel, Daily check-in, Challenges)

**Spin wheel / Lucky draw**

```
Mechanic:
- Every purchase = 1 spin (or spend 100K = 1 spin)
- Spin prizes:
  - 10K discount (50% probability)
  - 20K discount (25% probability)
  - Free product (15% probability)
  - 50K discount (10% probability)

Expected cost per spin: (0.5 × 10K) + (0.25 × 20K) + (0.15 × 500K) + (0.1 × 50K)
= 5K + 5K + 75K + 5K = 90K average

If your margin is 50%, cost is acceptable (10% margin hit)
```

**Daily check-in**

```
Mechanic:
- Log in daily = unlock daily reward
- Day 1: 5 points
- Day 2: 10 points
- Day 3: 15 points
- Day 4: 20 points
- Day 5: 25 points
- Day 6: 30 points
- Day 7: 50 points (big bonus!)
- Reset next week

User behavior:
- Check app 7 days per week
- This increases engagement, stickiness
- Higher chance they see promotions, buy again

Cost: (5+10+15+20+25+30+50) = 155 points per week
     = ~7,500đ value per customer per week
```

**Seasonal challenges**

```
Example: New Year Challenge (Jan 1-31)
- Buy 3 products, share photo → 100 points
- Refer 1 friend → 100 points
- Leave 5 reviews → 50 points
- Total possible: 250 points = 125K discount

Incentive: Users actively engage, spend more to get rewards

Leaderboard:
- Top 10 spenders get extra 500 points
- Creates FOMO (fear of missing out)
- Drives competition
```

### 3.2. Complaint Handling: HEARD Framework

**HEARD = Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, Delight**

**Example complaint:**
```
Customer message:
"Serum arrived leaking! Wasted 200K. Very disappointed.
I bought from you because you promised quality!!!"

STEP 1: HEAR (Listen to the issue)
- Don't interrupt
- Understand fully: Product damaged, customer upset about money loss
- Internal note: This is a Loyal customer (3M spent), worth keeping!

STEP 2: EMPATHIZE (Show you understand)
Response: "I understand how frustrating this is. You trusted us
with your money and got a damaged product. I'm truly sorry."

STEP 3: APOLOGIZE (Take responsibility)
Response: "This is completely our fault. We should have packed it
better. I sincerely apologize."

STEP 4: RESOLVE (Fix the problem)
Response: "Here's what I'll do:
  1. Send you replacement immediately (express shipping)
  2. If not satisfied, full refund no questions asked
  3. You keep the leaked product (no need to return)
Which option do you prefer?"

STEP 5: DELIGHT (Exceed expectations)
Response: "I'm also adding 500 points (250K value) to your account
as a gesture of goodwill. And once you receive replacement,
I'll gift you a hydration mask (our best seller) - no charge.
Please reply within 24 hours so I can process your request."

Result:
- Customer feels heard & cared for
- Problem solved quickly
- Loyalty actually INCREASES (they remember good service)
- 80% chance they stay (vs 10% if you ignore complaint)
```

**Complaint handling steps:**

```
Timeline:
0-30 min: Acknowledge complaint, apologize
30 min-2 hours: Investigate root cause
2-4 hours: Propose solution (get customer approval)
4-24 hours: Execute solution
24-48 hours: Follow-up ("Are you satisfied now?")

Tools:
- CRM: Log all complaints
- Root cause analysis: Why did this happen? (Quality issue? Shipping? Packing?)
- Fix: Address root cause, not just symptom
- Document: Build knowledge base of common complaints
```

### 3.3. Escalation Matrix

**Who handles what:**

```
LEVEL 1: Chat Bot / Auto-reply
- Order tracking: "Your order is at HCMC center"
- General questions: "How do I apply serum?"
- Status: "Is my order shipped?"
- Resolution rate: 60% (customers self-serve)
- If unclear: Escalate to Level 2

LEVEL 2: Customer Service Agent (Human)
- Product recommendations
- Simple complaints (wrong color shipped, not as expected)
- Refund requests <500K
- Promotional questions ("When does sale end?")
- Resolution rate: 35% (agent handles directly)
- If complex: Escalate to Level 3

LEVEL 3: Manager / Team Lead
- Major complaints (>500K, quality issues)
- Escalated complaints (customer unsatisfied after Level 2)
- Return/refund disputes (customer vs company)
- Loyalty program exceptions
- Resolution rate: 99% (manager has authority to offer more)

Example escalation:
Customer: "Serum causes rash, I want money back"
Level 1 bot: Cannot help → escalate
Level 2 agent: "Sorry to hear. Can you describe the rash? Any other products cause issue?"
  → If customer satisfied: Approve refund / replacement
  → If customer still upset: Escalate
Level 3 manager: "We sincerely apologize. Full refund + 500 points + free gift"
  → Problem usually solved here
```

## PHẦN 5: RETENTION METRICS

**Key metrics to track:**

```
╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║             RETENTION METRICS DASHBOARD                        ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

1. REPEAT PURCHASE RATE
   Formula: (Customers who bought 2+ times) / (Total customers) × 100
   Target: >30%
   Calculation: 250 repeat customers / 1000 total = 25% (below target!)
   Action: Increase loyalty program, referral incentives

2. CUSTOMER LIFETIME VALUE (LTV)
   Formula: (Total revenue from customer) / (# of customers)
   OR: (AOV × Purchase frequency × Customer lifespan)

   Example:
   - AOV (average order value): 500,000đ
   - Frequency: 4 purchases/year
   - Lifespan: 3 years (before churns)
   - LTV = 500K × 4 × 3 = 6,000,000đ per customer

3. CHURN RATE
   Formula: (Customers lost) / (Start-of-period customers) × 100
   Target: <5% per month, <40% per year
   Example: Lost 50 customers / 1000 total = 5% (at target!)

4. NET PROMOTER SCORE (NPS)
   Survey: "On scale 1-10, how likely to recommend us to friends?"
   - 9-10: Promoter (loyal, will refer)
   - 7-8: Passive (satisfied but not loyal)
   - 0-6: Detractor (unhappy, will complain)
   Formula: % Promoter - % Detractor
   Target: >50 (good), >70 (excellent)

5. CUSTOMER SATISFACTION SCORE (CSAT)
   Survey: "Rate your satisfaction 1-5"
   Target: 4.5+ (on 5-point scale)
   Use for: Service quality measurement

6. REPEAT CUSTOMER RATE (MONTH-OVER-MONTH)
   Formula: (Customers who bought last month AND this month) / (Last month customers)
   Target: >15% month-over-month
   Good indicator of loyalty

7. AVERAGE TIME BETWEEN PURCHASES
   Formula: Total days / Number of repeat purchases
   Example: Customer 1 buys Day 1, Day 25, Day 50 = avg 25 days
   Target: <30 days (frequent repeat buyers)

8. COST OF CUSTOMER ACQUISITION (CAC) VS LTV RATIO
   Formula: LTV / CAC
   Target: 3:1 or higher
   Example: LTV 6M / CAC 2M = 3:1 ✓ Good!
   If <3:1: Your acquisition cost too high
```

## PHẦN 7: COST ANALYSIS - GIỮ KHÁCH CŨ RẺ HƠN TÌM KHÁCH MỚI

**Research shows: Customer retention is 5-7x cheaper than acquisition**

**Example:**

```
Customer A: New customer acquisition
- Facebook Ads spend: 100K
- Expected conversion: 2%
- Cost per acquisition: 100K / 2 = 50,000đ per customer
- First purchase AOV: 500,000đ
- Profit: 500K × 50% margin - 50K CAC = 200K profit

Customer B: Retained from last year (existing customer)
- Loyalty program cost: 5,000đ (points earned)
- Email marketing cost: 1,000đ
- Total retention cost: 6,000đ
- Purchase AOV: 500,000đ
- Profit: 500K × 50% margin - 6K retention = 244K profit

Difference:
- CAC: 50K per new customer
- Retention: 6K per existing customer
- Ratio: 50K / 6K = 8.3x cheaper to retain!
```

**3-year LTV comparison:**

```
New Customer acquisition strategy:
- Year 1: Acquire 100 customers at 50K CAC = 5M spend
- Repeat rate: 30% → 30 come back next year
- Year 2: Acquire 100 new + 30 repeat = 150 total customers
- Year 3: Similar pattern
- Total customers after 3 years: 180 customers

Retention strategy (assuming 60% repeat rate):
- Year 1: Acquire 100 customers at 50K CAC = 5M spend
- Repeat rate: 60% → 60 come back next year
- Year 2: Acquire 50 new + 60 repeat = 110 customers
- Loyalty program cost: 110 × 6K = 660K spend
- Year 3: Acquire 50 new + 66 repeat = 116 customers
- Total 3-year spend: 5M + 660K + (50×50K + 116×6K) = 11.2M

Comparison:
- Acquisition only: 300M+ revenue, 15M+ marketing spend
- Retention focused: 280M revenue, 11.2M marketing spend
- Savings: 3.8M (25% less spend!) with similar revenue!
```

**Moral:** Invest in loyalty/retention, not just acquisition!
