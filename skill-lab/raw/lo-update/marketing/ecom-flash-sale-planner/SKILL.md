## 2. TIMELINE LẬP KẾ HOẠCH FLASH SALE (Detailed Planning Framework)

### T-30 (30 ngày trước ngày bán)
**PHASE: Strategic Planning**
- Quyết định có tham gia sàn hay self-organize
- Dự báo doanh thu mục tiêu (Revenue target)
- Xác định tỉ lệ margin mong muốn (Margin target: thường 20-35%)
- Lập danh sách product candidates (50-100 SKUs)
- Kiểm tra competitor pricing
- Bộ phận tài chính: Phê duyệt budget
- Bộ phận vận hành: Kiểm tra capacity (warehouse, staff, logistics partners)

### T-21 (21 ngày trước)
**PHASE: Product & Inventory Planning**
- Finalize sản phẩm sale (top 20-30 bestsellers)
- Xác định số lượng inventory cần (Forecast = Expected sales x 1.2-1.5 safety buffer)
- Liên hệ suppliers: đặt hàng bổ sung nếu thiếu
- Kiểm tra hạn dùng, chất lượng sản phẩm
- Sắp xếp warehouse & storage (allocation không gian)
- Chuẩn bị packaging đặc biệt nếu cần (gift boxes, branded stickers)

### T-14 (14 ngày trước)
**PHASE: Pricing & Content Strategy**
- **Pricing Decision**:
  - Original price vs Sale price (discount 30-70%)
  - Tính toán breakeven point
  - Profit margin per product
  - Tiếp cận theo từng category

- **Product Content**:
  - Cập nhật product title: Thêm keyword "SALE", "FLASH", "Giảm Sốc"
  - Refresh product images: Chụp ảnh sản phẩm mới, rõ nét, HD
  - Cập nhật product description: Highlight benefits, giải quyết pain points
  - Tạo video demo sản phẩm (30-60 giây)
  - Chuẩn bị 5-10 ảnh khác góc độ

### T-7 (7 ngày trước)
**PHASE: Marketing Foundation**
- **Content Creation**:
  - Tạo teaser content cho social media
  - Lập kịch bản livestream (30-60 phút)
  - Chuẩn bị email templates
  - Thiết kế banners (1200x300, 1200x628 pixels)

- **Advertising Prep**:
  - Thiết kế 10-20 creative ads (Facebook, TikTok, Shopee)
  - Chuẩn bị audience segments
  - Xác định daily ad budget
  - Tạo landing page/destination URL

### T-3 (3 ngày trước)
**PHASE: Execution Preparation**
- Tất cả product listings live trên sàn
- Test checkout flow: Làm đơn thử từ đầu đến cuối
- Chuẩn bị customer service: Training staff, prepare response templates
- Launch pre-warming campaign:
  - Email: "Flash sale sắp đến" (Early interest building)
  - SMS: 1 message thông báo
  - Social: First teaser posts
  - Paid ads: Low spend, brand awareness

- Double-check logistics partners: Họ sẵn sàng tăng capacity chưa?

### T-1 (1 ngày trước)
**PHASE: Final Check**
- Inventory final count: Đối chiếu số lượng hệ thống với thực tế
- Pricing final verification: Double check tất cả prices are correct
- Customer service prep: Brief team, set expectations
- Technical check: Server load test, payment gateway test
- Sắp xếp staff schedule cho flash sale day
- Backup plans: Plan B nếu server crash, plan C nếu hết hàng sớm

### D-Day (Ngày bán)
**PHASE: Execution & Monitoring**
- **Morning (05:00-09:00)**:
  - Final inventory check 1 hour before launch
  - Staff check-in & final briefing
  - Monitor payment gateway & server status

- **Launch Time (10:00 hay theo sàn)**:
  - Full ads deployment: Tất cả ads go live với max budget
  - Email blast: "Flash sale is LIVE" → Max open rate
  - SMS blast: "Giảm sốc 70% - Mua ngay"
  - Social media: ALL platforms heavy posting
  - Livestream GO LIVE

- **Hour 1-4 (Peak Traffic)**:
  - Monitor live metrics: GMV, orders, inventory
  - Adjust ads spend if needed
  - Real-time customer service: Chat, review, answer questions
  - Watch for technical issues

- **Hour 4-24**:
  - Maintain momentum: Continuous social posts, email campaigns
  - "Last chance" messaging 6 hours before end
  - Prepare for extension if needed

- **End of Day (23:00)**:
  - Final counts: Total GMV, Orders, AOV, Conversion rate
  - Inventory status: What's left?
  - Technical post-mortem: Any issues?

### D+1 (Ngày thứ 2)
**PHASE: Extension & Last-Chance**
- Evaluate performance of Day 1
- If successful → Extend sale 1-3 days more (với discount thấp hơn)
- "Last chance to grab deals" campaign
- Clear remaining slow-moving inventory with deeper discounts
- Email: Personalized follow-up "Items you viewed" with remaining stock

### D+3 to D+7
**PHASE: Analysis & Optimization**
- Compile all data: GMV, Orders, AOV, Conversion rate, Profit
- Customer feedback analysis: Reviews, ratings, complaints
- Product performance: Best sellers vs. flops
- Marketing performance: Which ads had best ROAS?
- Logistics performance: Any delivery delays?
- Create detailed post-mortem report
- Plan improvements for next flash sale

## 3. CHIẾN LƯỢC GIÁ (Pricing Strategies)

### 3.1 Price Anchoring
Kỹ thuật định giá dựa trên mục tiêu hiển thị giá gốc cao, sau đó giảm xuống sale price.

**Cách thực hiện**:
- Original Price: $50 (hiển thị gạch bỏ trên listing)
- Sale Price: $15 (giá bán)
- Discount %: 70% OFF
- Psychological impact: Khách hàng cảm giác "tiền của" lớn hơn thực tế

**Lưu ý**: Giá gốc phải hợp lý - không quá cao vô lý (sàn & khách hàng sẽ report)

### 3.2 Bundle Pricing
Bán nhiều sản phẩm cùng lúc với giá combo rẻ hơn mua lẻ.

**Ví dụ - Skincare Bundle**:
- Cleanser: $10 (sale price)
- Toner: $8
- Cream: $12
- Mask: $5
- Total if bought separately: $35
- Bundle price: $24 (31% discount)
- Result: AOV tăng, inventory turnover nhanh

### 3.3 Tiered Discount
Giảm giá tùy theo số lượng mua (Buy more, save more).

**Ví dụ - T-shirt**:
- Buy 1: $12 (sale price)
- Buy 2-5: $11 each (8% more savings)
- Buy 6+: $10 each (17% more savings)
- Motivation: Khách hàng cảm thấy càng mua càng tiết kiệm → Mua nhiều hơn

### 3.4 BOGO (Buy One Get One)
Mua 1 sản phẩm, tặng 1 sản phẩm miễn phí.

**Ví dụ - Sunscreen**:
- Regular price: $8/bottle
- BOGO offer: Buy 1 get 1 free = 2 bottles for $8 ($4/bottle)
- Discount perceived: 50%
- Actual margin: Nếu COGS=$2, profit/bottle=$3 (still profitable)
- Benefit: Tăng AOV, customer lifetime value (khách có 2 bottles dùng lâu hơn)

### 3.5 Gift with Purchase (GWP)
Mua sản phẩm chính, tặng quà miễn phí.

**Ví dụ - Laptop Purchase**:
- Laptop price: $500 (sale price, down from $700)
- GWP: Free USB-C Cable ($5 value) + Free Mouse Pad ($3 value)
- Customer perception: "Nhận free gifts worth $8"
- Actual margin: Lost $8 in gifts, but sold laptop at full margin
- Psychology: Cảm giác nhận được quà tặng → More satisfaction → Better reviews

## 4. INVENTORY PLANNING & FORECASTING

### 4.1 Dự Trù Nhu Cầu (Demand Forecasting)

**Method 1: Based on Historical Data**
```
Expected Flash Sale Units = (Average monthly sales) × (Historical flash sale multiplier)

Example:
- Average monthly sales: 100 units
- Flash sale multiplier (từ lần sale trước): 8x
- Expected sales: 800 units
- Safety buffer: 20%
- Target inventory: 800 × 1.2 = 960 units
```

**Method 2: Based on Inventory Capacity**
```
Target inventory = (Available warehouse space / size per unit) × (Target turnover rate)

Example:
- Available space: 500 square meters
- Each unit takes 0.5 sq m
- Capacity: 1,000 units
- Turnover rate: 80%
- Target inventory: 800 units
```

### 4.2 Safety Stock Calculation
Số hàng dự trữ để tránh stockout (hết hàng) trong trường hợp bất ngờ.

```
Safety stock = (Maximum daily sales during flash sale) × (Lead time days) + Buffer for uncertainty

Example:
- Max daily sales expected: 300 units
- Supplier lead time: 2 days
- Uncertainty buffer: 2 days
- Safety stock = 300 × (2+2) = 1,200 units
```

### 4.3 Backup Suppliers
Chuẩn bị nhà cung cấp dự phòng để tránh tình huống hết hàng đột ngột.

- Primary supplier: Công ty A
- Backup supplier 1: Công ty B (có thể cung cấp 50% nhu cầu)
- Backup supplier 2: Công ty C (có thể cung cấp 30% nhu cầu)
- Emergency supplier: Nhà cung cấp cứu cấp (biết rằng giá sẽ cao, nhưng tránh được stockout)

## 5. MARKETING TIMELINE & CONTENT STRATEGY CHO FLASH SALE

### 5.1 Giai Đoạn Teasing (T-7 đến T-3)

**Mục tiêu**: Tạo sự tò mò, build hype, encourage wishlist

**Nội dung**:
- Countdown posts: "7 days to flash sale", "3 days left!"
- Sneak peek: Show 3-5 sản phẩm sắp sale nhưng không show price
- "What would you buy?" polls on Instagram/TikTok
- BTS (behind-the-scenes) content: Team chuẩn bị cho sale
- Mystery box teasing: "Guess the discount level"

**Platforms & Frequency**:
- Facebook: 1 post/day
- Instagram: 1-2 Stories/day + 1 Feed post every 2 days
- TikTok: 1 video/day (trending sounds, humor)
- Email list: 1 email (teaser)

### 5.2 Giai Đoạn Warming Up (T-3 đến T-1)

**Mục tiêu**: Build anticipation, drive traffic, offer early access

**Nội dung**:
- VIP early access: "Subscribe/Member get 24-hour early access"
- Early bird deals: "First 100 customers get extra 10% off"
- Countdown timer on website & social
- Customer testimonials: "Last time I saved $200"
- Product spotlight: Deep dive into specific products

**Email Sequences**:
- Email 1 (T-3): "Exclusive early access for subscribers"
- Email 2 (T-2): "Popular items trending for flash sale"
- Email 3 (T-1): "Last chance to prepare - Flash sale TOMORROW"

**SMS (nếu có SMS list)**:
- SMS 1 (T-2): "2 days left! Flash sale starts [date & time]"
- SMS 2 (T-1 evening): "🚀 TOMORROW! Set alarm for [time]"

### 5.3 Giai Đoạn Launch (D-Day)

**Mục tiêu**: Max traffic, max conversions, momentum

**Morning Before Launch (08:00-09:45)**:
- Email blast pre-launch: "Get ready, sale starts in 1 hour"
- Social media count-down: "60 minutes", "30 minutes", "10 minutes"
- Live prep: Crew test cameras, audio, set

**Launch Time (10:00 chính xác)**:
- Email: "🔥 FLASH SALE IS LIVE! Shop now before items sell out"
- SMS: "Sale is LIVE! Check app/website" (if applicable)
- All social media: BLAST all platforms with full-volume posts
- Facebook: Multiple posts + Stories + Reels
- Instagram: Multiple Stories, Reels, carousel
- TikTok: 5-10 videos staggered throughout day
- Google/Shop ads: Max bid, max budget
- Shopee/Lazada/TikTok Shop native ads: Activated

**Livestream (10:00-12:00 & 19:00-21:00)**:
- 2 sessions: Morning & evening (cover different timezones + work hours)
- Host energetic MC
- Showcase 5-10 products with storytelling
- Interactive elements: Q&A, games, "name the price"
- Exclusive livestream deals: 5-10% extra discount for viewers
- Donation/tip options from viewers (if platform allows)

**Mid-Day (12:00-18:00)**:
- Content streams:
  - Instagram Stories: 1 every 2 hours with swipe-up links
  - TikTok videos: 1 every 4 hours
  - Facebook: 1 post every 4 hours
- Customer shout-outs: Share real customer purchases with their consent
- "Trending items" update: Show best sellers in real-time

**Evening Push (18:00-23:59)**:
- "6-hour last chance" messaging
- "Only X items left in stock" urgency
- Final email blast: "LAST CHANCE - Sale ends in 6 hours"
- SMS blast (if have list): "Few hours left!"
- Social media: Increase frequency to 1 post/hour

### 5.4 Giai Đoạn Extension & Last Chance (D+1 đến D+3)

**Nếu sale được extend (thường là nếu inventory còn)**:
- "Sale extended by 2 more days!"

**Email sequence**:
- Email 1 (D+1 morning): "You didn't order yet? Here's why you should"

## 6. ADS STRATEGY CHO FLASH SALE

### 6.1 Budget Allocation

**Typical Budget Distribution** (assuming $5,000 total ad budget for flash sale):

```
Facebook Ads:         $2,000 (40%)  → Broad reach, older demographics
Instagram Ads:        $1,000 (20%)  → Younger audience, visual focus
TikTok Ads:          $1,000 (20%)  → Gen Z, viral potential
Shopee/Lazada Ads:     $500 (10%)  → Platform-native ads (high conversion)
Google Search:         $500 (10%)  → High intent, last-click
```

**Adjustment based on best-performing channels**:
- Week 1: Equal distribution
- Day 2-3: Move 20% from underperforming to top 2 channels
- Day 4+: 70% budget to top channel

### 6.2 Facebook & Instagram Ads Strategy

**Campaign Structure**:
```
Campaign: Flash Sale 12.12
├── Ad Set 1: Prospecting (New audience, cold traffic)
│   ├── Audience: Interests (shopping, e-commerce)
│   ├── Budget: 40% of budget
│   ├── Bid: Lowest cost
│   ├── Creative: 3-4 variants (different angles)
│   └── CPC target: $0.50-$1.00
│
├── Ad Set 2: Warm Audience (Website visitors last 30 days)
│   ├── Budget: 35% of budget
│   ├── Bid: Highest - convert warm traffic
│   ├── Creative: Urgency focused "Last chance"
│   └── CPC target: $0.30-$0.60
│
├── Ad Set 3: Retargeting (Cart abandoners, product viewers)
│   ├── Audience: Custom audience (cart abandoners)
│   ├── Budget: 25% of budget
│   ├── Creative: "You left X in cart - $50 OFF today"
│   └── CPC target: $0.20-$0.40
```

**Ad Creative Best Practices**:
- **Video ads**: 15-30 second videos perform best (3:1 cost advantage vs. image)
- **Text overlay**: Large, clear text "70% OFF", "LIMITED TIME", "TODAY ONLY"
- **Urgency elements**: Countdown timer, "Only 50 left", "Ends tonight"
- **Hero image**: High-quality product photos, lifestyle shots
- **CTA button**: "Shop now", "Claim deal", "Buy now"

**Bidding Strategy**:
- **Lowest cost**: Broad prospecting (reach max people at lowest price)
- **Target cost**: High-intent audiences (target $2-5 cost per purchase)
- **ROI-focused**: When you have enough data (day 2+)

### 6.3 TikTok Ads Strategy

**Unique to TikTok**:
- Spark Ads (promote organic videos for authenticity)
- Low-cost production (don't need high-quality studio content)
- Native content style outperforms polished ads
- Trending audio drives performance

**Campaign Format**:
- **Spark Ads (40% budget)**: Promote native-looking content
- **In-feed ads (60% budget)**: More promotional, direct CTA

**Content Ideas**:
- Unboxing video (authenticity)
- Product demo / before-after
- Customer testimonials (UGC style)
- Trending audio + product tie-in
- "POV: You bought this and..." storytelling

### 6.4 Shopee/Lazada Native Ads

**In-Platform Ads** (on Shopee/Lazada themselves):
- **Shopee Ads**: SEM (Search Engine Marketing)
  - Top search placement for keywords: "flash sale", category names
  - Bid on keywords, pay per click
  - Recommended daily budget: $3-5 per flash sale day

- **Lazada Ads**: Similar model
  - Sponsored listings at top of search
  - Cost per click: $0.30-$1.50

**Best Practice**: Bid high on first 3 days of flash sale when traffic is peak

### 6.5 Google Search Ads (Optional, for high-intent users)

**For**:
- Brand name searches: "[Your brand] + sale"
- Category searches: "flash sale today", "discount shopping"

**Bidding**: Aggressive on brand name (high intent, lower CPC expected)

## 7. KPI TRACKING & METRICS

### 7.1 Primary Metrics (Must Track)

**GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)**
- Total value of all sales
- Target: $XXX,XXX (based on your baseline)
- Formula: Number of orders × Average order value

**Number of Orders**
- Total transactions completed
- Target: XXXX (historical avg × flash sale multiplier)
- Insight: Volume metric, shows traffic quality

**Average Order Value (AOV)**
- Total GMV / Number of orders
- Target: Increase AOV by 10-20% vs. baseline (through bundles, upsells)
- Formula: $500,000 GMV / 1,000 orders = $500 AOV

**Conversion Rate**
- Orders / Visitors
- Target: 3-5% (e-commerce avg is 1-2%)
- Formula: 1,000 orders / 20,000 visitors = 5% conversion rate

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**
- Revenue generated / Ad spend
- Target: $5+ ROAS (for every $1 spent, make $5)
- Formula: $50,000 revenue / $10,000 ad spend = 5.0 ROAS
- Threshold: Anything below 3.0 ROAS means losing money on that channel

### 7.2 Secondary Metrics

**Traffic Metrics**:
- **Unique visitors**: Total people who visited store/website
- **Page views**: Total pages viewed (engagement indicator)
- **Bounce rate**: % who leave after 1 page (lower is better, target <40%)

**Product Performance**:
- **Bestselling products**: Top 10 by revenue
- **Inventory turnover**: Inventory sold / Total inventory × 100%
  - Target: 80-100% (sold 8-10 out of 10 units)
- **Stockout rate**: Products that sold out
  - If >50% products sold out → Underestimated demand (good problem)
  - If <20% products sold out → Over-prepared

**Customer Metrics**:
- **New customer acquisition**: # of first-time buyers
- **Repeat rate**: % of customers who bought before (loyalty)
- **Customer acquisition cost (CAC)**: Ad spend / New customers acquired
  - Target: CAC < 20% of customer lifetime value

**Profit Metrics**:
- **Net profit**: Total revenue - COGS - Ads spend - Logistics
- **Profit margin**: (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100%
  - Target: 25-35% (after all costs)

### 7.3 Real-Time Monitoring Dashboard

**What to Track LIVE during flash sale** (update every 1-2 hours):
- ✓ Cumulative GMV
- ✓ Orders count
- ✓ Current conversion rate
- ✓ Top 5 products by revenue
- ✓ Inventory levels (% remaining)
- ✓ Cost per order (total ad spend / orders so far)
- ✓ Ad spend vs. budget (tracking if within budget)
- ✓ Checkout completion rate

**Alert thresholds** (trigger actions if metrics hit these):
- Conversion rate drops <2% → Increase ad spend or check for technical issues
- Checkout abandonment >25% → Simplify checkout, check payment methods
- Cost per order >$30 → Pause low-performing ads
- Any product inventory at 100% remaining by hour 6 → Price reduction needed

## 9. COMMON MISTAKES & HOW TO AVOID THEM

### 9.1 Discount Quá Sâu (Too Deep Discounts)
**Problem**: Giảm 80%+ → Margin quá thấp, có thể lỗ tiền

**Solution**:
- Xác định minimum margin acceptable: 20% (công thức: profit/revenue)
- Tính backwards: Nếu COGS=$10, need margin 20%, then min sale price = $12.50
- Nếu cần giảm 80%, chọn low-COGS products instead

### 9.2 Hết Hàng Sớm (Stockout Too Early)
**Problem**: 6 giờ sau bắt đầu sale, toàn bộ inventory sold out → Khách hàng disappointed, bad reviews

**Solution**:
- Tăng safety stock: Multiply expected sales by 1.5-2.0
- Prepare "Notify when back in stock" feature → Convert to future sales
- Have backup inventory from supplier ready to restock mid-sale

### 9.3 Server Crash / Technical Issues
**Problem**: Traffic quá cao → Website/app crash → Lost sales, customer frustration

**Solution**:
- Pre-sale infrastructure check: Load test website with 10x normal traffic
- Upgrade server capacity 48 hours before: CDN, database optimization
- Prepare fallback: If website down, continue sales on Shopee/Lazada
- Have tech support team on standby during sale

### 9.4 Delivery Delays (Giao Hàng Trễ)
**Problem**: Vì traffic cao → Logistics partners overwhelmed → Delayed delivery → Negative reviews

**Solution**:
- Coordinate with logistics partners 2 weeks before
- Negotiate additional capacity
- Prepare backup couriers
- Set realistic delivery expectations in product listings
- Offer compensation (discount on next order) if delayed

### 9.5 Poor Customer Service
**Problem**: Customer questions/complaints unanswered → Negative reviews → Lower conversion

**Solution**:
- Hire temporary customer service staff (add $500-1000 cost)
- Prepare response templates for common questions
- Set SLA: Respond within 1 hour during sale
- Use chatbot for instant replies to basic questions

### 9.6 Cannibalization (Eating Your Own Sales)
**Problem**: Customers buy at flash sale price instead of regular time → Lower overall revenue

**Solution**:
- Flash sale is ONLY for specific products (not everything)
- Don't promote as "get-out-of-jail" card (customers wait for sales)
- Balance: Use 20-30% of top products in sale, keep 70% at regular price

### 9.7 Inadequate Marketing
**Problem**: People don't know about flash sale → Low traffic

**Solution**:
- Start teaser campaign at T-7 (7 days before)
- Use all channels: Email, SMS, social, paid ads, in-app notifications
- Allocate sufficient ad budget (5-10% of expected revenue)

## 11. CHIẾN LƯỢC COMBO & BUNDLE ĐỂ TĂNG AOV

### 11.1 Bundle Strategy - Mục Đích Tăng AOV

**AOV (Average Order Value)** = Total GMV / Number of orders

**Nếu:**
- GMV = $500,000
- Orders = 1,000
- AOV = $500

**Goal**: Tăng AOV từ $500 → $650 (30% uplift)

**Cách**:
- Không cần bán thêm 30% số sản phẩm (khó)
- Thay vào đó, bundle 2-3 sản phẩm → Average order size tăng

### 11.2 Bundle Types

**Type 1: Complementary Bundle** (Sản phẩm bổ sung)
```
Skincare bundle:
- Cleanser: $10
- Toner: $8
- Cream: $12
- Total: $30 if bought separately
- Bundle price: $22 (27% savings)
- Margin: Still healthy if COGS is low
```

**Type 2: Tiered Bundle** (Nhiều mức giá)
```
- Starter bundle: Cleanser + Toner = $16 (vs $18)
- Premium bundle: Cleanser + Toner + Cream = $24 (vs $30)
- Deluxe bundle: All 4 items = $28 (vs $35)
```

**Type 3: Volume Bundle** (Mua nhiều cùng sản phẩm)
```
T-shirt bundle:
- 1 shirt: $12
- 3 shirts (same or mix): $30 (vs $36) = 17% savings
- 5 shirts: $50 (vs $60) = 17% savings
```

**Type 4: Flash Sale Bundle** (Exclusive cho flash sale)
```
- Normally sold separately
- Flash sale: Buy any 2 products, get 3rd at 50% OFF
- Encourages customers to buy more = higher AOV
```

### 11.3 Bundle Creation Formula

1. **Identify best sellers**: Top 10 products by revenue
2. **Identify products that don't sell well**: Need to move inventory
3. **Create bundles**: Pair top seller + slow seller
   - Example: Bestselling mascara + slow-moving eyeliner
4. **Set bundle price**:
   - Regular: $20 + $8 = $28
   - Bundle: $23 (18% savings, but still profitable)
5. **Highlight on product pages**: "Complete your look" section

## 12. VOUCHER STRATEGY (Mã Giảm Giá)

### 12.1 Voucher Shop (Do chính bạn phát hành)

**Ưu điểm**: Bạn kiểm soát hoàn toàn

**Example**:
- Shop voucher: "FLASHSALE10" → 10% off orders above $50
- Validity: Only during flash sale (24 hours)
- Quantity limit: 5,000 uses maximum
- Applicable products: All on sale

**How to execute on Shopee**:
1. Go to Shop → Promotion → Voucher
2. Create new voucher with discount %
3. Set budget cap (max total discount value: $5,000)
4. Promote through email, SMS, social

### 12.2 Voucher Sàn (Voucher của sàn)

**Shopee, Lazada offer seller vouchers**:
- Sàn provides branded vouchers
- Customers view these when browsing
- You set discount % and budget
- Example: Shopee "Seller Voucher" - 15% off purchases $30+

**Strategy**:
- Use shop vouchers for cold traffic (build loyalty)
- Use platform vouchers for warm traffic (already on platform)

### 12.3 Freeship Codes (Mã Freeship)

**Most popular in Vietnam** (customers love free shipping)

**Example**:
- Code: "FREESHIP50" → Free shipping for orders $50+
- Alternative: "FREESHIP30" → Free shipping for orders $30+
- Logistics cost: You subsidize or negotiate with courier

**When to use**:
- For customers in expensive shipping zones (rural areas)
- For customers making small purchases (want to encourage more)
- Cart abandonment recovery (incentive to complete purchase)

### 12.4 Affiliate/Referral Codes

**Encourage customers to refer friends**:
- Original customer: "REFER20" → 20% off referral's purchase
- Referred customer: Also gets 10% off
- Win-win: Both customers happy, you get new customer acquisition
